James Prescott Joule, 1818-1888 Famous physicist and brewer, born in Salford.

Joule conducted many different experiments to demonstrate the quantitative equivalence of electrical, chemical and thermal phenomena, and that the forms of energy could be converted from one to the other.

**_‘Believing that the power to destroy belongs to The Creator alone I affirm_** ... that any theory which, when carried out, demands the annihilation of force, is necessarily erroneous.’

SI unit of energy is named the Joule, in recognition of this work.

#### Grown Biomass= Energy for power generation Grown Biomass= Energy for food?

### content (e.g. Food).

- **calorie** (cal): amount of heat needed to raise the temperature of 1 gram of water 1 degree Celsius.

- **Calorie** (kcal): amount of heat needed to raise the temperature of 1 kilogram of water 1 degree Celsius.

- **calorie** = 4.18 joules of energy / heat

- This **heat content** can be used to fuel our activity, keep us warm, or used to make another form of energy (e.g. Electricity).
