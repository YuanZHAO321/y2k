## Proper, improper and percentages

- Proper fractions are those where the numerator < denominator (e.g. 2/7)

- An improper fraction is one where the numerator > denominator (e.g. 7/3)

- An improper fraction can therefore also be written as a mixed fraction (2 ⅓), that is a whole number and a proper fraction

- One percent (%) is one hundredth (1/100) of a whole. 10% is 10/100 (or 1/10, one tenth) of the whole, and so on.
