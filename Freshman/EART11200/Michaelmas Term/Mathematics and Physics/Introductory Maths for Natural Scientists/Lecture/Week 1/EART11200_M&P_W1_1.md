## Why is maths important in our science?

### • Geology and Environmental Sciences are not just qualitative science (describing fossils, or how pollution affects organisms).

- Like all _true_ science they are quantitative.

- Usually we try to predict what will happen in the future, or explain what has happened in the past – often as a way of helping us make those future predictions.

## Starting points

- Use your critical faculty

   - e.g. mass of a lake 100m diameter: if you calculate that it weighs 50-80 kg, think does that make sense?

   - Does it fit in with what you know already?

- Converting units.

   - A fairly basic thing, but a common problem. So we will spend some time talking through it.

- You can all develop simple mathematical models of real world situations and use these to predict the future or past

   - This can be very useful, but also be aware of the degree of uncertainty in your predictions, either from uncertain input data, or oversimplified models
