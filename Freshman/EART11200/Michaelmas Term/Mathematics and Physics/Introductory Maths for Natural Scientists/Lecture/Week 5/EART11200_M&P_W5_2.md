# Quantifying earthquakes

- We have discussed the Richter scale, which measures the amplitude of an earthquake.

- The Mercalli scale measures (qualitative) destructiveness or effect.

- The energy released (intensity) of the earthquake is given by

Log10 E = 11.8 + 1.5M Where M is magnitude on the Richter scale
