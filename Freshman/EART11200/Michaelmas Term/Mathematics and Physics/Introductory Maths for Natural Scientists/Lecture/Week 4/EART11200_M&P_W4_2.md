This week we will look at the burning of fossil fuels, and its consequences. Balancing equations is important.

## Burning hydrocarbons

CH + 2O  CO O 4 2 2 + 2H2 C H +3.5O  2CO O 2 6 2 2 + 3 H2 C H +5O  3 CO O 3 8 2 2 + 4 H2

If **_n_** is the length of the carbon chain, the number of hydrogens is **_2n+2_**

The number of CO2 molecules has to balance the length of the carbon chain i.e. **_n_**

The number of water molecules is determined by balancing the hydrogens i.e. **_(2n+2)/2_**

The number of molecular oxygens must balance with the oxygen on the right, i.e. **_n+(2n+2)/4_**
