### **The Carbon Cycle**

Importance of the Carbon Cycle:

- C is one of the basic elements for life, ~50% of all living tissues

- CO 2 and CH4 are two of the most important greenhouse gases

- Cycling of C approximates the transfer of energy around the

- Earth, the metabolism of natural, human and industrial systems

_“The near-surface carbon cycle profoundly affects Earth’s changeable climate, the health of ecosystems, the availability of inexpensive energy, and the resilience of the environment......._

_No other element engages in such an extraordinary range of chemical bonding environments: with oxidation states ranging from −4 to +4, carbon bonds to itself and more than 80 other elements.”_ (Hazen and Schiffries, 2013)

**Soils:** Humic and fulvic acids are produced by biodegradation of organic matter. **Kerogen:** a mixture of organic compounds that occur in sedimentary rocks. Formed from humic and fulvic acids.
