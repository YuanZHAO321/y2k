You have shown that calcite is the stable polymorph at the Earth’s surface (25 °C and 1 bar pressure). ## **D. Interpretation of basic phase diagrams and the Gibbs Phase Rule**

The Gibbs Phase Rule is:

f = c –  + 2

c = components. The number of components in a system is the minimum number of chemical species required to describe the composition of all the phases in the system

 = phases. A phase is a physically distinct body of matter that is (at least in principle) physically separable from the rest of the system.

f = degrees of freedom: the number of intensive variables that can be changed independently, without altering the state of the system, i.e. without changing the number of phases

k) How is it possible to find a grain of cristobalite at the Earth’s surface (1 bar, 20 °C)?

The phase diagram shows the thermodynamically stable phases. However, many metastable or disequilibrium phases persist to surface conditions because reactions can only proceed with sufficient energy. _Kinetic effects_ are very important in preserving disequilibrium minerals.
