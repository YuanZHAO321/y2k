### What else is in the definition of a mineral?

1. Naturally-occurring

2. Inorganic

3. Solid

4. With characteristic chemical composition (either fixed, or varying within specific limits)

1. Is ice (H2O) a mineral?

2. Is volcanic glass a mineral or a rock (or neither)?

#### Is ice a mineral?

Yes, it occurs in nature, it is solid, it is inorganic (it does not contain carbon), and it has a crystalline structure (if it didn’t, it wouldn’t form beautifully symmetrical snowflakes).

Is volcanic glass a mineral or a rock (or neither)?

It is not a mineral, as it does not have a crystalline structure. Glass is essentially super-cooled liquid, and has an irregular atomic structure.

As rocks are made of minerals, it is technically not a rock either, but rocks are allowed to contain glass, as long as it is all naturally-occurring.
