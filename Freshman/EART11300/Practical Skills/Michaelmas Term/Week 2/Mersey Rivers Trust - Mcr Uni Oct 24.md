### **Mersey Basin Challenges**

- Over 1,500 Km of rivers

- £8 Billion on improving sewage infrastructure in Mersey since 1996

- Toxic chemical pollution remains (due to sewage effluent, landfill, old waste tips, old mines, contaminated sediments)

- Poor access to waterside & bathing/paddling discouraged in majority of area

- Catchment-wide damage to river channels and river and riparian habitat

- Invasive plant species widespread such as Giant Hogweed & Himalayan Balsam

- Mersey Basin is home to 5 million people

- Why should they be satisfied with a 2<sup>nd</sup> class water environment?

## **River Bollin**

- **Important river catchment but not in good ecological condition**

- **Various internationally important wetlands and nationally important nature reserves and Sites of Special**

- **Scientific Interest**

- **Environmentally important but degraded lowland peat habitats, meres, mosses and mires**

- **River Bollin has a fragile recovering salmon population but adverse water quality, poor habitat & barriers to migration are impeding recovery**

## **River Bollin**

- **Key habitat challenges include:**

   - **Artificial or heavily modified river channels**

   - **Culverted reaches of river**

   - **Legacy industrial weirs**

   - **Transport impacts: road, rail, airport**

   - **Legacy of peat extraction industry**

## **River Bollin**

- **Key water quality challenges include:**

   - **Agricultural diffuse pollution and erosion impact, especially from dairy farming**

   - **Leachate from former landfill and waste disposal sites**

   - **Industrial pollution from chemicals used or stored near the river**

   - **Intermittent combined sewer overflows**

- **No continuous monitoring of river water quality in the catchment**

- **Limited “spot” sampling of water quality**
