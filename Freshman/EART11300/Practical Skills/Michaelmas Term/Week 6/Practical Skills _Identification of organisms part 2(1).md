- Has multiple segments

- Based heavily on the **chaetae**

- Chaetae (could be single or multiple pointed) require high magnification microscope to see them

- Anterior end is thicker than the posterior end

- The very front is the location of a rounded (or sometimes proboscislike) lobe called the prostomium

- The **clitellum** is a swollen area in which sexual organs are situated . The clitellum is always situated towards the front of the animal if present

- The segments are numbered from front to rear

- Pollution Score = 1

Un-segmented: No obvious head but eyes present; Distinct gliding movement

- Often black or grey

- Flat because they lack a fluid – filled body cavity

- Bilaterally symmetrical

- Soft bodied

- Pollution Score = 5

### Crustacea

- Characterized by 2 antennae

- Some are vestigial –i.e. hidden by an external carapace

- Some antennae are adapted as feeding or even locomotory organs so they are difficult to identify as antennae

- The antennae is a sensory organ found in most arthropods that occur in pairs attached to the anterior body segment in the head.

- Antennae are normally sub-divided into a series of smaller units (segments)

##### Water louse (Isopoda)

- Crustaceans have 2 pairs of antennae, insects have a pair
