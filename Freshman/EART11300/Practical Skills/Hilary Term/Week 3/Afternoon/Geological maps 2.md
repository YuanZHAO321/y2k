## **Drawing a cross-section on a map with an unconformity**

So far we have drawn stratum contours on maps where all the strata of different ages have the same orientation (e.g. Exercise 2). In reality, different series of strata may have different orientations (i.e. have different strikes and dips), this is always the case if there is an angular unconformity present on the map. NB remember from your EART11100 Understanding the Earth workshops that an angular unconformity is where older strata are tilted, eroded and exposed as a depositional surface and then an unconformable sequence of younger strata are deposited on top of them.
