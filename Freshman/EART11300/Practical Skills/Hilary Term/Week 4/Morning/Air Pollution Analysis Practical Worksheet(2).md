Section 2: Correlation Analysis

What is the relationship between:

Electricity consumption and CO2 emissions?

Precipitation and PM2.5?

I have created a template graph for you. The R² value tells you how well traffic (for example) explains the variation in CO2 emissions. A higher R² (closer to 1) means a strong correlation, while a lower R² (closer to 0) means a weak correlation. The gradient (slope) of the line tells you how much the CO2 concentration changes per unit increase in traffic.

Are the CO2 emissions correlated more with an increase in electricity demand or traffic volume? Use the R2 and gradient of the best fit line to explain your answer.
