# **Fossils: An introduction to fossil preservation Answers**

Definition of a fossil:

A fossil represents part of a once living plant or animal or it has been formed by the actions of a once living plant or animal. A fossil or trace fossil also has to be prehistoric – from a time before any historical written records.

Some types of plants and animals are more likely to become fossilised than others e.g. an organism that dies on the land surface is liable to be scavenged, dismembered and decayed before there is any chance of burial and preservation. An organism which lives on the sea floor where there is rapid sedimentation stand a much better chance of burial and removal from scavengers and agents of decay. So, in general, marine organisms have a better preservation potential than terrestrial organisms.

Decide which of these organisms has the greater chance of being preserved as a fossil and write down your reasons: -

## a) a cow or a seal?

Although both a cow and a seal are mammals with robust skeletons the seal has the higher preservation potential due to the fact that it spends most of its life in the ocean, unlike the cow!

## b) A jellyfish or an oyster?

The oyster because it has a mineralised shell composed of calcium carbonate, in the form of the mineral calcite.

## c) Plankton or pollen?

Pollen is produced by seed bearing plants (common from the Mesozoic onwards) and so are terrestrial, however, they are very small and very resistant to chemical change and produced in vast numbers, so preservation potential is high – part of specialist subject called palynology see this web site for more details - <u>https://www.ucl.ac.uk/GeolSci/micropal/spore.html</u>

Plankton include both plants and animals and are organisms that live in water and are carried by drifting within the water column. Plankton found as fossils can include the diatoms, radiolarian, foraminifera, nanno planktons like coccolithophores

<u>https://www.ucl.ac.uk/GeolSci/micropal/welcome.html</u>

Both plankton and pollen have a high preservation potential, but the marine realm is much larger than the terrestrial, so overall I would have to go for plankton.
