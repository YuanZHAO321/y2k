## Slide 11

### Health and Safety

- Hazards

- Exposure (cold and sun)

- Falling objects from cliffs

- You falling / slipping

- Cut off by tide

- Mitigation

- Wear correct clothing

- Wear Helmet

- Take care go at your own pace

- Be aware of state of tide and escape points

- Biggest risk is normally due to excessive alcohol intake in evenings. So do not drink too much - and look after each other

- Alcohol is NOT to be consumed in the field. You will be sent home!

- Who is Responsible for Safety?

- Read Risk Assessment!

- On EART11300 BB site

- Sign off you have read it

- Fill out Medical Information Form.
