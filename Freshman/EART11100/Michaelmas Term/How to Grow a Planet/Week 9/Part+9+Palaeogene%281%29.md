## **Mesozoic Summary**

- Major groups of archosaurs originate in the aftermath of the Permian-Triassic mass extinction event (the big one)

- Vertebrates diversify and generally grow in body size in the Jurassic and Cretaceous whilst the climate becomes more humid and plants also diversify and spread

- The Mesozoic ends with an impact-related extinction

### **Adaptive Radiation**

An **adaptive radiation** is a rapid diversification of organisms from an ancestral species into a multitude of new forms, particularly to occupy newly available **niches** A good example is the adaptive radiation of Darwin’s **Galapagos Finches**

### **Ecological Niche**

Each organism has an **ecological niche** : the _position_ of a species within an ecosystem in terms of the range of conditions it can persist in This includes the **biotic environment** (interactions with other species such as competitors, predators, etc) and **abiotic environment** (e.g. temperature, O2 etc).

### **Palaeocene-Eocene Thermal Maximum**

Palaeocene-Eocene thermal maximum ( **PETM** ) temperatures were about 5-8º higher (greenhouse world) than today (ice house world)

Massive injection of atmospheric CO2 over a short period of time. Possible causes include volcanism, comet impacts, and their effects on carbon cycles and ocean circulation
