## Slide 6

- Chlamydomonas reinhardtii

- Green alga

- Lives in fresh water and on soil

- Haploid most of the time

- Mitosis gives 4 daughter cells

- Sexual reproduction when stressed

- Gametophytes in 2 mating types	mt+ and mt- (genetically set)

- Persistent Zygospore – diploid, inactive, survives stress

## Slide 15

- Liverworts

- Marchantia polymorpha

- By J.F Gaffard CC BY-SA 3.0, https://commons.wikimedia.org/w/index.php?curid=1666055

- Dominant gametophyte - may grows as a plate -thalloid

- Or may be “leafy”

- Specialised cells, not all photosynthetic

- Rhizoids grow down and absorb water

- Primitive water transport

- Gametophores grow up – male vs female

- Sperm swim from male gametophores (from antheridia) to female ones (to archegonia)

- Sporophyte grows on gametophore
