# **End Ordovician Extinction Event**

At the end of the Ordovician was the first of the ‘big five’ mass extinction events

It was actually two pulses of extinction close to each other in time

The first pulse was related to global cooling, massive glaciation and drop in sea-level The second was related to changing ocean cycles and widespread anoxia, warming again _Harper et al 2014 Gondwana Research_

# **Late Devonian Extinction(s)**

Within the **jawed vertebrates,** placoderms and lobe-finned fishes decline at End Devonian More modern clades (Chondrichthyes and Actinopterygians) thrive after The cause(s) of the end Devonian extinctions is unclear, but there were at least two pulses of extinction

Body sizes was increasing in all vertebrates toward the end Devonian The extinction event(s) wiped out large bodied vertebrates Mostly small bodied afterwards with slow recovery to former sizes

# **Silurian and Devonian Summary**

- Vertebrates originate in the Cambrian ( _Metaspriggina_ from Burgess Shale)

- Armoured jawless fishes (ostracoderms) diversify during Ordovician and Silurian
