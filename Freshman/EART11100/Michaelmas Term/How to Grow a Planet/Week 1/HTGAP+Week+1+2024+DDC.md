EART11100 How to Grow a Planet Lecture 1: In the beginning…

What is the course about?

What is Life? Where did it come from? How did it evolve? Why are there lots of different species? What do they all do? The story of life on Earth

### **Learning objectives**

After this week’s lecture and independent study you should be able to:

• Identify the main characteristics of living organisms and describe key features thought to characterise LUCA - the last universal common ancestor

- Define the major groups of organisms present on earth and explain

- how these are classified

- Explain that organisms use energy to make complex molecules out

- of simple ones

- Identify the major classes of biological molecules - proteins,

- carbohydrates lipids and nucleic acids

- Describe and contrast features of light capture in the archaen and

- bacterial lineages

**Codons** : combinations of 3 nucleotides code for 20 amino acids

**RNA** : single strand; acts as a “translator” of the DNA code
