## Slide 5

- Angiosperm innovations:  Vessels

- Gymnosperms – tracheids:

- narrow hollow cells

- overlapping ends allow for water 			to flow

- Angiosperms – also xylem vessels

- Wider, better contact between 			cells so better water flow

- But… more prone to embolisms (air 			bubbles)

- [Also differences in phloem]

- Gnetophytes have vessels like angiosperms

## Slide 12

- Key steps in flower evolution

- Gymnosperms:   Male and female cones

- Pollen spread by wind –

- Uncontrolled;  risk of 			selfing/hybridisation

- Pollen stolen by insects

- Angiosperms:  Male and female organs come together

- So… pollen carried to female organ

- But… increased risk of self pollination

- and… hybridisation still possible
