# Understanding the Earth Workshop

Week 5 Earthquakes

### Learning objectives

In this workshop we will:

- compare and contrast the different types of seismic waves generated by earthquakes

- identify P and S waves generated by an earthquake on a seismograph

- locate an earthquake epicenter on a map using calculations made of the arrival times of P and S waves

- identify some natural hazards associated with earthquakes and assess the risk for different locations

- elucidate the relationship between the oil and gas exploration and seismicity
