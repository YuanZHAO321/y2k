## Introduction to lithostratigraphy

_Recap from last week’s workshop:_

Law of superposition: in any succestion of strata (rock layers) that has not been overturned since being deposited, younger rocks lie on top of older rocks.

Contacts: planar or irregular surfaces that separate diffeternt rock units (lithologies). Contacts can be abrupt or gradational.

Unconformity: a surface of erosion or non-deposition separating youger strata from older rocks that represents a signbificant gap in time.

Lithostratigraphy subdivides the rock record into bodies of rock that have specific lithological characteristics and stratigraphic position relative to other bodies of rock.

## The division of geological time today

The golden spike defining the base of the Ediacaran Period (635 Ma), Enorama Creek, Flinders Ranges, South Australia

- Global Stratotype Section and Point (GSSP) _aka a_ “golden spike”

   - define the base of geochronological units

   - physical point within a succession of strata at a particular place somewhere in the world

   - rocks at GSSP dated (i.e. located in an absolute time scale)
