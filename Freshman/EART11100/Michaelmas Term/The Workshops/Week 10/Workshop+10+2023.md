## Fermi’s Argument

**If they existed, they would be here** - _Enrico Fermi_

- Galaxy scale is ~100,000 light years*  (90 Pm).

- In a few centuries we might expect to be able to

   - build a probe capable of 0.01c**.

   - make the probe self-replicating on arrival at another system.  A **_Von Neumann probe_** .  (Could be dangerous… Universal Paperclips**)

   - Takes 10 million years to colonise the galaxy

- The galaxy is ~13.8 billion years old

   - *1 light year is the distance light travels in one year.

   - **c is the speed of light in a vacuum.

**https://www.decisionproblem.com/paperclips/ (if you have a few days to waste)

#### Where are the alien civilizations?

Should we expect to have evidence of them? Fermi says “Yes”.

But do they exist?

Several decades ago a way of addressing the question was proposed, now known as... “The Drake Equation”.

Using it relies on the _Principle of Mediocrity_ .

# Our ideas about the universe.

- Geocentric view – the Earth was the centre of the universe.  Special

- Heliocentric view – placed the sun at the centre of the universe.

- Modern view – the sun is just one star in a galaxy, and there are many galaxies.

# Principle of Mediocrity

_There is nothing special about the solar system or the planet Earth._

_We view the universe from an average position, as if we picked a place and time at random._

## How the argument works…

- Assume our solar system is typical of planetary systems – assume the principle of mediocrity is correct.

- Use what we know about it (and can see from it) to estimate how many other civilizations there are in the galaxy right now – The Drake Equation.

- If the estimate suggests there are lots of civilizations out there, the fact that we haven’t been visited suggests our assumption is wrong.

- This would our solar system has some unusual features that make it favourable for civilizations like ours.

- What might they be?

# The Drake Equation

* _NC_  _N f pne fl fi fc f L_

- Nc = Number of communicating/travelling civilizations in the galaxy today.

- N<sup>*</sup> = Number of stars in our galaxy.

- fp = Fraction of stars with planets.

- ne = Planets per star capable of supporting life.

- fl = fraction of ne where life starts.

- fi = given life, fraction intelligence evolves.

- fc = fraction of intelligent life that develops technology to enable space exploration (Fermi style).

- fL = fraction of planet’s lifetime for which exploration continues.

• A civilization that can compare its environment with other places in the universe.

- How common are these (or places where they

- might form and survive)?

• If they are rare, then something about our solar system might be special.

• The absence of alien visitors tests the Principle of Mediocrity
