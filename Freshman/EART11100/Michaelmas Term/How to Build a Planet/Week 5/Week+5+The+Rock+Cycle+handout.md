## Stage 4

Oceanic crust and sedimentary rocks from continental margin subduct beneath a continent. Subducting plate melts as it descends.  Magma rises from melting plate and mantle to form volcanic mountain chain and cools to make granitic igneous rocks. The volcanoes inject ash and CO2 into the atmosphere, affecting global climate

The Rock Cycle

## Stage 5

Further closure of the ocean basin leads to continental collision, forming high mountain ranges.  Where continents collide, rocks are buried deeper and modified by heat and pressure, forming metamorphic rocks. Uplifted mountains force moisture-laden air to rise, cool, condense, and precipitate.  Weathering creates sediment that can be eroded
