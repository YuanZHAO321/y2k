## _t_ ~  10<sup>-12</sup> s The appearance of mass:

- The Universe is permeated with an energy field (Higgs Field)

- If it did not exist no particles would have mass and would move at light speed

- Just after the Big Bang the Higgs field was zero

- As the universe cooled, the field grew spontaneously

- As other particles pass through the field, their interactions with Higgs bosons confers mass on them

- The visible manifestation of energy fluctuations in the Higgs field is the Higgs Boson

mass is a resistance to force (or via _F_ = _ma_ ) to acceleration

A cocktail party analogy If the guest-filled room is the Higgs field, clumps of guests within it are Higgs bosons. The passage of another particle (celebrity or otherwise) across the room is slowed down (experiences resistance) as it has to negotiate its way around and through the clumps of other guests- it has acquired mass

### Hadron Epoch: The first normal matter forms:  10<sup>-6</sup> s  < _t_ ≤  1 s

The quark structure of protons and neutrons

By 10<sup>-6</sup> s, the Universe had cooled to a temperature of 3 x 10<sup>12</sup> K

- the first normal matter formed: quarks combined to form hadrons (including protons and neutrons)

### Photon Epoch: Primordial nucleosynthesis: 3 min < _t_ ≤  20 min

• By 3 min, the Universe had cooled to a temperature of 10<sup>9</sup> K • protons and neutrons combine through nuclear fusion to form simple elements of hydrogen, helium and lithium • By ~ 20 min the temperature and density of the Universe had fallen to values where nuclear fusion could not continue • the elemental abundances in the Universe were then set at ~ 75% hydrogen and ~25% helium with trace lithium, abundances that have been only slightly changed through subsequent stellar nucleosynthesis

The dominant fusion reactions that led to the production of helium-4

Primordial nucleosynthesis effectively stopped at helium-4

Cosmic Dark Ages:     380 kyr  < _t_ <  150 myr

- All the atoms and light in the universe make up <5% of the content.

- The rest is dark matter and dark energy, which are invisible but dominate the structure and evolution of the universe.

- • Dark energy is driving the accelerated expansion of the universe.

## The future of expansion

The currently observable Universe is estimated to comprise: 68.5% dark energy 26.6% dark matter 4.9% normal matter with only trace amounts of radiation

Dark energy has become increasingly dominant resulting in an accelerating expansion of the Universe beginning ~ 7500 years after the Big Bang
