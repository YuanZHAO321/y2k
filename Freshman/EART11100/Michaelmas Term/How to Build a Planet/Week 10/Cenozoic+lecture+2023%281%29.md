## Atlantic Meridional Overturning Circulation (AMOC)

~33 Ma deep ocean connection est.  between Arctic and North Atlantic

~15-13 Ma Panama Isthmus _formed and gateway closed separating Atlantic and Pacific Oceans_

Gulf stream _Delivers warm surface waters to N Atlantic_

Ice masses started appearing in the Northern Hemisphere ~5 Ma, although the growth of ice sheets on Arctic land masses did not begin until after ~3 Ma when AMOC system intensified.

### Onset of the Quaternary ice ages

- The following were important in establishing conditions for the onset of the Quaternary glaciations:

➢ Position of continents allowing ice build-up at poles Continents at poles (Antarctic case) → growth of ice sheets  OR Continents form a ring around the poles (Arctic case) → prevent sea ice dispersal  (Cenozoic cooling trend)

- ➢ Polar regions allowed to cool

Circum-polar oceanic currents → extract heat from polar continent (opening of Tasman and Drake Passages, 35 Ma)

- ➢ Relatively low levels of greenhouse gases

Tectonic uplift and enhanced weathering → removal of CO2 from atmosphere (uplift of Himalayas, Miocene)

➢ Delivery of atmospheric moisture to polar regions flow of shallow, warm ocean currents from the tropics to high latitudes → increased precipitation at high latitudes (intensification of the Atlantic Meridional Overturning Circulation - system after closure of the Panama Gateway, 15 13 Ma)

- Early Middle Pleistocene Transition (EMPT) changes in symmetry and intensification of glacial–interglacial climate cycles between about 1.2-800k.

- Low-amplitude 41-ka obliquity-forced climate cycles of the earlier Pleistocene were replaced by high-amplitude 100-ka cycles.

- These later cycles are distinctly asymmetrical and indicative of slow ice build-up and subsequent rapid melting.

- Ice sheet aerial extent before and after the EMPT was similar but ice volume afterwards was greater, and therefore the sheets were much thicker.

###### **Main hypothesis:**

Catastrophic drainage of Lake Agassiz (glacial lake in US), leading to freshwaterinduced Atlantic Meridional Overturning Circulation (AMOC) collapse and abrupt reduction of the associated northward heat transport.

###### **Alternative hypotheses:**

Extraterrestrial impact or volcanic – leading to increase atmospheric dust and cooling.

### **Summary of main events**

- The Cenozoic era (0-66 Ma) has a well-preserved geological record

- Climate switched from greenhouse conditions to icehouse conditions at the present day as recorded by oxygen isotopes in benthic microfossils.

- Max. temperatures occurred 50 Ma ago during the Eocene Climatic Optimum caused by Pacific Ocean circulation carrying warm waters to high latitudes.

- • PETM hyperthemal event (55.5 Ma) related to volcanic CO2 from North Atlantic Igneous Province, ocean acidification and methane release (gas hydrates)

- First continental icesheets present at end-Eocene (35 Ma ago) due to thermal isolation of Antarctica by ACC from opening of oceanic gateways.

- Permanent southern polar ice sheets at 32 Ma resulting from atmospheric CO2 drawdown by Himalayan uplift

- Arctic ice sheets established by 3 Ma ago by intensification of the AMOC (closure of oceanic gateways) leading to increases precipitation in polar regions.

- Quaternary marine and ice core oxygen isotope records of glaciations related to Earth’s orbital variations in 100 Ka (eccentricity) and 41 Ka (obliquity) cycles with transition (EMPT).

- Since the Last Glacial Maximum (LGM) a combination of eustatic sea level rise (global = 130 m) and isostatic rebound (local = glacial unloading of land surface).
