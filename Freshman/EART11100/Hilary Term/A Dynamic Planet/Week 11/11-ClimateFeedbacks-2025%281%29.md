## Slide 28

### The Glacial Buzzsaw

- Glaciers erode mountain peaks.

- Mountains undergoing active orogeny are inhibited from rising much above the elevation of permanent glaciation, leaving just glaciated peaks.

## Slide 31

### Limitation of the Glacial Buzzsaw

- Glaciers that are frozen to the bedrock protect mountains from erosion more than they erode them (e.g., Patagonia, Antarctica, Alaska).

-  Why glacial buzzsaw relationship breaks down at higher latitudes

- (Thomson et al. 2010, doi:10.1038/nature09365)

## Slide 49

- Summary

- Climate feedbacks help us understand the interactions between the different climate components.

- Glacial buzzsaw limits the growth of mountains.

- Weathering of silicates removes carbon dioxide from the atmosphere.

- Hot-house climates limit the decay of organic material in ocean.

- Be careful interpreting single-site changes in climate in your fieldwork.

## Slide 67

- Although the Earth has undergone large changes over geologic time, the patterns of moist tropics and dry subtropics with polar jet streams are relatively consistent.

- Details about these features change and their locations may shift slightly on the planet, but the planetary-scale circulation (Hadley cells, polar jet streams) is a consistent feature throughout geologic time.
