## Slide 18

### Tipping points of Biodiversity

- Mammal, amphibian, and invertebrate richness decreases with logging intensity

- Increases in bird species richness are caused by the influx of habitat generalists

- Logging intensity of 38 m3 ha−1 would cause halving of mammal richness

- Logging intensity of 63 m3 ha−1 would cause halving of amphibian richness

- https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cub.2014.06.065

**Notes:**

Logging causes huge impacts on biodiversity….with huge potential to affect ecosystem functioning Some groups of animals reach thresholds or tipping points beyond which change is irreversible. Perhaps logging can be managed to ensure the survival of some key biodiversity groups? But we need to know what these thresholds are and be able to police the logging

### Mining: ClosureAcid Mine Drainage (AMD)

- Occurs at abandoned mine sites where groundwater is no longer pumped out of the mine

- Produced when Fe sulfide minerals are exposed to oxidation (water and/or air/bacteria)

- Form sulfuric acid, sulfate compounds and iron oxides

- Pyrite occurs with many coals

## Slide 31

### Mining: TailingsBrumadinho dam failure (Minas Gerais, Brazil) 2019

- 12 million m3 of iron ore tailings

- 270 people died

- Ba, Pb, As, Sr, Fe, Mn and Al, have high mobility from the tailings to water

- In the water, the levels of Fe, Al, Mn, Zn, Cu, Pb, Cd and U were higher than those allowed by Brazilian legislation.

- In the sediments, Cr, Ni, Cu and Cd levels were higher than the established sediment quality guidelines (TEL-NOAA)
