## Slide 21

- Alpha Decay

- Alpha decay is characteristic of heavy nuclei (A > 200)-

- ●Decay energies are typically 3.5-6 MeV

- ●Alpha particles are heavy and highly charged so interact readily with matter and have only a short range (5 cm in air)

- Pu239primary decay is via α –decay (half life 24,000yr)

## Slide 22

- Beta decay - negatron

- This decay mode is characteristic of lighter, neutron-rich nuclei, high speed electron or positron

- ● β particles have a range of energies a few keV (3H) up to 2-3 MeV (90Y)

- ● Low mass, medium energy. Less damaging but ~3m in air

- e.g. Tc99 , Cs134

## Slide 28

### Radwaste definitions

- High-level waste: (HLW)

- Produces radioactivity and heat.

- From reprocessing spent fuel, nitric acid solutions and glasses

- Intermediate-level waste (ILW)

- High radioactivity but little heat

- Steel, graphite, concrete, cement and sand, sludges etc. (e.g. from buildings)

- Low-level waste (LLW)

- Wastes less than 4 GBqs per tonne of alpha activity or 12 Gbq per tonne of beta/gamma activity

- Plastics, paper and metal, soils, building materials and plant.

- Very low-level waste (VLLW)

- Controlled or general landfill depending on volume.

- https://ukinventory.nda.gov.uk/wp-content/uploads/2021/03/20201222-Official-Rep-PO023346_fact-sheet-03.pdf

### Wind turbine

- Kinetic energy = 0.5mv2

- m = rAv

- Therefore:

- KE per sec (power)

- =0.5rAv3

- m = mass of air through turbine

- v = velocity of the air

- r = density of the air

- A = swept area of the turbine

- Wind turbines rotor blades, made from e.g. fibreglass-reinforced polyester

- 30-80 m diameter

- the longer the blades, the greater the energy output.

- rotate at 10-30 revolutions per minute

- Rotational energy is converted into electricity

## Slide 47

### Hydroelectric power

- Usually generated at large dams in order to increase the vertical drop of the water

- Energy available (in J) = m x g x h

- m = mass of water available in kg

- g = acceleration due to gravity (9.81 ms-1)

- h = height differential

- By using two reservoirs at different heights, water can be pumped uphill to store energy during periods of low demand

### Biofuels and waste products

- Any solid, liquid or gaseous fuel derived from recently living organisms
