#### Biaxial indicatrix: two optic axes

###### Minerals with three refractive indices have a biaxial indicatrix

- The shape of the biaxial indicatrix is a **general ellipsoid**

- The refractive indices are represented by the lengths of the semiaxes of the ellipsoid, and are denoted a , b and g

- a, b and g are always at 90° to each other

- By convention, a < b < g . Light travels fastest along a and slowest along g

- There are two possible 2D slices that give circular cross-sections. The circular sections both have radius b

- The optic axis is defined as the direction we look down to see the circular sections (i.e., the optic axes are perpendicular to the circular sections)
