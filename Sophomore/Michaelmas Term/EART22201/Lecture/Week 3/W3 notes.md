### Isomorphism

Same structure, different chemistry

Example: All the possible compositions of the olivine solid solution

### Polymorphism

Same chemistry, different structure Example: Water, ice, water vapour

See also EART 26201 Principles of Geochemistry, Lecture 3

The stable polymorph is determined by the pressure and temperature conditions. Polymorphs found outside their stable P-T range are said to be **metastable** .

### Which alkali feldspar is it? What should I call it in my rock description?

It is often possible to give a more detailed mineral identification than just “alkali feldspar” based on the type of twinning, presence/absence of perthitic exsolution, and presence/absence of zoning.

||**Cooling rate**|**Symmetry**|**Twinning**|**Perthite lamellae**|**Zoning**|
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
|Sanidine|fast|monoclinic|simple|no|rare|
|Orthoclase|intermediate|monoclinic|simple|yes|possible|
|Microcline|very slow|triclinic|cross-hatch|yes|possible|

1. Look for twinning, exsolution and zoning. Use your observations to decide the most appropriate name for the feldspar.

2. Hence, interpret the cooling rate of the rock

### Quartz

###### Distinguish from feldspars by the absence of twinning, zoning or alteration

Key optical properties:

- Very low relief

PPL

- Colourless

- No cleavage

- Never undergoes alteration

- Low first-order interference colours

XP

- No twinning

- No zoning

- May show patchy or undulose extinction if the rock has been deformed
