##### Volatile solubility and degassing

**Fragmentation:** If viscosity and bubble pressure are high enough, an explosive eruption will occur.

**Vesiculation:** H2O exsolves and gas bubbles form. At the same time, T is decreasing and the crystal content is increasing, so viscosity increases.

High pressure: All H2O is entirely dissolved in the melt.

##### Evolution of magma viscosity during ascent through a volcanic conduit

- **In conduit, during magma ascent** (decreasing pressure): • Temperature decreasing

- • H2O solubility decreasing Viscosity increases

- • Crystal content increasing

- **In magma reservoir at depth** (high pressure): • Temperature is high*

- • H2O solubility is high* Viscosity is low*

- • Crystal content is low*

##### Pyroclasts from explosive eruptions

**Tephra:** the fragmental material produced by a volcanic eruption – regardless of composition, fragment size, or emplacement mechanism.

**Tuff:** a lithified rock made of tephra ejected by a volcanic eruption.

The components of a tuff are:

- Volcanic pumice, scoria and glass

- Crystals and crystal fragments

- Lithic (rock) fragments

##### Two types of ignimbrite deposit: unwelded and welded

###### Unwelded ignimbrites

- Massive and poorly sorted: large pumice and lithic clasts in a fine ashy matrix

- May see reverse grading

###### Welded ignimbrites

- Massive and poorly sorted

- Layered appearance due to collapsed, flattened and elongated pumices, referred to as fiammé

- Harder to see any original grading

- A welded ignimbrite containing fiamme has a eutaxitic texture

- Layered or banded texture created by compaction of compaction of glass shards and pumice fragments

- Banding wraps around undeformed crystals and lithic fragments

##### Cone sheets and ring dykes

Magma chamber inflating: Cone sheets

Dykes arranged in a curving array or set, that all dip steeply towards the magma chamber

Magma chamber deflating: Ring dykes

Intrusions that are circular or arcute in plan form, with steeply dipping contacts. Most commonly form after caldera collapse
