#### The five types of 3D unit cell

**Primitive (P)** Lattice points only at corners

**Face-centred (F)** Lattice points at corners and at the centre of every face

**Rhombohedral (R)** Lattice points only at corners

Lattice points at corners, and at the centre of one pair of opposite faces

**Body-centred (I)** Lattice points at corners and at the intersection of the body diagonals

margaret.hartley@manchester.ac.uk

EART 22201

#### How to work out the number of lattice points per 3D unit cell

**Primitive (P)** and **Rhombohedral (R)** Corners: 8 × ⅛ **1 lattice point per unit cell**

**Body-centred (I)** Corners: 8 × ⅛ Fully enclosed: 1

**2 lattice points per unit cell**

Lattice points on corners = ⅛ Lattice points on faces = ½ Lattice points fully enclosed = 1

**Face-centred (F)** Corners: 8 × ⅛ Faces: 6 × ½

- **4 lattice points per unit cell**

See if you can work out the number of lattice points in a C face-centred unit cell
