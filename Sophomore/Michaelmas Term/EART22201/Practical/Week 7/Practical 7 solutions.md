# **Introduction**

The composition of magma that is erupted at the Earth’s surface depends on:

- The composition of the source mantle

- The amount of partial melting

- The process by which the melting occurred

- The fractional crystallization history of the magma between source and surface.

In this practical we will be investigating the origins of two suites of basaltic rocks from Hawaii.
