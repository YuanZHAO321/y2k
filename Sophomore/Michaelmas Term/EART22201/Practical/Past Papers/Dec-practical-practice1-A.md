### 6) Any two of the following:

Plagioclase – Most crystals have oscillatory zoning, probably formed in response to changing magma composition due to magma mixing. Evidence for disequilibrium is the sieve textures within the oscillatory zoning, where the crystal has partially resorbed at its edges before continuing to grow.

Hornblende: (a) Crystals are often rounded, which forms when crystals partially resorb into the melt, thus evidence for disequilibrium. (b) Thin, dark, fine-grained reaction rims surround some crystals – evidence that the hormblende crystals spent time outside their stability field before the magma was erupted.
