## Slide 23

### Phase diagrams and mineral textures: pyroxenes

- Slow cooling (equilibrium)

- Di-rich augite is the first solid to crystallise

- Melt and augite become richer in En until the eutectic is reached, where Di-poor pigeonite and Di-rich augite crystallise

- Once the system has solidified, augite exsolves from pigeonite and vice versa

- Augite becomes richer in Di

- Pigeonite becomes poorer in Di

- Eventually, Di-rich pigeonite undergoes a phase transition to Di-poor orthopyroxene

- Orthopyroxene exsolves from augite and vice versa, changing compositions further

## Slide 24

### Phase diagrams and mineral textures: pyroxenes

- Moderate cooling (close to equilibrium)

- Di-rich augite is the first solid to crystallise

- Melt and augite become richer in En until the eutectic is reached, where Di-poor pigeonite and Di-rich augite crystallise

- Once the system has solidified, augite exsolves from pigeonite and vice versa

- Augite becomes richer in Di

- Pigeonite becomes poorer in Di

- However, exsolution takes time (kinetics) and may not run to completion

- Di-rich Pigeonite does not go through phase transition to Di-poor orthopyroxene

- Augite and pigeonite are homogenous and retain compositions from eutectic

- Di-rich pigeonite does not go through phase transition to Di-poor orthopyroxene
