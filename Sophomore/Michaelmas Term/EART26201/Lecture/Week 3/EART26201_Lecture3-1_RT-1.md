### Equilibrium in Earth systems: from mantle to surface

- Equilibrium is reached when constituents of a system do not change with time

- At equilibrium, basaltic magmas are liquid under mantle conditions, but…

- …solid under surface conditions

- Crystallisation reflects the re-equilibration of hot magmas with cooler surroundings

- Thermodynamics lets us quantitatively describe chemical changes in the Earth

## Slide 23

### Equilibrium and fractional processes

- Equilibrium melting and crystallisation

- Solid and liquid remain in equilibrium with each other

- Fractional melting

- As liquids are formed, they are removed and are no longer in equilibrium with the solid

- Fractional crystallisation

- As solids are formed, they are removed and are no longer in equilibrium with the liquid
