## Slide 32

### The Nd isotope evolution of the crust and mantle

- Analogous to Sr isotope evolution of the crust and mantle except...

- (143Nd/144Nd)0 is estimated from chondrites because Sm and Nd are refractory (unlike volatile Rb)

- The parent isotope (147Sm) is more compatible than the daughter isotope (143Nd) – opposite of Rb-Sr

- Thus, Depleted Mantle evolves to high 143Nd/144Nd while Continental Crust evolves to low 143Nd/144Nd

## Slide 36

### The mantle array: summarising Sr and Nd isotopes

- Negative correlation between Sr and Nd isotopes for most basalts

- Mid-ocean ridge basalts are sourced from the Depleted Mantle

- Ocean island basalts sourced from mantle plumes are often similar to Bulk Silicate Earth (recycling)

- Continental basalts are from the mantle but are contaminated by melts from the Continental Crust

## Slide 40

### Tracking changes in mantle source beneath Hawaii

- Compositions of lavas erupted on Hawaiian islands change with time

- Lavas erupted early and late in a volcano’s lifecycle (Alkalic Pre- and Post-Shield) are relatively depleted

- Lavas from the main phase of volcano growth (Tholeiitic Shield) are more enriched and closer to Bulk Silicate Earth (mantle plume)
