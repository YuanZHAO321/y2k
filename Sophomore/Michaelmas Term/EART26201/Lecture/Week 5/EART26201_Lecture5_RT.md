## Slide 19

### Mass transfer in geochemical systems

- Theory assumes that reactants are always available for reaction, but…

- Reactants in geochemical systems are often isolated from each other

- Many reactions can only take place at the interfaces between phases

- Phases are often locked within large reservoirs (oceans, the mantle etc.)

- Mass transfer is thus often required for reactions to occur:

- Advection: physical bulk movement

- Diffusion: spontaneous movement down a concentration gradient

- Semester 1

- EART26201 | Principles of Geochemistry | Lecture 5 | Part 4

- 19

## Slide 20

### Advection versus diffusion

- Advection

- Reactants brought into contact by bulk physical movement (stirring)

- External forces (e.g. gravity)

- Diffusion

- Reactants brought into contact by spontaneous movement down a concentration gradient

- Diffusion occurs all the time, but is only visible when homogenising a concentration gradient
