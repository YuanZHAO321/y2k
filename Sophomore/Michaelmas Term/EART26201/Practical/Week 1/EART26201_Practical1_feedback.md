- j) The two unknown basalts were erupted in different tectonic settings. Given what you

know about the behaviour of the elements identified in i) during subduction, which basalt

was erupted from an arc volcano? Where might the other basalt have erupted?

Unknown basalt 2 probably erupted in an arc because HFSE depletions are consistent with flux melting in the mantle source driven by the release of HFSE-poor aqueous fluids from subducting slabs. Unknown basalt 1 probably erupted at a midocean ridge where the mantle source was dry. Moreover, it is relative depleted in highly incompatible trace elements, which suggests it did not erupt from an ocean island (i.e. hot spot) volcano.

- k) Given your answer to j), can you explain the difference in the Rb and Ba contents of the two unknown basalts?

Unknown basalt 2 probably contains high concentrations of Rb and Ba, large ion lithophile elements, because they were transported in the aqueous fluids released from subducting slabs; unknown basalt 1 does not contain any slab-derived fluids.
