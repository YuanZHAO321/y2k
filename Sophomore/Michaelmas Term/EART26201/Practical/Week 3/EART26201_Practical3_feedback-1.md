- c) How many components are there in the system, and what are they?

- d) How many degrees of freedom are there in a system with _X_ An = 0.5 at 1000 °C? How many at 1300 °C? You might find the Gibbs phase rule for an isobaric system useful:

𝑓= 𝑐−Φ + 1

At _X_ An = 0.5 and 1000 °C, 𝑐 = 2 , Φ = 1 and therefore 𝑓 = 2 , which means that both _T_ and _X_ An can be changed without changing the system (i.e. changing Φ )

At _X_ An = 0.5 and 1300 °C, 𝑐 = 2 , Φ = 2 and therefore 𝑓 = 1 , which means that _T_ and _X_ An cannot be changed without altering the system because although Φ will remain 2, the proportions of _L_ and _plag_ will change.

- e) At what temperature does a liquid of composition _X_ An = 0.5 start to crystallise?

- b) What is the equilibrium phase assemblage of system with the composition _X_ Tol = 0.4 at

365 K? What are equilibrium compositions and proportions of the phases present?

The equilibrium assemblage is _V + L. V_ has a composition of _X_ Tol ~ 0.27 and _L_ has a composition of _X_ Tol ~ 0.51. _V_ and _L_ are present in proportions of ~0.43 and ~0.57, respectively.

- c) Quantitatively describe the equilibrium and fractional condensation of a vapour with the composition _X_ Tol = 0.4.

Equilibrium: First L condenses at ~369 K with an _X_ Tol of 0.63. All V has condensed by ~362 K when L has a composition of _X_ Tol = 0.4 and the last V has a composition of _X_ Tol ~ 0.2.

Fractional: First L condenses at ~369 K with an _X_ Tol of 0.63. L is continually removed from the system, with V and L compositions evolving along the vaporus and liquidus respectively until the system is pure Benzene ( _X_ Tol = 0) at 353 K.

- d) How could you synthesise pure toluene from a liquid with the composition _X_ Tol = 0.2?

Pure toluene could be produced by fractional vaporisation – heating the system and removing the vapour such that the final fraction of liquid – pure toluene – can be separated. This is fractional distillation.
