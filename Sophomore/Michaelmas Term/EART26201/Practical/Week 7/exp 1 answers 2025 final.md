- If the titrations were continued past the precipitation end-point and the volume of potassium bromide solution added recorded and used in the calculations for n, which equation outlined in section 2 would become invalid and why?

The equation that would become invalid would be the one below:

AgBr (s) ⇋ Ag+ (aq) + Br- (aq)

It would become invalid because the solution would become cloudier, which means that there is more of the solid AgBr within the solution and that it is no longer in equilibrium. So, the above equation, which represent equilibrium wouldn’t be valid anymore and the amount of Br used would not (via Ksp for AgBr) not equate to amount of Ag ions remaining in solution.
