- Label the solidus, liquidus and phase fields on a binary phase diagram

- d) How could you distinguish between rocks produced during fractional crystallisation of

liquids with initial compositions of _X_ Di = 0.1 and _X_ Di = 0.8 in the field?

Fractional crystallisation of XDi = 0.1 would produce rocks of pure anorthite and then rocks with a mixture of anorthite and diopside. The rocks of pure anorthite would be more voluminous than the mixed rocks because the bulk composition is nearer anorthite than the eutectic.

Fractional crystallisation of XDi = 0.9 would produce rocks of pure diopside and then rocks with a mixture of anorthite and diopside. The rocks of pure diopside would be more voluminous than the mixed rocks because the bulk composition is nearer diopside than the eutectic. However, the eutectic is nearer XDi = 0.9 than XDi = 0.1, so there would be relatively fewer diopside-only rocks in the XDi = 0.9 case than anorthite-only rocks on the XDi = 0.1 case.

- d) After discussion with your neighbours, briefly describe how each of the melting or

   - crystallisation scenarios would differ if the system had a composition of _X_ Qz = 0.28, _X_ Fo=

   - 0.72 instead of _X_ Qz = 0.32, _X_ Fo= 0.68.

## Equilibrium crystallisation:

When the bulk composition lies between Fo and En instead of between En and Qz, L will be exhausted before Fo during the peritectic reaction Fo + L = En, meaning that the last liquid will crystallise at the peritectic and the solid residue will contain Fo and En.

## Fractional crystallisation:

Because the peritectic reaction does not occur during fractional crystallisation, there will be no substantial change in the crystallisation behaviour. The only difference will be that slightly more Fo will crystallise from the liquid before the system composition passes through the peritectic point and En crystallises from the liquid instead of Fo.

Fractional melting:

The first liquid will form at the lowest temperature point of the phase diagram where Fo and En are in equilibrium with liquid. When the bulk composition lies between Fo and En instead of between En and Qz, the first liquid therefore forms at the peritectic rather than the eutectic.
