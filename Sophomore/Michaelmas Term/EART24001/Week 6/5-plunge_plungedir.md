# **PLOTTING  PLANES  AND  LINES**

## **PLOTTING LINES**

## **(a) which are specified as plunge and plunge direction (** **_e.g._ , 50/260)**

This procedure is the same as plotting planes which are specified as dip and dip direction, except that a point is plotted rather than the great circle.

1. Place the tracing paper over the stereonet and label the tracing paper with the north point.

2. Count around the perimeter of the stereonet and mark the tracing paper at the value of the plunge direction (here 260°).

3. Rotate the tracing paper until the marked plunge direction is on the E-W line of the stereonet.

4. Count in along the E-W line of the stereonet, from the marked plunge direction, the amount of plunge (here 50°), and draw a point.

5. Rotate the tracing paper so that its north point corresponds to north on the stereonet, and annotate the tracing paper with the reading of the line which has been plotted.
