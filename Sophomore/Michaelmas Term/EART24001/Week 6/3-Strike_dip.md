# **PLOTTING  PLANES  AND  LINES**

## **PLOTTING PLANES**

## **(a) which are specified as strike and dip (** **_e.g._ , 060/30SE)**

1. Place the tracing paper over the stereonet and label the tracing paper with the north point.

2. Count around the perimeter of the stereonet and mark the tracing paper at the value of strike (here 060°).

3. Rotate the tracing paper until the marked strike is on the north or south point of the stereonet.

4. Count in, along the E-W line of the stereonet, the amount of dip (here 30°), and draw in the great circle encountered there. Whether the count is in from the east side of the E-W line or the west side depends upon the direction of dip − when the tracing is rotated back so that north is north (see 6), the drawn great circle should bow in the direction of dip (here SE).

5. To plot the pole to the plane, count 90° along the E-W line from the great circle drawn in 4.

6. Rotate the tracing paper so that its north point corresponds to north on the stereonet, and annotate the tracing paper with the reading of the plane which has been plotted.
