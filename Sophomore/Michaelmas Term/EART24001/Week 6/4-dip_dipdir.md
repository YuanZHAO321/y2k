# **PLOTTING  PLANES  AND  LINES**

## **PLOTTING PLANES**

## **(b) which are specified as dip and dip direction (** **_e.g._ , 50/260)**

1. Place the tracing paper over the stereonet and label the tracing paper with the north point.

2. Count around the perimeter of the stereonet and mark the tracing paper at the value of the dip direction (here 260°).

3. Rotate the tracing paper until the marked dip direction is on the E-W line of the stereonet.

4. Count in along the E-W line of the stereonet, from the marked dip direction, the amount of dip (here 50°), and draw in the great circle encountered there.

5. To plot the pole to the plane, count 90° along the E-W line from the great circle drawn in 4.

6. Rotate the tracing paper so that its north point corresponds to north on the stereonet, and annotate the tracing paper with the reading of the plane which has been plotted.
