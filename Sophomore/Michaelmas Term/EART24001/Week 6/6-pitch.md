# **PLOTTING  PLANES  AND  LINES**

## **PLOTTING LINES**

## **(b) which are specified as pitch on a plane (** **_e.g._ , 40°NE on 060/30SE)**

1. Place the tracing paper over the stereonet and label the tracing paper with the north point.

2. Plot the great circle for the plane (here 060/30SE), that is, follow steps 2-4 given previously for plotting planes. .

3. Count in along the great circle, from the end from which the angle of pitch is specified (here NE), the amount of pitch (here 40°), and mark a point.

4. Determine the plunge and plunge direction of the marked point by (a) rotating it onto the E-W line of the stereonet, (b) counting in from the perimeter along the E-W line (amount of plunge, here 18°), and (c) marking the perimeter on the E-W line of the stereonet − the plunge direction (here 095°) may then be obtained after the rotation in step 5.

5. Rotate the tracing paper so that its north point corresponds to north on the stereonet, and annotate the tracing paper with the plunge and plunge direction of the line which has been plotted.
