# **Thickness of limestone**

_thickness of lmst may be measured from section or calculated using the sketch shown left (not to scale)_

<!-- Start of picture text -->
700 ft<br>b<br>x t<br>200 ft<br>8.1°<br>a 8.1°<br>top lmst<br>base lmst<br><!-- End of picture text -->

- _x_ = map distance between 200 ft base of lmst contour (blue) and 700 ft top of lmst contour (green) = 633 ft

_a_ = _x_ tan 8.1° = 90.25 ft _b_ = 500 ft _t_ = ( _a_ + _b_ ) cos 8.1° = 584 ft

- Strike lines for conglomerate/limestone and limestone/mudstone contacts (blue and green respectively) indicate that these three units all dip 030/8SE (=8/120)

- Dips: dolerite dyke 015/24W (= 24/285) ; ore vein 170/V (= 90°); fault 105/V (= 90°)

- Conglomerate, limestone, mudstone, dyke and vein are all offset by fault

- Strike lines for unconformity (magenta) are not affected by fault (same spacing north and south of fault) and indicate that the unconformity dips 095/3N (=3/005)

# **Sequence of Geological Events**

- **(1) Deposition of conglomerates, limestones and mudstones in that order**

- **(2) Intrusion of dolerite dyke** [ _crosscuts conglomerates, limestones and mudstones_ ]

- **(3) Tilting** [ _located here assuming that the vein and fault were originally vertical; if they were not, then the tilting is only constrained to after (1) but before (6)_ ]

- **(4) Intrusion of ore vein** [ _crosscuts dolerite dyke_ ]

- **(5) Faulting** [ _offsets ore vein_ ]

- **(6) Uplift and erosion to form unconformity** [ _not offset by fault_ ]

- **(7) Deposition of sandstones** [ _above unconformity_ ]

- **(8) Tilting** [ _sandstones are gently tilted_ ]

- **(9) Erosion to present topography**
