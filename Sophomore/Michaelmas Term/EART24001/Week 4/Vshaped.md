# **V-SHAPED  OUTCROP  PATTERNS**

A uniformly dipping surface which crops out across a valley can give rise to a V-shaped outcrop pattern in which the V points either upstream or downstream. The direction and sharpness of the V depends upon the dip of the surface as illustrated below.

Since folding may also produce similarly shaped outcrop patterns, when locating fold axial traces on maps it is important to pay attention to this topographically produced effect.
