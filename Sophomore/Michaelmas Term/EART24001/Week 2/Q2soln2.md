# **V-SHAPED  OUTCROP  PATTERNS**

A uniformly dipping surface which crops out across a valley can give rise to a V-shaped outcrop pattern in which the V points either upstream or downstream. The direction and sharpness of the V depends upon the dip of the surface and so its shape is useful for establishing approximately what this dip is
