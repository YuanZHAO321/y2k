# **V-SHAPED  OUTCROP  PATTERNS**

A uniformly dipping surface which crops out across a valley can give rise to a V-shaped outcrop pattern in which the V points either upstream or downstream. The direction and sharpness of the V depends upon the dip of the surface and so its shape is useful for establishing the approximate dip of the units.

On the maps below a contact between two units crops out at A. In each case the dip of the contact is indicated below the map. For each map complete the outcrop pattern of the contact and shade the area of the map which is structurally beneath the contact.
