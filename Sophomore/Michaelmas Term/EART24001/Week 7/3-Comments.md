## **Interpretative pointers**

- (1) Beware observer bias (more data from certain horizons or localities)

- (2) Analyse the data by stratigraphic position (changing current directions with time)

- (3) Analyse the data by locality (changing current directions with place), plotting the results on a map

- (4) Bear in mind how the palaeocurrent indicator was formed
