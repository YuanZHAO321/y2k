## Slide 3

- Petroleum systems exercise

- Overleaf shows a geological cross section through the south Pyrenean foreland basin in Spain (modified after Robert 2018), the geological column in a well drilled in the center, and the subsidence curve for that well. This data set allows an assessment of the petroleum system in the basin. The aim of the exercise is to complete the systems chart overleaf.

- Tasks:

- 1. Using the stratigraphic column, identify the petroleum systems elements (source, reservoir, seal, overburden). There may be more than one of each.

- 2. Determine from the cross section the most likely traps, where they are, their type (stratigraphic vs structural), and when they formed – the latter information should go in the systems chart.

- 3. Draw the subsidence chart for the well, establish the thermal history of the well, and determine timings for oil/gas generation (see last week’s exercise).

- 4. When was (were) the critical moment(s)?

- 5. Do you expect oil and/or gas in any reservoirs?
