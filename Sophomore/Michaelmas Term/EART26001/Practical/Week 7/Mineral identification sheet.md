# **Mineral identification sheet (1/4)**

## **Diamond**

**Formula: C**

**Color: colourless, yellowish to yellow, brown, black, blue, green or red, pink, champagne-tan, cognac-brown Lustre: adamantine, greasy Hardness: 10 Crystal System: Isometric**

**Diopside Formula: CaMgSi2O6**

**Color: light to dark green, blue, colourless, snow white, grey, pale violet**

**Lustre: vitreous, dull Hardness: 5.5-6.5 Crystal System: monoclinic**

**Garnet group Formula: (Mg,Ca,Fe,Mn)3Al2(SiO4)3**

**Color: almandine - Deep red, brownish red, red-violet, black;** **<u>pyrope - Blood red, orange red, purple red, pink, black red</u> Lustre: vitreous, resinous Hardness: 6.5-7**

**Crystal System: isometric**

# **Mineral identification sheet (2/4)**

## **Ilmenite**

**Formula: FeTiO3 Color: iron black, black**

**Lustre: metallic Hardness: 5-6 Crystal System: trigonal**

**K Feldspar Formula: (Na,K)AlSi3O8 Color: Colourless, white, grey, yellowish white, or reddish white**

**Lustre: vitreous, nacreous Hardness: 6**

**Crystal System: monoclinic/triclinic**

**Olivine**

**Formula: (Mg,Fe)2SiO4 Color: pale green, pale yellow, white Lustre: vitreous**

**Hardness: 7 Crystal System: orthorhombic**

# **Mineral identification sheet (3/4)**

## **Quartz**

**Formula: SiO2 Color: Colorless, purple, rose, red, black, yellow, brown, green, blue, orange**

**Lustre: vitreous**

**Hardness: 7**

**Crystal System: trigonal**

## **Calcite**

**Formula: CaCO3 Color: White, Yellow, Red, Orange, Blue, Green, Brown, Gray**

**Lustre: Vitreous, Resinous, Waxy, Pearly Hardness: 3 Crystal System: trigonal**

# **Mineral identification sheet (4/4)**

## **Halite**

**Formula: NaCl Color: Colorless, whitish, purple, red, yellow, blue Lustre: vitreous**

**Hardness: 2.5 Crystal System: cubic-isometric**

## **Mica**

**Formula: K(Mg,Fe)2(Al,Fe,Mg,Ti)2Si3O10(OH)2 (biotite) KAl2(AlSi3O10(OH)2 (muscovite)**

**Color: dark green/brown, black (biotite); White to colorless, silvery-white, and tinged various colors by impurities (muscovite) Lustre: vitreous Hardness: 2.5-3**

**Crystal System: monoclinic**
