# **Practical 4: PGE petrography and volumetrics calculation for a PGE deposit**

# **Part B**

This calculation illustrates the basic simplicity of maths used in reserve/resource calculations. Note that a number of uncertainties such as ore losses are not considered here. Full evaluations would also take a statistical approach. Data modified from Grobler (2019): https://doi.org/10.1007/s00126-0180800-x

# <u>Data:</u>

Consider a prospect on the northern limb of the Bushveld Complex, exploring for PGEs and Cr. The extract from the geological map, block diagram and cross section on the next 2 pages (Figs. 1-3) show the stratigraphy, dip of strata, main faults, and exploration data (boreholes, cross sections). The prospect area is outlined in the red dashed line. Exploration focuses on several units, including the Tennis Ball Marker (TBM, shown in blue on the map and block diagram).

Although in reality the rocks are folded, for this exercise consider the TBM as a simple sheet of rock with a thickness of 0.8 m.

The reef consists of a pegmatitic pyroxenite with the following modal composition: 70 wt% pyroxene (diopside, CaMgSi2O6) 25 wt% plagioclase (anorthite, CaAl2Si2O8) 2 wt% sulfide (pyrrhotite, Fe1-xS)

# <u>1. calculate rock volume:</u>

# **a. thickness is known: 0.8 m**

b. width: measure along outcrop section (can break it down into segments for more precision, or measure straight between prospect boundaries **width (measured straight) = 5488.4 m**

# c. length along dip

first determine the dip angle from cross section, or calculate using trigonometry  = 23°

tan  = 447.2 / 1053.5

d. length along depth to prospect boundary in SW (following dip section 9.0): horizontal distance from outcrop: 6790.7 m

dip length at this point: cos 23 = 6790.7 / x  x = 6790.7 / sin 23= 7377.6 m **dip length = 7377.6 m**

e. **volume:** 7377.6 x 5488.4 x 0.8 = 32392846.2 m<sup>3</sup> of rock = **32.4 x 10**<sup>**6**</sup> **m**<sup>**3**</sup>

# <u>2. convert to mass</u>

Need to determine bulk density of reef (find information via mindat.org): Pyroxene: density = 3.3 g/cm<sup>3</sup> = t/m<sup>3</sup> Plagioclase: density = 2.75 g/cm<sup>3</sup> = t/m<sup>3</sup> Sulfide: density = 4.62 g/cm<sup>3</sup> = t/m<sup>3</sup> Chromite: density = 4.65 g/cm<sup>3</sup> = t/m<sup>3</sup> Reef density: 70x3.3 + 25x2.75 + 2x4.62 + 3x4.65 = 3.23 g/cm<sup>3</sup> = t/m<sup>3</sup> **Mass of reef: 32392846.2 m**<sup>**3**</sup> **x 3.23 t/m**<sup>**3**</sup> **= 104628893.2 t = 104.6 10**<sup>**6**</sup> **t rock**

# <u>3. resources</u>

Take a grade from fig 3: range 3-5 g/t, eg 4 g/t 104628893.2 t x 4 g/t = 418515572.8 g PGE= 418.5 t PGE 1/3 is Pt: 13950519.1 g Pt = 4.9 million oz (1g = 0.035274 oz)
