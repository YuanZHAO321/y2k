# **Test**

An isotope of technicium, technicium-99, has a half-life of 6 hours. A hospital has a sample which contains 40 mg of technicium-99. Calculate how much technicium-99 will be in this sample after 12 hours.

After 1 half life (6 hours) the amount of technicium-99 will have halved from 40 mg to 20 mg. After another 6 hours, it will have halved again to 10 mg.

Porosity = Vpore / Vbulk

= 1414.1 cm<sup>3</sup>

V = V – V pore bulk grain
