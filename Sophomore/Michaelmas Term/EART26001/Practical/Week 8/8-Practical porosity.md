# **Practical 8 – Porosity determination**

# **Introduction**

Porosity is a fundamental property of rocks and sediments. It measures the capacity of a granular solid to store fluid (gas or liquid). Hence it determines the capacity of a reservoir (petroleum or water aquifer).

Porosity is defined as (vol. voids) / (vol. voids + vol. solids), expressed as a percentage or a fraction.

# **Learning outcomes**

By the end of this practical, you should be able to:

- explain methods to estimate total porosity

- describe a simple analytical technique, write up results, and interpret them scientifically

# **Materials**

In a group of 3 or 4, you are provided with:

- two rock cores, a sandstone (red) and a limestone (white)

- loose, 100% quartz sand in a plastic bottle

- plastic beaker

- calipers

- weighing scale

# **Task**

# <u>1. To determine the porosity of the core plugs:</u>

- Calculate the bulk volume

- Weigh the plug

- Calculate the bulk density

- Using the grain density provided, calculate the grain volume

- Calculate the pore volume

- Calculate porosity

# <u>2. To determine the porosity of the loose sand:</u>

- Weigh the container

- Fill the container with loose sand, weigh and subtract the weight of the container

- Calculate the bulk volume

- Calculate the bulk density

- Using the grain density provided, calculate the grain volume

- Calculate the pore volume

- Calculate porosity
