### **Diamond deposits Key learning points week 5**

**Diamonds (pure C) are the hardest material we know, and are used mainly as abrasive and as gemstones.**

- **Diamonds form in the mantle below deep lithospheric keels of old cold crust – evidence: inclusions of mantle material, density of diamonds.**

**Explosive volcanism brings diamonds to surface – kimberlite and lamproite pipes. These are windows into mantle conditions. Most are intracontinental volcanics. Primary diamond deposits occur in volcanic pipes, secondary deposits are placer. Links exist between kimberlite volcanism, plate tectonics and mantle plumes.**
