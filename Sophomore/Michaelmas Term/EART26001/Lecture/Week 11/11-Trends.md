## **Global geological trends for commodity deposits Key learning points week 11**

**All commodity deposits represent suitable combinations of favorable geological processes, but similar processes created similar deposits through time. Hence, . knowledge of these processes helps predict deposits**

**Resource deposits are geologically complex, and a detailed understanding of formation mechanisms is essential. In addition, the same commodity can be accumulated via different processes, and/or different processes may affect a single deposit over time.**

**A useful first-order classification takes into account fluid types and origins: igneous, igneous-hydrothermal, hydrothermal, sedimentary and surficial processes The general relation between rock-forming and ore-forming processes means that an understanding of overarching controls (atmosphere, biosphere, geosphere, hydrosphere) is indispensable in predicting and studying commodity deposits. Economic geology draws on and integrates concepts from diverse disciplines: (geo)physics, geochemistry, igneous and metamorphic geology, volcanology, sedimentology, paleontology, landscape evolution…...**
