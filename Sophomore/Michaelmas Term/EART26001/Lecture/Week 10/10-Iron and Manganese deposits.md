## **Iron and manganese deposits Summary week 10**

**Iron and manganese are mostly used in the steel and construction industry; further applications are in battery manufacturing and chemical industry.**

**They are relatively abundant in the crust, and enrichment factors are low compared to other metals.**

**Their redox chemistry (soluble at low redox state, insoluble at high redox state), and association with mafic magmas (for Fe) largely determine deposits.**

**Iron and manganese occur in a variety of deposit types:**

- **Magmatic and volcanic exhalations, metamorphic**

- **Hydrothermal**

- **Marine chemical sediments**

- **Laterites**

**Major iron and manganese formations in the Proterozoic are also key for scientific understanding of the early atmosphere.**
