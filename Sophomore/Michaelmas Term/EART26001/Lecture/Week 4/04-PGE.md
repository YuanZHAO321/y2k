## **PGE ore deposits and magmatic processes Summary**

**The Bushveld Complex is the largest layered intrusion in the world, and currently the main source of PGEs.**

**Magmatic processes of PGE ore genesis are strongly tied to processes of partial melting and crystallization in a magma chamber.**

**PGEs bond with iron and sulfide minerals, and the formation of sulfide melt droplets to scavenge PGEs from melt is a key factor in ore genesis. This can be achieved by several mechanisms:**

- **Density segregation of sulfides**

- **Magma re-injection and mixing**

- **Assimilation of and reaction with (S-rich) country rock**

- **Later alteration by aqueous phases**

**Layered intrusions are also important sources of Cr, Ni and V.**

## **Platinum group element deposits Summary week 4**

**Platinum group elements (PGE) are very rare in the crust and require high enrichment to ore grade.**

**They are precious metals, resistant to corrosion and heat, and are widely used in catalytic applications, electronics/electrical applications, medical applications and as jewelry. They are considered critical resources.**

**The main deposit type are layered igneous intrusions where orthomagmatic processes concentrate PGEs.**

**Bonding with iron and sulfide phases and segregation of these from the parent melt allows local strong enrichment of PGEs to ore grade.**

**Potential environmental impact of PGEs is little studied.**
