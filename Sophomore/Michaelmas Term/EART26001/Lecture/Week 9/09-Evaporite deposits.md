## **Evaporite deposits Summary week 9**

**Evaporites form when brines become oversaturated with ions, because of solar evaporation or diagenetic processes. The mineralogy is highly variable and reflects composition of the brine (and thus its source terrains).**

**The mineralogical variability translates into a range of applications. Key raw materials deriving from evaporation processes are potassium and lithium.**

**Formation and preservation require arid climate, negative water balance, and restriction from source of brine. Evaporite minerals typically form in playas, sabkhas, and salinas (saline lakes). Thick and cyclic evaporite successions require repeated replenishment of brine pool.**

**Evaporite mining occurs via precipitation in solar ponds (salars), or subsurface mining by conventional galleries or solution mining. It usually involves a large industrial footprint and water consumption.**
