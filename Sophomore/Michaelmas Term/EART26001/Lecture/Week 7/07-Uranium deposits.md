## **Uranium deposits Key learning points week 7**

**The dominant use of uranium is as fuel for nuclear power generation.**

**Uranium is concentrated by igneous processes associated with granitic rocks (lithophile behavior of U), and by redox processes in sedimentary and hydrothermal ore-forming environments. A variety of uranium deposit types exist as a consequence.**

**Natural reactors developed criticality and nuclear chain reaction in Gabon ~2.0 Ga ago. Key factors to reach criticality were:**

- **U enrichment factor**

- **relatively high**<sup>**235**</sup> **U/**<sup>**238**</sup> **U ratio**

- **minimum ore thickness**

- **high water/rock ratio**

**The study of natural reactors provides insights into the geological processes for uranium enrichment, physics of the chain reaction, and conditions for nuclear waste storage.**

**Uranium is radioactive and toxic, which has major environmental implications from mining through to radioactive waste disposal.**
