# **Energy and Resources Summary week 1**

# **Energy and Resources Summary week 1**

**Energy & resources include raw materials extracted from the Earth: metallic and non-metallic mineral deposits, as well as fossil fuels and ground water. Renewable resources will become more important in the future, but are not covered in this unit. Human society has developed using raw materials, and raw material extraction will remain important to:**

- **meet economic needs of developing nations**

- **meet requirements of energy transition to low-C economy**

**The right combination of geological processes and constraints forms a deposit. Its economical extraction is governed by economic, environmental, legal and societal constraints.**

**Exploration using mapping, geophysical and geochemical methods aims to reduce the inherent uncertainty in the subsurface.**

**Geoscientists know how to find and assess a deposit using parameters such as mineral grade, volumes in place. Geoscientists are no experts in the economic, environmental, legal and societal factors, but are aware of them. Key skills are:**

- **observational skills at a range of scales**

- **3D understanding of the subsurface**

- **numerical skills**
