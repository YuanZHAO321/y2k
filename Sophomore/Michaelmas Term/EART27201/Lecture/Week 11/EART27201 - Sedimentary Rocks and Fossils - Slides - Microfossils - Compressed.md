# **What is a microfossil?**

**Microfossil** : Any fossil that is best studied by means of a microscope. Material may include dissociated fragments of larger organisms, whole organisms of microscopic size, or embryonic forms of larger fossil organisms. Various groups, e.g. acritarchs, Foraminiferida, Ostracoda, and Conodontophora, are studied and used as stratigraphic markers. **Eukaryote** : An organism comprising cells that have a distinct nucleus enveloped by a double membrane, and other features including double-membraned mitochondria and 80S ribosomes in the fluid of the cytoplasm (i.e. all protoctists, fungi, plants, and animals).

Source: Allaby, M., 2013. A dictionary of geology and earth sciences. Oxford University Press. Image source: Nautral History Museum, London.

# **What is a diatom?**

**Frustule** : The cell wall of a diatom, which is impregnated with silica. The wall consists of two halves that overlap one another.

**Chrysophyte** : A large phylum (or group, i.e. the chrysophytes) of mostly freshwater photosynthetic protists, including the golden, or golden-brown, algae. Source: Armstrong, H. and Brasier, M., 2013. Microfossils. John Wiley & Sons.
