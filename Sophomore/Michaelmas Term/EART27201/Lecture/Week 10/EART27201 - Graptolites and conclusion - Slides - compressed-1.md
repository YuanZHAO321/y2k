# **What is a hemichordate?**

**Hemichordata** : A phylum of bilaterally symmetrical soft-bodied marine invertebrates that comprises two classes: the Enteropneusta (acorn worms), which are cylindrical burrowing animals; and the Pterobranchia, which are colonial forms with vase-shaped bodies. Left: Tassia, M.G., Cannon, J.T., Konikoff, C.E., Shenkar, N., Halanych, K.M. and Swalla, B.J., 2016. The global diversity of Hemichordata. PloS one, 11(10), p.e0162564. Right: Benton, M.J. and Harper, D.A., 2020. Introduction to paleobiology and the fossil record. John Wiley & Sons.

# **What is a graptolite?**

**Graptolite** : A member of a group of extinct marine colonial animals that were common in the Palaeozoic era. Graptolites are generally regarded as colonial hemichordates. **Pterobranch** : Class of minute, fixed, colonial, deep-sea organisms which secrete an external cuticular skeleton in which they are housed (e.g. Rhabdopleura). **Zooid** : In colonial invertebrates, an individual member of a colony.

**Sicula** : The skeleton of the initial zooid of a graptolite (Graptolithina) colony.

**Stipes** : A branch of a rhabdosome (graptolite colony). A stipe or stipes originate(s) from an initial conical cup (sicula). The number of stipes can range from one to as many as 64.

**Theca** : In graptolites (Graptolithina), one of the cup-like structures that occur along the stipe, each of which houses an individual member of the colony.

# **What do dendroid graptolites look like?**

**Dendroidea** : An order of graptolites that existed from the Middle Cambrian to the Lower Carboniferous. They are many-branched, with numerous small thecae, in some connected by struts. Benton, M.J. and Harper, D.A., 2020. Introduction to paleobiology and the fossil record. John Wiley & Sons.

Biofacies: Rock unit, or an association of rock units, characterized by the presence of a fossil assemblage that is restricted to that particular facies and that is typical of a specific environment.

Definition source: Allaby, M., 2020. A dictionary of geology and earth sciences (5th ed). Oxford University Press. Image source: Brenchley, P.J. and Harper, D., 1998. Palaeoecology: Ecosystems, environments and evolution. CRC Press.

Ichnofacies: A rock or sequence of rocks characterized by its lithology , inorganic sedimentary structures , and specific trace fossils. The traces represent the behaviour of fossil organisms under particular environmental conditions.

Definition source: Allaby, M., 2020. A dictionary of geology and earth sciences (5th ed). Oxford University Press. Image source: Source: Benton, M.J. and Harper, D.A., 2020. Introduction to paleobiology and the fossil record. John Wiley & Sons.
