# **(Limestones, Dolomites, Cherts ad Evaporites)**

“Ah! Vanitas vanitatum! Which of us is happy is this world?  Which of us has his desire? Or, having it, is satisfied? – Come, children, let us shut up the box and the puppets, for our play is played out.”

The class this week is devoted to the study of carbonate sediments and rocks with the emphasis on recognizing and interpreting grain types and fabrics as seen in hand specimen and thin section.  We will also be looking at other sedimentary rocks, particularly the evaporites.

By the end of today’s class you should:

- be able to recognize some of the more common grain types in limestones

- understand the Dunham classification of limestones and be able to apply it tentatively to hand specimens

- be able to interpret depositional mechanisms of common limestone types

- aware of the different types of carbonate mudstone and how to identify them

- able to estimate amounts and describe types of porosity in sedimentary rocks

- able to recognize dolomite in hand specimen  able to recognize chert and the common evaporate minerals in hand specimen and understand their origin
