## **Average Grain Size**

The most widely-used measure of average grain size is the 'mean diameter'. This is calculated by taking two or more percentiles and dividing by the number of percentiles used.  The more percentiles used, the more meaningful the results. For thus sample use

## **Skewness**

Skewness is a measure of the symmetry of the frequency curve.  In symmetrical distributions the value will be close to zero, a negative value indicates a tail in the coarser grades and a positive value a tail in the finer grades.  Values are always small, so a skewness of, say, ±0.2 is a significant departure from a normal distribution.
