## 二、核心概念

### 2.1 什么是全球气候模式（Global Climate Model, GCM）

**是什么。** 讲义 Slide 9 的定义方式是先把它放回课程脉络里：

> "We have covered the main concepts that feed into computer models to simulate and predict climate change. We now look at **how these models work and their results**."

也就是说，GCM 不是一个新的物理概念，而是**把前五周学过的所有物理机制（辐射定律、温室效应、辐射强迫、反馈、气溶胶、土地利用变化……）写成计算机代码，让它们在一个数值化的地球上同时跑起来**。讲义紧接着点出核心：

> "**The concept of radiative forcing is central to climate models.**"（辐射强迫的概念是气候模式的核心。）

讲师在转写里重复并强调了这一句：「The concept of **radiative forcing** is central to these climate models, and you should now be **very familiar** with that concept.」——这是明确的「Week 3 内容会在本周被复用」的信号。

**关键区分：模式预测的是「气候」，不是「天气」。** 这是本周最容易在考试里被追问的定义性区分，讲义 Slide 9 写得很死：

> "Climate models project **climate** (the average weather over a long period of time, e.g., **a 30-year period**), not **weather** (what an area experiences on an **hourly or daily** basis)."

| | 天气（weather） | 气候（climate） |
|---|---|---|
| 时间尺度 | 小时、天 | 多年 → 气候态（climatological），典型取 **30 年** |
| 空间尺度 | 局地、具体地点 | 区域到全球聚合 |
| GCM 能不能做 | **不是 GCM 的目标** | **是 GCM 的目标** |
| 典型问题 | 下周二会不会下雨 | 2050 年代英国南部夏季降水频次会变化多少 |

讲师的口头版本：「we're really thinking about these on a **multi-annual timescale up to climatological timescales of around 30 years** or so. So we're **not predicting weather and isolated events**, we're predicting **global aggregate effects** in large part.」

**边界条件 / 为什么这个区分重要。** 一切「模式没预测到今年这场暴雨，所以模式没用」的论证都建立在混淆天气与气候之上。GCM 从设计上就不承诺逐年、逐日的准确性——这一点在 Slide 26 的 hindcast 图上会以「集合平均抓不住年际起伏、但抓住了 20 年趋势」的形式再次出现（见 §2.15 与第三节）。

### 2.2 为什么科学家非用模式不可

讲义 Slide 9 给了两条理由：

> - "Models help us **understand how our actions can affect the future climate**."
> - "They allow modelers to look at **different scenarios of increased greenhouse gas emissions** and see how increases may affect us."

答案 deck（Q1 Answer）把它拆得更细，构成本周最标准的一段答题模板：

1. **重建过去（hindcasting）**——recreating historical conditions，理解已经发生的气候变化；
2. **预测未来（forecasting）**——在不同温室气体排放情景下预估未来气候趋势；
3. **分因子归因**——examine the impacts of **different factors**（GHG、气溶胶、毁林、土地利用变化……）on global temperature，看每一个因子各自贡献多少；
4. **物理基础 + 真实数据**——"These models are **based on physics** and incorporate **real-world data**"；
5. **观测反过来验证模式**——"Accurate measurements are essential for **verifying and improving** the reliability of global climate models."

讲师给了一个讲义上没有、但概括力最强的说法，值得直接背下来：

> "once we have an understanding that they're performing sufficiently well, we have **a tool to play with different emission scenarios, different interventions, different scenarios — both economic, behavioural, and environmental** — to see how each one of those decisions we make now might affect us into the future. **So they allow us to pull different levers in a way that we can't do with observations.**"

📎 背景补充：这句「pull different levers in a way that we can't do with observations」在方法论上其实是说 GCM 提供了**反事实实验（counterfactual experiment）**的能力。地球只有一个，我们无法「再跑一遍没有工业革命的 20 世纪」来做对照组；模式是唯一能提供对照组的工具。这一点是 §2.16「有/无强迫双跑」协议的哲学基础，也是把模式称为「归因证据」而不仅仅是「预测工具」的原因。

**边界条件。** 上面这句话的前提是括号里那半句——"**once we have an understanding that they're performing sufficiently well**"。模式的可信度不是先验给定的，必须先通过 hindcasting 检验（§2.15）。**先验证，后使用**，这个顺序在答题时不能颠倒。

### 2.3 GCM 计算什么：四大组件与输入变量（Slide 10）

讲义 Slide 10 给出的是最基础的 GCM 骨架：

> "Climate models calculate the **physical interactions between four components**:
> - **Atmosphere**（大气）
> - **Land**（陆地）
> - **Ocean**（海洋）
> - **Sea ice**（海冰）"

计算所依据的输入量（inputs）：

> "**Air temperature**（气温）, **Pressure**（气压）, **Density**（密度）, **Water vapor content**（水汽含量）, **Wind magnitude**（风速大小）**and many more…**"

⚠️ **注意一个内部落差**：Slide 10 说的是「四大组件」（大气/陆地/海洋/海冰），而**答案 deck 的 Q4 Answer 说的是「五大组件」（Atmosphere / Cryosphere / Chemistry / Ocean / Biosphere）**。这两个列表不一样，而且不是简单笔误——前者是**经典 GCM**的四分法（Slide 10 讲的是模式最基础的物理内核），后者是**现代耦合地球系统模式**的五分法（Q4 讲的是 Slide 14 之后的完整架构）。答题时要看题目问的是哪一层；如果题干只说 "a Global Climate Model"，用 **Q4 的五组件版本**更安全，因为那是答案 deck 的官方答案。此项已登记勘误表。

讲师在转写里描述了 Slide 10 的配图（一张 GCM 过程示意图），描述本身就是一份「模式里有什么」的清单：

> "You can see **different forms of precipitation, both over land and ocean**. You can see **different transfer of heat**. You can see the **incoming shortwave solar radiation**, the **outgoing longwave radiation**, and the **greenhouse effects** associated with that. We have **land ice or sea ice**. Changes, which we know from week four to affect **albedo**."

并且补了一句量级感很强的话：

> "in these climate models, essentially there are **hundreds of thousands of lines of code** which simulate individual processes that are then looked at together."

**怎么用。** 考试里若问「What are the key components / inputs of a GCM」，标准答法是分两层：**组件层**（大气—陆地—海洋—海冰，或扩展到冰冻圈—生物圈—化学）＋ **变量层**（温度、气压、密度、水汽、风速……），再加一句「这些组件之间通过能量、水汽和动量交换耦合」。

### 2.4 三维网格：GCM 的空间骨架（Slide 11）

**是什么。** 讲义 Slide 11：

> - "The **size and complexity of the Earth** makes this challenging."
> - "**Different places have different responses to energy** (e.g., land warms up and cools down faster than oceans)."
> - "We divide Earth into a **3D grid** across the surface, up into the atmosphere, and down into the ocean."
> - "**Each cube is analysed individually over time** while receiving information (**inputs**) from and passing information (**outputs**) to **neighbouring cubes**."
> - "The **size of the cubes varies among models**."
> - "A **higher resolution** model has **more, smaller cubes** and provides a **more accurate** picture of how changes to the climate impact an area."

**为什么要切网格。** 因为大气和海洋的控制方程是**偏微分方程**，没有解析解，只能离散化后数值求解。地球太大太不均匀，不可能用一组方程描述整体；把它切成足够小的立方体，在每个立方体内部把物理量当作近似均匀，就可以逐格求解、再让格子之间交换信息。

**怎么运作（讲师的口头版本，比讲义清楚）：**

> "essentially, the Earth is **split into a grid**, both across the surface and **up into the atmosphere and down into the ocean**. And **each one of these physical processes are calculated for each one of these 3D grids** across the surface of the Earth. So these hundreds of thousands of lines of codes **interact in each grid**. And then **the interactions between the grids** are then assessed. So you can see how **computationally this becomes very expensive**."

**核心权衡（trade-off）。** 讲师把这一点说了两遍，是本周的重点考点：

> "Typically, the **smaller the size of the cube, the more accurate** the model is. But computationally, that becomes **much more expensive and time consuming**. So there's a **trade-off between the number of simulations that you can run** and **the resolution and perhaps accuracy** of these models."

注意这个权衡的两端不是「精度 vs 速度」，而是**「单次模拟的精度」vs「你能跑几次模拟」**。后者直接决定你能不能做集合（ensemble）、能不能测试多个情景——这是讲义没点明、但对理解 §2.13 RCP 与 §3 集合平均非常关键的一层。

**时间分辨率（讲义完全没有，纯口头补充）：**

> "We also need to think about the **time resolution** as well. So it's **not just the size of the cubes**, it's **how often these cubes are analysed in time**, but also has an effect."

📎 背景补充：数值模式里空间步长与时间步长不能独立随便取——时间步长必须小于某个由网格尺度和最大传播速度决定的上限，否则数值解会发散。这就是**CFL 条件（Courant–Friedrichs–Lewy condition）**。它的直接后果是：**把水平网格加密一倍，计算量不是变成 2 倍，而是大致变成 2³（三维）×2（时间步）≈ 16 倍**。这正是「分辨率提升为什么这么贵」的定量原因。讲义与转写都没有给出这个机制，此处为补充。

**边界条件。** 网格化本身就是误差的第一来源（Slide 25 把 "Grid size" 列为误差来源之首）。任何尺度小于一个格子的过程（云、湍流、对流、地形细节）都**无法被显式解析（resolved）**，只能靠**参数化（parameterisation）**近似——见 §2.8 与 §2.14。

### 2.5 GCM 发展史（一）：1990s–2000s，各组件各跑各的（Slide 12）

**讲义原文（Slide 12 / 13 共用的前半段）：**

> - "Earlier models calculated physical processes for the **atmosphere, land, ocean, and sea ice components separately**."
> - "Initially GCMs treated **atmosphere and ocean models as separate entities**."
> - "**Atmosphere models** focused on simulating **atmospheric dynamics**, while **ocean models** represented **oceanic circulation and thermodynamics**."
> - "These separate models were **run independently** and their **outputs were not directly interconnected**."
> - Slide 12 末尾抛出问题：「**Why is this a problem?**」

**讲师的口头补充（讲义没有的信息）：**

> "The **first real true global climate models were introduced in the early 1990s** and **incorporated into the first IPCC report**. These models just looked at the **atmosphere and the ocean in isolation**. And **by the atmosphere, I mean energy transfer in the atmosphere rather than atmospheric composition**. There was an ocean model that again looked at **thermodynamics** in that area."

这句划线部分很重要：**早期「大气模式」里的「大气」指的是能量传输，不包括大气化学成分**。所以早期模式根本没有气溶胶、没有臭氧化学、没有气体寿命的概念——这解释了为什么后来必须单独加一个 Chemistry Model（§2.7）。

**为什么不耦合是个问题（讲师给的具体例子，讲义上只有抽象表述）：**

> "we know that from the course that these are **interacting with one another very readily**. And there can be **positive and negative feedback loops** associated with these sorts of questions. An example of that might be as the temperature of the Earth gets slightly warmer — that can be due to a **Milankovitch cycle**, for example. **We know that warmer oceans contain less CO₂ in solution. So as the planet gets warmer, there's more outgassing of CO₂.** Hopefully now you appreciate that that will lead to a **stronger greenhouse effect** and that will continue to **exacerbate** that temperature increase. So that's an example of a **positive feedback loop where the atmosphere and the ocean are interacting**."

把这个例子写成链条：

```
轨道强迫（Milankovitch）→ 温度略升 → 海水变暖
        → CO₂ 在海水中溶解度下降 → 海洋向大气排放 CO₂（outgassing）
        → 大气 CO₂ 上升 → 温室效应增强 → 温度进一步上升 ↺（正反馈）
```

**这个链条的每一环都跨越大气—海洋边界。** 如果大气模式和海洋模式各跑各的、输出不互通，这条反馈在模式里**根本不存在**——这就是 Slide 12 那句 "Why is this a problem?" 的标准答案。

⚠️ 转写里 "Milankovitch cycle" 被自动语音识别成了 "melancholic cycle"，属转写讹误，已登记勘误表。

### 2.6 GCM 发展史（二）：大气—海洋耦合（Slide 13）

**讲义原文（Slide 13 新增的后半段）：**

> - "**Coupling** atmosphere and ocean models allowed for the **interaction to be simulated**."
> - "These now allow **feedback loops** between the atmosphere and ocean, such as the **influence of ocean currents on atmospheric circulation patterns and vice versa**."
> - "Thus capturing **complex interactions and feedback mechanisms**."
> - "Can capture phenomena such as **El Niño–Southern Oscillation (ENSO)** which are **crucial for understanding global climate variability**."
> - "GCMs can now produce **more realistic climate projections on both regional and global scales**."

**讲师口头补充：**

> "one of the first things that was done was to **couple these models together**. And that therefore allows these sorts of interactions to be simulated in a way that **can't be done when these independent models are running in isolation**. As a result of this, we can then start to think about simulating important **natural variability** in things like the **La Niña and El Niño cycles, which we know can drive temperature changes of a few tenths of a degree**."

「a few tenths of a degree（几十分之一度，即 0.1–0.3 °C 量级）」这个数量级是转写独有的，讲义没写。**它是理解「为什么 ENSO 必须被模拟出来」的关键**：ENSO 造成的年际温度摆动，量级上和几十年的人为增温趋势的年际起伏可比。如果模式抓不住 ENSO，模式输出的年际变率就会失真，就无法把「自然波动」和「人为趋势」干净地分开（呼应 §2.16 归因）。

**边界条件。** 耦合让模式**能够**产生 ENSO 这类内部变率，但**不保证模式产生的 ENSO 事件与真实世界的 ENSO 事件在时间上对齐**。📎 背景补充：ENSO 是气候系统的**内部变率（internal variability）**，其具体发生时刻依赖初始条件，属于混沌行为；耦合模式追求的是「ENSO 的统计特征（振幅、周期、空间结构）正确」，而不是「2027 年会不会有 El Niño」。这也正是 Slide 26 里集合平均「抓趋势不抓年际」的物理原因之一。此段为补充说明，材料未展开。

### 2.7 GCM 发展史（三）：2010s+，耦合地球系统模式（Slide 14、15）

进入 2010 年代，模式在大气—海洋之外又接入了三个新模块。讲义 Slide 14 逐个说明：

**① 生物圈模式整合（Biosphere Model Integration）**
> - "**Terrestrial vegetation, ecosystems, and biogeochemical cycles.**"
> - "Simulates processes like **photosynthesis, respiration, and carbon cycling**."
> - "Enables assessment of **land use changes, deforestation, and vegetation**."

讲师补充：「it also enables assessment of things like **land use change**, which **Simon taught you about in detail in week four**.」——直接把本周和 Week 4（Anthropogenic impacts，Simon 主讲）挂钩。

**② 化学模式（Chemistry Model）**
> - "Simulate interactions of **atmospheric gases and aerosols**, including **pollutants and greenhouse gases**."
> - "Predicts atmospheric composition changes like **ozone depletion, aerosol formation, and air quality**."
> - "Enhances understanding of atmospheric processes, their climate impacts, and **the effectiveness of mitigation strategies targeting pollutant emissions**."

讲师口头补充了化学模式为什么最要紧（讲义没有这一层）：

> "we know that there's **huge uncertainties** in things like **aerosol production** and the interaction of the aerosols with incoming solar radiation. Both through the **direct effect**, which is directly interacting with incoming solar radiation. Or the **cloud albedo effect**, which is the impact of aerosols on **cloud formation, lifetime and precipitation rates**. So these chemistry models allow us to track and understand production of things like **aerosols, tropospheric ozone**. And it helps us understand **how long these greenhouse gases will live in the atmosphere**."

这里出现了两个必须分清的术语：

| 术语 | 机制 | 影响什么 |
|---|---|---|
| **气溶胶直接效应（direct effect）** | 气溶胶粒子自身散射／吸收入射太阳辐射 | 直接改变行星反照率与地表短波辐射 |
| **云反照率效应（cloud albedo effect，即间接效应）** | 气溶胶作为**云凝结核**，改变云滴数浓度与大小 → 改变云的形成、**寿命**与降水率 | 通过云间接改变反照率，还改变降水 |

以及一句考点句：化学模式的作用之一是搞清楚**温室气体在大气中能活多久（lifetime）**——这直接对应 Slide 36 的例题「Explain how the **lifetime** of atmospheric components (e.g., aerosols or greenhouse gases) influences climate model predictions」。

**③ 冰冻圈模式引入（Cryosphere Model Incorporation）**
> - "Cryosphere models represent **ice sheets, glaciers, snow cover, and permafrost dynamics** within GCMs."
> - "Captures feedback mechanisms like **ice-albedo, sea level, and climate**."
> - "Improves prediction of **ice mass balance, sea ice extent**."

**④ 耦合器（Coupler）——架构上的关键零件**

Slide 14/15 的架构图把六个方框摆在一起：**Atmospheric Model / Chemistry Model / Cryosphere Model / Biosphere Model / Ocean Model**，中间是一个 **Coupler（耦合器）**。

讲师解释了 coupler 的真正意义，这是**本周最有价值的口头增量之一**，讲义完全没写：

> "In 2010, this sort of **coupling approach** was developed. And what this allows you to do is **pick your favourite atmospheric model to couple to your favourite biosphere model, to couple to your favourite ocean model** and so on. **There is not a single atmospheric chemistry model. There are many different types.** But this coupling approach allows you to **integrate different approaches to answer different, unique questions**. So this was a **huge step forward** in terms of the **operability** of these sorts of models."

换句话说，coupler 带来的不只是「组件之间能对话」，而是**模块化（modularity）**：各研究组可以自由组合不同来源的组件模块，去回答各自的问题。这解释了为什么全世界会有几十个不同的 GCM（呼应 §2.13：正因为模式这么多样，才需要 RCP 统一输入）。

**Slide 15 给出的耦合收益（benefits of coupling）：**
> - "**Comprehensive representation** of Earth's interconnected systems."
> - "Facilitates a **deeper understanding** of climate change impacts."
> - "**Informs decision-making** for adaptation and mitigation efforts."
> - "Enables **more accurate climate projections**, aiding in **policy formulation and resource allocation**."

讲师收尾时补了一条讲义没有的、机制层面的理由：

> "You can now pick up **different feedback loops, which may induce nonlinear responses**. And ultimately this leads to a much more accurate representation of the climate system."

**「非线性响应（nonlinear responses）」是关键词**：耦合不是把各组件的效应简单相加，而是让它们互相放大或抵消。这也解释了为什么 AI/ML 在 §2.18 会以「擅长处理非线性关系」为卖点。

**边界条件。** 复杂度是有代价的。讲义 Slide 28 明说 "**As complexity is increased so is computing power**"，讲师说「Simulation can take **weeks on supercomputers**」。所以耦合再多组件也不是免费的午餐——每加一个模块，你能跑的情景数就变少。

> "the **lack of resolution**, in this case over the UK, makes it very difficult to predict **small scale changes that might affect individuals**. An example of that might be a **UK farmer** who is trying to understand what the climate might look like into the future. **How many rainy days might he expect? 这个比喻讲师后面又用了两次，用来说明**「模式输出能不能被使用」是分辨率之外的另一个问题**：

This is just a zoomed in version of this high warming scenario. **A farmer couldn't make good use of outputs like this**, so the resolution on which these models are run is very important. **But also it's not just what models and how models are operated, it's how those outputs are communicated becomes very important as well.** So something like this is **almost useless for someone trying to make a decision** based on upcoming change."

**📌 讲师隐含的重点：模式的价值 = 分辨率 × 沟通方式。** 一张全球平均升温图对政策制定者有用，对农民毫无用处；把同样的模式输出重新处理成「夏季降水日数变化百分比地图」，对农民才有用。这一层「science communication」的强调是讲义完全没有的。

**Slide 19：英国「局地」气候预估——夏季降水（UK 'local' climate projections: summer rainfall）**

讲义只给了一句结论：

> "**Summer rainfall is less frequent but more intense.**"（夏季降水**频次下降但强度上升**。）

讲师读图时给出了讲义没有的区域细节：

> "what you can see here is the **number of rainy days on the top figure** and the **intensity of those rainy days in the bottom**. And you can start to see **disparities across the UK** in terms of where you might see larger frequency changes. So **in the south you might expect the frequency to change by ... 50 to 60 percent**. And that's particularly going to affect **southern parts of the UK**. But in terms of the **intensity of rainfall** that might affect the **eastern side of the UK** more prominently."

整理成可背的结论：

| 图（Slide 19） | 变量 | 空间格局 | 数值 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 上图 | 夏季**降水日数（number of rainy days）**变化 | **英国南部**变化最大 | 频次变化约 **50–60%**（减少） |
| 下图 | 降水**强度（intensity）**变化 | **英国东部**更显著 | 讲师未给具体百分比 |
| 合起来的结论 | — | — | **"less frequent but more intense"** |

📎 背景补充：「频次降低但强度增大」在物理上并不矛盾。大气持水能力随温度按 **Clausius–Clapeyron 关系**约每升温 1 °C 增加约 7%，所以一旦降水发生，可降水量更大；但全球尺度降水总量受能量收支限制、增幅远小于 7%/K，于是「总量增幅有限 + 单次事件更强」只能靠「事件更少」来平衡。这个机制讲义与转写都没讲，此处为补充，考试若要求解释「为什么会 less frequent but more intense」可以用它，但务必自己确认是否属于本课要求范围。

### 2.10 网格盒子里发生什么：大气成分的箱模型（Slide 21）

这是本周唯一一张**定量**幻灯片，也是唯一涉及公式的地方。它回答的问题是：**在一个网格格子内部，某个化学物种的浓度是怎么算出来的？**

**讲义 Slide 21 的元素（原文标签）：**
- 中间是一个 **Atmospheric "box"（大气箱）**，里面装着物种 **X**
- 说明文字：「**spatial distribution of X within box is not resolved**」（箱内 X 的空间分布不被解析）
- 「**box can be global atmosphere or some subdomain**」（箱子可以是整个全球大气，也可以是某个子区域）
- 进出项：**Inflow / Fin**（流入）、**Outflow / Fout**（流出）
- 源：**Emission, E**（排放）、**Chemical production, P**（化学生成）
- 汇：**Deposition, D**（沉降）、**Chemical loss, L**（化学损耗）
- 「**Let m [kg] be the mass of X in the box**」（设 m 为箱内 X 的质量，单位 kg）

**讲师的口头讲解（把每一项都举了例子，讲义只有标签）：**

> "In terms of what these grid boxes look like in terms of **atmospheric composition** we can start to look at this **box modelling approach** for different molecules or different constituents of the atmosphere. An example of this is **molecule X**. This could either be a **greenhouse gas** or be an **aerosol particle** of different kinds. You can have **sources and sinks** of this molecule X and that will dictate **the concentration of X in that grid** and **how then it might move between other grids**."

逐项的口头例子：

| 项 | 符号 | 讲师的解释与例子 |
|---|---|---|
| **直接排放** | **E** | "you can either have **direct emission** and those emissions can be widespread. You should now have a good understanding of the emission sources of **CO₂, methane and N₂O** which are the key greenhouse gases." |
| **沉降** | **D** | "you can also have **deposition to the surface** that can either be through **wet deposition** i.e. incorporated into precipitation **or gravitational settling**." |
| **化学生成** | **P** | "**Not all atmospheric constituents are directly emitted** into the atmosphere. An example of that ... is **ozone**. **Ozone in the troposphere is not directly emitted but it is made through a series of photochemical reactions**." |
| **化学损耗** | **L** | "you can also have **chemical losses** where different chemicals interact with one another to lead to the loss of molecule X. An example ... might be something like **methane** which readily reacts with different **oxygen[-containing radicals]** in the atmosphere and acts as an important **removal mechanism of methane**." |
| **流入 / 流出** | **F_in / F_out** | "You can also have **interaction with other boxes** that lead to **increases** in concentration or you can have **outflow** which leads to **decreases** in concentration." |

**收束公式（讲师的口头表述）：**

> "So if you want to then calculate **the concentration of X in a particular box** we can start to look at the **sum of the sources minus the sum of the sinks**. And as I say **not all molecules will have chemical production or direct emission or direct chemical losses or deposition. But in many cases it will be the sum of all of them put together.**"

完整写法见第三节 §3.1。

**为什么这张图重要（连接作用）。** 它把 §2.4 的「三维网格」和 §2.7 的「化学模式」缝在一起：**网格是空间骨架，箱模型是每个格子内部的化学/成分收支方程。**GCM 就是「几十万个箱子 × 每个箱子里若干物种的收支方程 × 箱子之间的输运」。

**边界条件（讲义明写的关键限制）：** 「**spatial distribution of X within box is not resolved**」——**箱内被当作充分混合、没有内部结构**。这就是分辨率限制在化学层面的具体表现：格子越大，「箱内均匀」这个假设越离谱。

**讲师的两处课外指路：**
- 「we touch on and cover [tropospheric ozone photochemistry] in a **huge amount of detail in my atmospheric composition lecture series in year three**」——对流层臭氧化学是**大三大气成分课**的内容，本课只需知道「臭氧不是直接排放的，是光化学生成的」。
- 「these sorts of models have been used **extensively by the IPCC since the 1990s** when the first real global climate model came into effect.」

⚠️ 转写把甲烷的氧化剂说成 "different oxygens"，真实机制是与**羟基自由基（OH radical）**反应；转写不精确，已登记勘误表。

### 2.12 现代耦合 GCM 表现如何：四条「好 / 差」判据（Slide 23）

这是**本周最可能出简答题的一张幻灯片**，四条必须背下来：

> **Modern Coupled GCMs**
> - ✅ "Perform **well at continental scales**"（在**大陆尺度**表现好）
> - ✅ "Perform **well at multiannual → climatological scales (30 year)**"（在**多年到气候态（30 年）尺度**表现好）
> - ❌ "Perform **less well at short time scales**"（在**短时间尺度**表现差）
> - ❌ "Perform **less well at regional scales**"（在**区域尺度**表现差）

讲师的口头版本一字不差地对应：

> "these global climate models now **perform very well at continental scales** and perform very well at **multi-annual climatological time scales**. These are **not yet perfect** and they perform much **less well at short time scales** and much **less well over smaller areas**."

**记忆结构：GCM 在「大 + 长」上好，在「小 + 短」上差。**

**为什么会这样（把前面的内容串起来的因果解释）：**

| 「小」为什么差 | 「短」为什么差 |
|---|---|
| 网格尺度限制：小于格子的过程只能参数化，不能解析（§2.4） | 内部变率（ENSO 等）具混沌性，模式不追求时间对齐（§2.6） |
| 高空/深海观测稀疏，垂直层缺约束（§2.9） | 集合平均会把各成员的年际起伏抹平（§3.4、§2.15） |
| 区域响应本身就是模式间分歧最大的地方（Slide 25、Slide 28） | 模式的设计目标就是气候而非天气（§2.1） |

**边界条件与现实含义。** 这四条直接决定了模式输出的**可用边界**——它同时是「农民案例」的理论说明：农民要的是「区域 + 具体年份」的信息，恰好落在模式最弱的那个象限里，所以才需要不断提高分辨率、需要区域降尺度、需要更好的沟通方式。

### 2.13 代表性浓度路径（Representative Concentration Pathways, RCPs）（Slide 24）

**是什么。** 讲义 Slide 24（这段基本照抄 IPCC AR5 综合报告措辞）：

> "Anthropogenic GHG emissions are mainly driven by **population size, economic activity, lifestyle, energy use, land use patterns, technology and climate policy**."
>
> "The **Representative Concentration Pathways (RCPs)**, which are used for making projections based on these factors, describe **four different 21st century pathways** of **GHG emissions and atmospheric concentrations, air pollutant emissions and land use**."
>
> "The RCPs include a **stringent mitigation scenario (RCP2.6)**, **two intermediate scenarios (RCP4.5 and RCP6.0)** and one scenario with **very high GHG emissions (RCP8.5)**. Scenarios **without additional efforts to constrain emissions ('baseline scenarios')** lead to pathways ranging **between RCP6.0 and RCP8.5**."
>
> "**RCP2.6** is representative of a scenario that aims to keep global warming **likely below 2 °C above pre-industrial temperatures**."

四条路径整理：

| RCP | 性质 | 讲义/讲师描述 |
|---|---|---|
| **RCP2.6** | **严格减排（stringent mitigation）** | 目标是把全球升温**很可能（likely）控制在 2 °C 以内**；讲师说这意味着「**severe mitigations** in terms of reductions of GHG emissions, **very quickly, very abruptly**, and probably with the use of **negative emission technologies** as well」 |
| **RCP4.5** | 中间情景（intermediate） | 有一定减排 |
| **RCP6.0** | 中间情景（intermediate） | — |
| **RCP8.5** | **极高排放（very high GHG emissions）** | 讲师原话：「**cracking on as normal**」（照常干下去） |
| — | **基线情景（baseline scenarios）** | **无额外减排努力**的情景落在 **RCP6.0 与 RCP8.5 之间** |

📎 背景补充：RCP 后面的数字（2.6 / 4.5 / 6.0 / 8.5）是**该路径在 2100 年达到的近似总辐射强迫值，单位 W m⁻²**（相对于工业化前）。讲义和转写都**没有**说明这个数字的来源，但知道这一点才能理解为什么 RCP 序号越大升温越多——它直接接的是 Week 3 的辐射强迫概念。此为补充，请自行确认是否需要在考试中给出。

**为什么必须有 RCP —— 讲师的核心论证（讲义完全没有这一层，务必掌握）：**

> "Like I say **there is no one perfect global climate model**. So the **IPCC brings together many climate models** together. But what this means is you're **comparing a global climate model that might be run in the US to a global climate model that is run in the UK**. **How do you know that the inputs and the emissions are the same between the models? If you didn't know that you'd be comparing apples with oranges.** To ensure that you can have good comparability between these global climate models it's very important to know that **the inputs are the same**. And that is done through these **universally accepted representative concentration pathways**."

他在结尾又重复了一遍：

> "**With these representative concentration pathways you are ensuring that the inputs are the same. Therefore you are trying to predict the effects of the same scenarios.** Without that ... you'd be **comparing apples with oranges** and it wouldn't be particularly useful."

**📌 一句话答题版：RCP 的作用是「把情景输入标准化」，让世界各地、互不相识的团队所跑的几十个不同 GCM，其结果可以被合法地放在一起比较和集合平均。** 这既是 Slide 36 例题「Explain the role of RCPs in projecting future climate change」的最佳切入点，也是本周最容易被忽略的高分点——多数人只会答「RCP 是四种排放情景」，而不会答「它保证了跨模式可比性」。

**边界条件：预测到什么时候为止？** 讲师给了具体年份和理由（讲义没有）：

> "Much like the measurements, **the further you go back in time, the inaccuracies increase. It's the same with global climate models.** **Typically we predict out to 2100**, because **after that the discrepancies between the models becomes much larger**, and the **uncertainties on the estimates also become larger**. But for the purposes of today ... **we have included data up to 2300**, which is **about as far out as some of these global climate models will consider predicting**."

📌 **必记：常规预估终点 2100；极限外推 2300；2100 之后模式间分歧急剧扩大。**

⚠️ **一个必须注意的术语混用：** 讲师在描述这张图时说的是 **SSP** 而不是 RCP：「**SSP 2-4.5** is there's mitigations, and **SSP 1-2.6** are severe mitigations」，但他在同一段里把它们统称为 "representative concentration pathways"。**SSP（Shared Socioeconomic Pathways，共享社会经济路径）与 RCP 是两代不同的情景框架**，讲义 Slide 24 通篇只用 RCP。这属于口头混用，已登记勘误表。答题时**以讲义的 RCP 术语为准**，除非题目明确问 SSP。

📎 背景补充：SSP 是 IPCC AR6（CMIP6）采用的新一代情景框架，写法形如 **SSPx-y**，其中 x 是社会经济发展路径编号（1–5），y 是 2100 年的辐射强迫水平（与 RCP 数字同义）。所以 "SSP1-2.6" 大致对应 "RCP2.6" 的强迫水平但配上了「可持续发展」的社会经济假设。本课讲义未涉及此框架，此处仅为解释讲师口误的来源。

### 2.15 后报 / 回报（Hindcasting）：怎么知道模式是对的（Slide 25、26）

**是什么。** 讲义 Slide 25 底部：

> "Climate models are evaluated for accuracy by '**hindcasting**,' or **replicating the climate of the past 100 years**."
>
> "Because models have been **run since the 1990s**, scientists can now **see how accurate earlier models were at projecting future changes**."

讲师的定义更直白：

> "how do we know whether these models are working correctly? We are working on this principle of **hindcasting**. Which is the principle of **predicting what has happened in the past**. **If the observations match very well with those predictions you've got a good idea that we might be able to extrapolate out to later years into the future.**"

**注意 hindcasting 有两种用法，讲义把两种都写了，别混淆：**

| 用法 | 做什么 | 检验什么 |
|---|---|---|
| **① 后报（hindcast）** | 用**今天的模式**去重现**过去 100 年**的气候 | 模式的物理是否能重现已知的历史 |
| **② 回顾旧模式的预测（retrospective evaluation）** | 看**1990 年代跑的旧模式**当年对未来的预测，与后来真实发生的对比 | 模式**当年**的预测能力是否经得起时间检验 |

第 ② 种更有说服力，因为当年的建模者**不可能知道**后来的观测结果——这是一个真正的「盲测」。

**Slide 26：How did we do in 2004?**

> "Observed changes in **temperature and precipitation** have **generally been consistent** with the changes projected by earlier models."
>
> "The **accurate projections of future climate and hindcasting of past climate makes us confident** that models can **reliably project changes in the climate**."

**讲师对这张图的完整讲解（转写独有，图的解读全在这里）：**

> "An example of that was **a piece of work that was completed in early 2020**. Which looked at **global climate models that were run in 2004**. And **how well they predicted what has happened from 2004 to today**. So to do that they used this **hindcast approach** ... They compared that with the observations and said **look we're doing a pretty good job**. What might happen through to **2022**. And you can see the period here where we **moved from hindcast to forecast**. And we can see that the **ensemble mean of the global climate models captures rather nicely the multi-year variation and trends over the last 20 years**. This gives us a ... **confident standpoint** where we can think that these global climate models are doing a relatively good job."

**然后是这张图最重要的一句「反向读法」：**

> "The other thing that you should be able to pull away from this plot is that **the mean doesn't capture the variability on a yearly timescale very well. But it does capture the overall trends very well over the 20 years.** This just backs up the point of the previous slide that **we predict and can use these models on a climatological timescale but less well on predicting what is happening from year to year.**"

📌 **这是本周最典型的「一张图两个结论」考点**：
- **结论 A（正面）**：集合平均很好地重现了 20 年的**多年变化与趋势** → 我们对模式有信心；
- **结论 B（限制）**：集合平均**抓不住逐年的起伏** → 印证「模式适用于气候尺度，不适用于年际/天气尺度」。

**边界条件（hindcasting 的逻辑漏洞，材料未明说但值得想清楚）：** 📎 背景补充：hindcast 表现好并不必然保证未来预测好，因为模式可能是通过**调参（tuning）**去拟合历史的，即「用已知答案反推参数」；而且未来可能出现历史上从未出现过的状态（如超出记录的温度区间），模式在该区间未被检验过。**这正是答案 deck Q9 中批评 AI 模式「depend strongly on the quality and quantity of training data, which may limit their reliability when projecting conditions that have not previously occurred」的同一个逻辑，只不过 AI 模式的这一弱点更严重。**此段推论为补充。

**答案 deck 给的「如何检验一个模式」完整清单（讨论题第二问的官方答案，Slide 5）——这是本周最完整的一份答题模板：**

> - "**Run the model to simulate past climate conditions rather than the future.**"
> - "**Compare the model output with real observations** such as temperature, rainfall, and atmospheric circulation records."
> - "Check whether the model reproduces **long-term trends, seasonal cycles, and natural variability**."
> - "**Test the model with and without specific forcing factors** (e.g. greenhouse gases or volcanic aerosols) to see whether it captures observed changes."
> - "Compare results with **multiple independent datasets** (satellite observations, weather stations, ocean measurements)."
> - "Evaluate how well the model reproduces **large-scale climate patterns such as ENSO**."
> - "Test the model across **different regions and timescales** to assess **where it performs well or poorly**."
> - "**Compare results from multiple climate models** to see whether they produce consistent outcomes."
> - "This process is called **hindcasting**, and **good agreement with observations increases confidence** that the model can project future climate change."

### 2.16 人为影响的模拟协议：「有强迫 / 无强迫」双跑（Slide 27）

**这是本周把「模式」变成「归因证据」的那一步，四步协议必须背。** 讲义 Slide 27 原文：

> "How would such a model simulation be carried out? The following protocol is typical.
> 1. A GCM model is **run for many decades with no external forcing**.
> 2. The **statistics of the run** (**mean climate, annual cycles and variability on a range of time scales**) are then **compared with observation** to **assess performance**.
> 3. The model is then **re-run with changes in forcing** (such as **greenhouse radiative forcing**).
> 4. **The difference in the two climates provides an estimate of the consequent climate change arising from that forcing factor.**"

把它写成流程：

```
① 无外强迫控制试验（control run，只有自然内部变率）
        ↓
② 与观测比对统计特征（平均气候、年循环、各尺度变率）→ 确认模式基本功过关
        ↓
③ 加入强迫（如温室气体辐射强迫）重跑
        ↓
④ 两次气候之差 = 该强迫因子造成的气候变化估计
```

**讲师的口头版本，把它明确指向「归因」：**

> "how might we think about **separating out natural variability from anthropogenic climate variability**. We can start to think about **running models with and without climate forcing**. And this again gives us some **confidence that it is in fact us that is driving the climate change we're seeing, not natural variability**. And again, that's only because **global climate models predict very nicely what is happening up to today but only when anthropogenic forcings are included**."

📌 **最后半句是整个归因论证的落点：「只有当把人为强迫放进去，模式才能复现到今天为止的观测」。**换句话说：
- 只跑自然强迫（太阳、火山）→ 模式给出的 20 世纪后期温度曲线**是平的或略降**，对不上观测；
- 加上人为强迫 → 曲线与观测**吻合**。

这就是 Slide 5 中 "Is it us?" 的模式端答案，也是 Slide 36 例题「Describe **how global climate models can be used to attribute observed warming to human activities**」的标准解法。

**边界条件。** 第 ② 步不可省——**必须先证明模式在无强迫情况下能重现观测到的气候统计特征，第 ④ 步的差值才有意义**。如果模式连基本气候态都跑不对，两次跑的差值也不可信。这一点讲义写在协议里，但很多人答题时会跳过，属于失分点。

### 2.17 仍待解决的问题（Slide 28）

**讲义 Slide 28 原文：**

> "Short term changes are **not reproduced** but the results look **encouraging**. However, problems remain –
> - **Is the temperature record reliable?**（温度记录本身可靠吗？）
> - **Cause and effect not finally established**（因果关系尚未最终确立）
> - **Large uncertainties in radiative forcing and the effect of aerosols**（辐射强迫与气溶胶效应的不确定性大）
> - **Discrepancies remain between predicted and observed regional pattern of change**（预测与观测的区域变化格局仍有差异）
> - **Not all factors are represented in GCMs – e.g. nitrogen cycle**（并非所有因子都进了模式，例如**氮循环**）
> - **As complexity is increased so is computing power**（复杂度上升 → 算力需求上升）"

**讲师的口头对应：**

> "There's some remaining factors to consider which is **how representative things like the temperature record are across the surface of the earth**. There are still **large uncertainties in aerosol effects both from the direct and indirect effects** of aerosols. And there are still relatively large **discrepancies in regional predictions versus observed regional patterns of change**. And **not all biogeochemical cycles are completely incorporated** into global climate models. Things like the **nitrogen cycle** might have effects on **atmospheric chemistry but also the biosphere**. Ultimately a large part of this is the **reliance on very heavy computer power** to come up with these **parameterisations**. **Simulation can take weeks on supercomputers.** So you can start to think how this might **limit the number of simulations that any one person could run**."

📌 两条讲义没有的口头细节：
1. **氮循环缺席的具体后果**：影响**大气化学**和**生物圈**两方面（讲义只列了名字）。
2. **「一次模拟可能要在超级计算机上跑数周」** —— 这个具体量级把「计算成本」从抽象词变成了可感知的限制，并直接推出「你能跑的模拟次数被严重限制」这个结论，进而成为 §2.18 中 AI/ML 的最大卖点。

⚠️ 转写把 "biogeochemical" 说成 "biozoonochemical"，属转写讹误，已登记勘误表。

**关于第二条「Cause and effect not finally established」的读法提醒：** 这句在讲义上是孤立的一行，容易被误读成「科学界不确定人类是不是元凶」。结合讲师的其余表述（§2.16「只有加入人为强迫模式才对得上」、答案 deck Q6「it is **scientifically established** that human activities have been the **primary driver**」），这一行应理解为**模式方法论意义上的谨慎表述**（模式相关性不等于严格因果证明），而非对归因结论本身的怀疑。

### 2.18 GCM 的未来：AI 与机器学习（Slide 29、30）

这是讲师**在课前几天临时加进来的新内容**（他自己说 "a bit that I've added in the last couple of days that might not be reflected on the canvas side"），因此格外可能出现在考题里——Slide 36 的例题列表最后一条正是它。

**Slide 29：为什么需要新方法**
> - "Current GCMs rely heavily on **physical equations and parameterisations**."
> - "**Struggle with small-scale processes and nonlinear climate behaviour**."
> - "Require **very high computational resources**."
> - "**Artificial Intelligence (AI) and Machine Learning (ML) are possible solutions**"
>   - "Used to **analyse large and complex climate datasets**."
>   - "Improves **prediction accuracy** in weather and climate systems."
>   - "Helps **accelerate climate research and simulations**."

**Slide 30：优势与挑战**
> - **优势**："handles **nonlinear relationships and large datasets**"；"**Faster simulations** and **improved extreme event prediction**"
> - **挑战**："**model interpretability** and **data bias**"；"Ensuring **physical consistency with climate laws**"

**讲师的架构对比图讲解（转写独有，非常清楚）：**

> "this is a **current setup** where you have a **range of inputs**. You run through your **differential equations, hundreds of thousands of lines of code** to come up with **different outputs and different impacts**. These are all based on **physical constants and physical parameterisations and different differential equations** which ensures that these models are **based in science**. But they are **computationally expensive, very hard to run** and lots of effort goes into them.
>
> An **alternative** is that you input the same, **you give the AI and machine learning models the same inputs**. It runs through an **unregulated machine learning or AI model** and it comes up with the **same outputs**. The advantage of that is that **nonlinear relationships are much more easily picked up** ... and ultimately it leads to **much faster simulations and potentially improved extreme weather prediction**. The **challenge** here is **interpretability** of these models given that they are **largely unregulated and not underpinned by that physical science parameterisation**. Which means that there is a **possibility of these models not complying with physical laws**."

两条路线的对照（可以直接画在答卷上）：

| | **物理型 GCM** | **AI / ML 模式** |
|---|---|---|
| 中间层 | 微分方程 + 物理常数 + 参数化，几十万行代码 | 从数据中学到的统计映射（"unregulated"） |
| 输入 / 输出 | 相同 | 相同 |
| 优点 | **物理自洽**，结果一定符合守恒律；可解释 | **快**；擅长**非线性关系**；能跑大量情景；**极端事件预测**可能更好 |
| 缺点 | **算力极贵**（超算数周）；小尺度过程差 | **可解释性差（interpretability）**；**数据偏差（data bias）**；**可能违反物理定律**；对训练集之外的状态外推不可靠 |

**讲师的现实注脚：**

> "But nevertheless this is an **exciting opportunity** and is indeed **being used by our department quite regularly now**."

**答案 deck（Q9 Answer）给出的官方立场——这是本周最明确的一个「标准答案」：**

> "The most likely future approach is **hybrid modelling**, where **machine learning techniques complement physics-based models** to improve accuracy, efficiency, and representation of complex processes."

也就是说：**问「AI 会取代物理模式吗」，标准答案是「不会取代，而是互补，走混合建模路线」**，并要给出理由（物理约束 vs 数据依赖）。

## 三、公式与图表

### 3.1 唯一的定量框架：大气箱模型的质量收支（Slide 21）

**讲义给出的全部符号（Slide 21 图上的标签）：**

| 符号 | 讲义写法 | 中文含义 | 单位 |
|---|---|---|---|
| **X** | X | 箱内被追踪的物种（molecule X，可以是温室气体或气溶胶粒子） | — |
| **m** | "Let **m [kg]** be the mass of X in the box" | 箱内 X 的**质量** | **kg** |
| **E** | Emission | **直接排放**（源） | 质量/时间，kg s⁻¹ |
| **P** | Chemical production | **化学生成**（源） | kg s⁻¹ |
| **F_in** | Inflow / Fin | 从相邻箱**流入**（源） | kg s⁻¹ |
| **D** | Deposition | **沉降**（汇：湿沉降或重力沉降） | kg s⁻¹ |
| **L** | Chemical loss | **化学损耗**（汇） | kg s⁻¹ |
| **F_out** | Outflow / Fout | 向相邻箱**流出**（汇） | kg s⁻¹ |

**讲义与转写共同给出的关系（讲师原话："the sum of the sources minus the sum of the sinks"）：**

$$
\frac{dm}{dt} \;=\; \underbrace{E + P + F_{in}}_{\text{sources 源}} \;-\; \underbrace{\left(L + D + F_{out}\right)}_{\text{sinks 汇}}
$$

- **左边 dm/dt**：箱内物种 X 的质量随时间的变化率，单位 kg s⁻¹。
- **稳态（steady state）条件**：当 dm/dt = 0，即源汇平衡，箱内浓度不随时间变化。

⚠️ **诚实说明**：讲义 Slide 21 只画了箱子和标签、写了 "Let m [kg] be the mass of X in the box"，**并没有把这条微分方程写出来**；上面这个等式是把讲义的符号与讲师口头的「源之和减汇之和」拼合而成的，符号与配对关系全部来自材料，但**方程的书写形式是整理结果**。

**适用条件 / 边界（讲义明写）：**
- 「**spatial distribution of X within box is not resolved**」——**箱内充分混合假设**：不区分箱内部的空间分布。
- 「**box can be global atmosphere or some subdomain**」——箱子的尺度可大可小：可以是**整个全球大气**（做全球收支时），也可以是 GCM 中的**一个网格格子**（做三维输运时）。

**典型物种的项数差异（讲师明确强调）：**
- 「**not all molecules will have chemical production or direct emission or direct chemical losses or deposition**」——不同物种缺项不同：
  - **CO₂**：以直接排放 E 为主；化学生成 P ≈ 0。
  - **对流层臭氧 O₃**：**没有直接排放**，全靠化学生成 P（光化学反应）。
  - **CH₄**：有直接排放 E，主要汇是化学损耗 L（与 OH 自由基反应）。
  - **气溶胶**：既有直接排放（一次气溶胶）又有化学生成（二次气溶胶），主要汇是**湿沉降与重力沉降 D**。

📎 背景补充：由这个收支式可以自然定义**大气寿命（atmospheric lifetime）** τ = m / (总汇通量) = m / (L + D + F_out)，单位为时间（s 或 yr）。这解释了讲师说的「化学模式帮我们理解温室气体在大气里能活多久」，也解释了他另一句话——「**aerosol concentrations change rapidly between times and locations, whereas greenhouse gases are much more evenly spread due to their longer [lifetime]**」：**寿命短 → 来不及混合 → 时空分布不均；寿命长 → 充分混合 → 全球近乎均一。**τ 的定义式**不在本周材料中**，此处为补充。

### 3.4 Slide 18：多模式集合（Temperature Measurements: Future）

讲义 Slide 18 只有标题，**全部内容在转写里**，而且这是理解「集合（ensemble）」概念的唯一出处，务必掌握：

> "So what could our global temperatures look like **through to 2090-ish**? **图的结构（按讲师描述复述）：**
- **左侧面板**：**多模式集合的最佳估计（best estimate）**——把约 35–40 个 IPCC 认可的 GCM 聚合后的结果。
- **右侧/其余面板**：按升温幅度分组的子集——**较高升温模式（higher warming）** 与 **极高升温模式（very high warming）** 分别的空间格局。

- **空间维度**：不只是全球平均，而是**世界不同地区的升温格局**（讲师："we're **not just looking at global aggregate effects**, we're starting to look at **how things might change in different parts of the world**"）。

1. **没有「最好的模式」**——因此 IPCC 用**多模式集合**而不是挑一个冠军模式。**模式之间的离散度（spread）本身就是不确定性的度量。**
2. **区域格局比全球均值更有决策价值**——讲师："that can **inform the adaptations or mitigation strategies that will be employed in that region**"。

#### Q1. Why do scientists use models to study climate change?
（科学家为什么用模式来研究气候变化？）

**答案 deck 解答（Slide 8）：**

> "Climate models are used because they allow us to **develop an understanding of**:
> - **Past climate changes by recreating historical conditions (hindcasting)**
> - **Forecasting future climate trends under various greenhouse gas emission scenarios.**
> - **Examining the impacts of different factors** (e.g., GHG, aerosols, deforestation, land use change……) on **global temperature** and the effect these may have
>
> These models are **based on physics and incorporate real-world data**, improving our understanding of how climate will change into the future.
>
> **Accurate measurements are essential for verifying and improving the reliability of global climate models.** They provide the **observational data needed to test model predictions, identify uncertainties, and guide future model development**."

**🎙️ 讲师课上相关口述（可直接搬进答案）：**
- 「once we have an understanding that they're performing sufficiently well, we have **a tool to play with different emission scenarios, different interventions** ... **they allow us to pull different levers in a way that we can't do with observations**」——**这是本题最强的一句论证**；
- 「the question is **what is going to happen into the future**, and global climate models **play a key part, or are the answer**」——观测只能告诉我们过去和现在，**未来没有观测**。

**解题路径（三段式）：**
1. **过去**：hindcasting，重建历史条件，理解已发生的变化；
2. **未来**：在不同排放情景下预估未来趋势（**未来没有观测数据，只能靠模式**）；
3. **分因子**：把 GHG、气溶胶、毁林、土地利用变化等因子分开，量化各自的贡献 → 这是**归因**的基础，也是观测做不到的**反事实实验**。
- 收尾必写：模式**基于物理**、**吸收真实观测数据**，且**观测反过来验证模式**（双向关系）。

#### Q2. What are the major uncertainties in climate models, and how can they be improved?
（气候模式的主要不确定性有哪些？如何改进？）

**答案 deck 解答（Slide 10）：**

> "Sources of uncertainty include but are not limited to:
> - **Cloud representation**: Clouds contribute to **both warming and cooling**, but **small-scale cloud processes are challenging to model**. **This is a very active area of research at the University.**
> - **Aerosol effects**: The **cooling impact of aerosols** (e.g., from pollution or volcanic eruptions) is **not yet fully understood**. Again, **this is a very active area of research at the University**.
> - **Regional climate responses**: **Model resolution limits accuracy at a local scale**, particularly for **extreme weather events**. This makes **identifying local impacts challenging**.
>
> Advances can be made through **higher-resolution models**, **enhanced observational data** (e.g., satellite measurements or more dense ground-based observations), and maybe the use of **artificial intelligence** to refine operations."

**🎙️ 讲师课上相关口述：**
- Slide 25 的完整六项误差来源（**grid size / atmospheric levels / regional response / natural variability / cloud representation / rainfall parameterisation**）——**答题时用讲义的六项比答案 deck 的三项更全**；
- 「there are **lots of information at the surface** of the earth. But **much less as we move up into the atmosphere and down into the ocean**」——垂直方向观测稀疏；
- 「**predicting rainfall as a result of those aerosol–cloud interactions is also more challenging**」；
- 「**Simulation can take weeks on supercomputers**」——算力是改进的物理约束。

**解题路径与判分要点：**
1. **先分类再展开**：可分为 ①**分辨率类**（网格大小、大气分层）②**过程表征类**（云、降水参数化、气溶胶）③**内在不可预测类**（自然变率、火山）④**结构缺失类**（氮循环等未纳入的过程）；
2. **对每一项要说清「为什么难」**，不能只列名字。云的标准说法是：**同时贡献升温与降温，净效应是两个大项之差；且过程尺度远小于网格，只能参数化**；
3. **改进措施三件套**：**更高分辨率 + 更好的观测（卫星 + 加密地基）+ AI/ML**；
4. 📌 **答案 deck 两次写「这是曼大非常活跃的研究领域」**（云、气溶胶）——这是讲师在意的点，答题时提一句「cloud and aerosol processes remain the leading uncertainties and are active research areas」很讨巧。

#### Q3. What is the difference between natural variability and human-induced climate change? How do we model their effects in climate studies?
（自然变率与人为气候变化有何区别？我们在气候研究中如何模拟两者的效应？）

**答案 deck 解答（Slide 12）：**

> - "**Natural variability** refers to fluctuations in the climate system caused by processes such as **ocean circulation, solar activity, solar input and volcanic eruptions**."
> - "**Human induced climate change**, by contrast, results from activities such as **burning fossil fuels, deforestation, and industrial emissions**, which **alter greenhouse gas concentrations**."
> - "**Observational records**, including temperature and precipitation data, are **compared with climate models** to distinguish natural variability from anthropogenic effects."
> - "Climate models can also be **run with and without anthropogenic inputs** to differentiate between the two types of processes."

**🎙️ 讲师课上相关口述：**
- Slide 27 的四步协议（§2.16）就是本题「how do we model」的详细版；
- 「that's only because global climate models predict very nicely what is happening up to today **but only when anthropogenic forcings are included**」——**这是本题的杀手句**；
- ENSO/La Niña 可驱动**几十分之一度**的温度变化 → 自然变率的**典型量级**。

**解题路径：**
1. **定义对照**：自然变率的**驱动源**（海洋环流、太阳活动、太阳输入、火山）vs 人为变化的**驱动源**（化石燃料燃烧、毁林、工业排放 → 改变温室气体浓度）；
2. 📎 背景补充：还可以按**时间特征**区分——自然变率多为**振荡型（往复、无长期净趋势）**，人为强迫是**单调累积型（持续趋势）**。此对照未见于材料，属补充；
3. **建模方法两条**：① 观测记录与模式输出对比；② **有/无人为输入的双跑（run with and without anthropogenic inputs）**；
4. **判分关键**：必须点出「**只有加入人为强迫，模式才能重现观测**」这个结论，否则只答了「怎么做」没答「结论是什么」。

#### Q4. What are the key components of a Global Climate Model, and why is coupling different models important?
（GCM 的关键组件是什么？为什么耦合不同模式很重要？）

**答案 deck 解答（Slide 14）：**

> "GCMs consist of **five major components**:
> - **Atmosphere** – Governs **weather patterns, temperature, and precipitation**.
> - **Cryosphere** – Includes **ice sheets, glaciers, and sea ice**, which influence **albedo and ocean circulation**.
> - **Chemistry** – Represents **atmospheric composition**, including **greenhouse gases and aerosols**.
> - **Ocean** – Regulates **heat distribution through currents** and interacts with the atmosphere.
> - **Biosphere** – Encompasses **vegetation, land use, and ecosystems**, which affect **carbon cycling and climate feedbacks**.
>
> **Coupling these components is crucial, as their interactions drive climate variability.** For example:
> - **Ocean currents influence atmospheric circulation patterns.**
> - **Land-use changes impact surface albedo and greenhouse gas concentrations.**
> - **Ice dynamics regulate sea level and energy balance.**
>
> **Without coupling, GCMs would fail to represent critical climate feedback mechanisms**, such as **ENSO** and long-term climate shifts. Integrating these components ensures a more accurate representation of Earth's climate system. **Very big steps forward have been made in this space over the last 20 years.**"

**⚠️ 注意组件数目的差异**：本答案说**五个**（Atmosphere / Cryosphere / Chemistry / Ocean / Biosphere），而讲义 Slide 10 说**四个**（Atmosphere / Land / Ocean / Sea ice）。**本题作为「GCM 的组件」标准答案，用答案 deck 的五组件版本**；若要兼顾，可写成「基础的物理内核是大气—陆面—海洋—海冰四大组件，现代耦合地球系统模式在此之上再加化学与生物圈模块」。已登记勘误表。

**🎙️ 讲师课上相关口述（本题的最佳素材全在这里）：**
- **反例论证**：早期大气模式与海洋模式**各跑各的、输出不互通**；
- **具体反馈链**：暖海洋 → CO₂ 溶解度下降 → 脱气 → 温室效应增强 → 继续升温（**正反馈，横跨大气—海洋边界**）；
- **ENSO 论证**：耦合后才能模拟 La Niña/El Niño，其温度效应达**几十分之一度**；
- **Coupler 的模块化意义**：「pick your favourite atmospheric model to couple to your favourite biosphere model」；
- **非线性**：「You can now **pick up different feedback loops, which may induce nonlinear responses**」。

**解题路径与判分要点：**
1. **列五组件 + 每个一句功能说明**（不要只列名字）；
2. **回答「为什么耦合」要用「反例 + 机制」结构**：先说不耦合会漏掉什么（跨组件反馈），再举 1–2 个具体反馈链（**海洋 CO₂ 脱气正反馈** 或 **冰—反照率反馈**）；
3. **必提 ENSO**——答案 deck 明确点名，是关键词；
4. **加分点**：指出耦合捕捉的是**非线性**响应，不是各组件效应的简单相加；以及 coupler 带来的**模块化**。

#### Q5. How can volcanic eruptions serve as a "natural experiment" for studying climate response to aerosol forcing?
（火山喷发如何充当研究气候对气溶胶强迫响应的「自然实验」？）

**答案 deck 解答（Slide 16）：**

> - "Large volcanic eruptions can **inject large quantities of aerosols (mostly sulphate) into the stratosphere**, which **temporarily cool the Earth's surface**."
> - "**Aerosols in the stratosphere have a longer lifetime than those in the troposphere**, so their **cooling effects last longer**."
> - "These events allow scientists to observe **short-term (around a year) climatic responses to changes in radiative forcing**."
> - "**Smaller eruptions** also emit large concentrations of aerosols **into the troposphere, like anthropogenic sources of aerosol**."
> - "They provide **real world data to test and validate climate model predictions** about aerosol impacts."
> - "Case studies, such as the eruption of **Mount Pinatubo in 1991**, are important examples where **measurable effects have been observed**."
> - "Figure to the right shows key examples too"（⚠️ 该配图未能从 pptx 提取，本笔记无法复述其内容。）

**🎙️ 讲师课上相关口述：**
- Slide 25 误差来源里：「Things like **natural climate variability are often hard to predict. Things like volcanoes.**」——火山既是**检验工具**，也是**不确定性来源**；
- 答案 deck D2 第 4 条：「Test the model **with and without specific forcing factors** (e.g. greenhouse gases **or volcanic aerosols**)」——火山正是这套「有/无强迫」检验的标准案例。

**解题路径（「自然实验」这个词要拆开答）：**
1. **为什么是「实验」**：火山提供了一次**已知时刻、已知量级、脉冲式的辐射强迫扰动**，而这是我们无法人为制造的；
2. **为什么能观测到响应**：**平流层**气溶胶（主要是**硫酸盐 sulphate**）寿命比对流层长 → 冷却效应能持续足够久（**约一年量级**）被观测到；
3. **怎么用**：把观测到的降温幅度与模式预测的降温幅度对比 → **验证模式对气溶胶强迫的响应是否正确**；
4. **举例**：**1991 年皮纳图博火山（Mount Pinatubo）**；
5. **延伸**：**小型喷发把气溶胶排入对流层，与人为气溶胶源类似** → 可用于检验模式对**人为气溶胶**的处理。
- **判分关键**：必须区分**平流层 vs 对流层气溶胶的寿命差异**，这是本题的核心机制；只写「火山让地球变冷」得不到高分。

📎 背景补充：平流层气溶胶寿命长的原因是平流层**层结稳定、没有对流性湿沉降**，清除只能靠缓慢的重力沉降与向对流层的输送；而对流层气溶胶几天到一两周就被降水冲刷掉。此机制解释未出现在材料中。

#### Q6. Can natural variability explain observed trends in global warming? Why or why not?
（自然变率能解释观测到的全球变暖趋势吗？为什么？）

**答案 deck 解答（Slide 18）：**

> - "Earth's climate history has been reconstructed using **indirect records** such as **ice cores, tree rings, glacier lengths, pollen remains, and ocean sediments**, alongside studies of **Earth's orbital changes**."
> - "These records reveal **natural climate variability over long timescales**, but **they do not explain the warming observed since the 1950s**. Instead, it is **scientifically established that human activities have been the primary driver** of this warming."
> - "Key greenhouse gases, **carbon dioxide, methane, and nitrous oxide** have risen significantly ... due to human activities. **Can you remember how much by?**"
> - "Their concentrations are now **higher than at any point in the last 800,000 years**. These emissions have **intensified the greenhouse effect**."
> - "While natural processes have always influenced climate, **they cannot account for the recent warming**."
> - "**This is all confirmed by the IPCC**."
> - "**Solar variations**, for instance, can affect the amount of energy reaching Earth, but **satellite data since 1978 show no net increase in solar output**, even as global temperatures continue to rise."

**📌 答案 deck 里嵌了一道回头题：「Can you remember how much by?」**（三种关键温室气体各上升了多少？）——**答案 deck 没有给出这个数字**，它指向 Week 1–4 的内容。**本笔记不凭空补数值**，请回查 Week 01–Week 04 笔记中的 CO₂ / CH₄ / N₂O 工业化前对比值。

**🎙️ 讲师课上相关口述：**
- Slide 5 的三段式论证（Is it changing / Is it unusual / Is it us）；
- 「it's **unprecedented change** and it's unusual, certainly in the **last 800,000 years**」；
- Slide 27 协议 + 「**only when anthropogenic forcings are included**」——这是本题的模式端证据。

**解题路径（答案必须是「不能」+ 三层理由）：**
1. **古气候记录说明自然变率的量级和节奏**（冰芯、树轮、冰川长度、花粉、海洋沉积、轨道变化）——**但它们解释不了 1950 年代以来的增温**；
2. **温室气体浓度已超出过去 80 万年的任何时刻**——超出自然变率的包络；
3. **逐个排除自然候选因子**：太阳——**1978 年以来的卫星数据显示太阳输出没有净增加**，而温度持续上升（这是最漂亮的一条反证）；火山——只有短期冷却效应；
4. **模式证据**：只跑自然强迫对不上观测，加入人为强迫才吻合；
5. **权威背书**：IPCC 确认。
- **判分关键**：「太阳输出自 1978 年卫星观测以来无净增加」这条**必须写**，它是把「自然变率」这个假说定量证伪的关键证据。

  1. **输入端**：更好的**初始条件**、更准的关键变量估计；
  2. **验证端**：validate 模式输出、建立**长期连续序列**以检出趋势；
  3. **改进端**：改进**薄弱过程的表征**与**参数化**，填补偏远地区（海洋、极地、高空）的空白。
- **加分句**：观测不仅验证模式，**还告诉建模者哪些过程必须被包含进去**（讲师的「three pillar approach」）。

#### Q9. Do you think AI and machine learning will eventually replace physics-based climate models, or just complement them? Why?
（你认为 AI 与机器学习最终会取代基于物理的气候模式，还是只是补充它们？为什么？）

**答案 deck 解答（Slide 24）：**

> - "Current GCMs are **grounded in physical laws**, which ensures that simulations **remain physically realistic**. **AI models typically learn patterns from data and do not inherently enforce these physical constraints.**"
> - "AI and machine learning are **powerful for analysing large datasets**. They can rapidly identify patterns in climate observations and model outputs that may be **difficult for traditional methods to detect**."
> - "Machine learning can **improve parameterisations of small-scale processes** (such as **clouds, turbulence, or convection**) that are difficult to represent accurately in traditional models."
> - "AI can **significantly accelerate climate simulations**. They can reproduce parts of climate models much faster, allowing scientists to **run many more scenarios or sensitivity tests**."
> - "AI can help **improve prediction of extreme events** (e.g., heatwaves, storms, or heavy rainfall) by learning from large historical datasets and high-resolution simulations."
> - "**However, AI models depend strongly on the quality and quantity of training data, which may limit their reliability when projecting conditions that have not previously occurred.**"
> - "**The most likely future approach is HYBRID MODELLING, where machine learning techniques complement physics-based models** to improve accuracy, efficiency, and representation of complex processes."

**🎙️ 讲师课上相关口述：**
- 两条路线的架构对比（同样的输入 → 微分方程 vs 「unregulated」ML 模型 → 同样的输出）；
- 优势：「**nonlinear relationships are much more easily picked up**」「**much faster simulations and potentially improved extreme weather prediction**」；
- 挑战：「**interpretability** ... given that they are **largely unregulated and not underpinned by that physical science parameterisation** ... there is a **possibility of these models not complying with physical laws**」；
- 现实注脚：「this is an **exciting opportunity** and is indeed **being used by our department quite regularly now**」。

**解题路径（这是一道「立场题」，但立场是被指定的）：**
1. **明确表态：互补，不是取代**（答案 deck 的官方立场是 **hybrid modelling**）；
2. **给 AI 记功（至少三条）**：处理大数据集与**非线性关系**；改进**次网格过程的参数化**（云、湍流、对流）；**加速模拟** → 能跑更多情景与敏感性试验；改进**极端事件**预测；
3. **给 AI 记过（关键的两条）**：
   - **不内含物理约束** → 结果可能违反守恒律等物理定律；**可解释性（interpretability）差**；
   - **强烈依赖训练数据的质量与数量** → **对历史上从未出现过的状态外推不可靠**；
4. **收尾**：物理模式保证**物理自洽**，AI 提供**速度与模式识别能力**，两者结合 = **混合建模**。
- **判分要点**：⭐ 第 3 条的第二点是本题的**思维深度分**——气候预估的本质就是外推到从未出现过的气候状态，而这恰恰是纯数据驱动模型最弱的地方。答出这一点，本题就答到位了。

### 6.2 概念混淆点

| 易混淆的一对 | 区别 | 常见错法 |
|---|---|---|
| **气候（climate） vs 天气（weather）** | 气候 = 长期平均（典型 **30 年**）；天气 = 小时/日尺度的具体状况。**GCM 预测前者，不预测后者。** | 用「模式没预测到某场暴雨」质疑模式；把 GCM 当天气预报模型 |
| **预报（forecast） vs 后报（hindcast）** | forecast = 往未来预测；hindcast = **用模式去重现已知的过去**，目的是**检验模式** | 把 hindcasting 说成「预测过去会发生什么」而不点出它的**检验**功能 |
| **RCP vs SSP** | 两代不同的情景框架；**本课讲义只用 RCP**。讲师口头混用了 SSP1-2.6 / SSP2-4.5 | 在答卷里把两套编号混着写 |
| **气溶胶直接效应 vs 云反照率（间接）效应** | 直接：粒子自身散射/吸收太阳辐射；间接：粒子作为云凝结核改变**云的形成、寿命、降水率** | 只说「气溶胶让地球变冷」，不分机制 |
| **平流层气溶胶 vs 对流层气溶胶** | 平流层**寿命长** → 冷却效应持续更久（约一年量级）；对流层寿命短 | Q5 只答「火山让地球变冷」，漏掉寿命差异这个核心 |
| **GCM 的四组件 vs 五组件** | Slide 10 的四组件（大气/陆地/海洋/海冰）是**基础物理内核**；答案 deck Q4 的五组件（大气/冰冻圈/化学/海洋/生物圈）是**现代耦合地球系统模式** | 两个列表混着背，答题时说不清问的是哪一层 |
| **解析（resolved） vs 参数化（parameterised）** | 大于网格尺度的过程被**显式解析**；小于网格尺度的（云、湍流、对流）只能用经验关系**参数化** | 把「参数化」当成「调参」；不理解为什么云一定要参数化 |
| **模式内部变率 vs 观测的年际起伏** | 耦合模式**能产生**统计上正确的 ENSO，但**不承诺时间上对齐**真实的 ENSO 事件 | 用「模式没预测到 2015 年的 El Niño」否定模式 |
| **适应（adaptation） vs 减缓（mitigation）** | 讲师明说：「There is a **big difference** between adapting to climate change and mitigating climate change」；Week 8 讲适应，Week 10 讲减缓 | 两个词混用 |

### 6.4 常见推理谬误

1. **「模式没算对去年的天气，所以模式不可信。」**
   ❌ 混淆气候与天气。Slide 26 的图恰恰同时展示了「集合平均抓不住年际起伏」和「20 年趋势抓得很好」——**前者不是缺陷，是设计取舍**。

2. **「模式很复杂，参数很多，所以是调出来的。」**
   ❌ 忽略了 Slide 27 协议的第 ② 步：模式必须先在**无强迫状态下重现观测到的气候统计特征**才能用；以及 §3.6 的盲测——**2004 年的模式预测了它当年不可能知道的 2004–2022 观测**。

3. **「自然变率过去造成过更大的变化，所以现在也可能是自然的。」**
   ❌ 答案 deck Q6 的三重反驳：① 古气候记录显示的自然变率**解释不了 1950 年代以来的增温**；② 温室气体浓度**超出过去 80 万年任何时刻**；③ **1978 年以来卫星观测显示太阳输出无净增加**，而温度仍在上升。

4. **「分辨率越高越好，那就一直加密网格。」**
   ❌ 忽略权衡：分辨率提高会**挤占能跑的模拟次数**（§2.4），而集合与多情景本身也是精度的来源。讲师说「there's a **trade-off between the number of simulations you can run** and the resolution」。

5. **「耦合就是把各组件的效应加起来。」**
   ❌ 耦合捕捉的是**反馈**与**非线性响应**，不是线性叠加。海洋 CO₂ 脱气正反馈就是典型例子。

6. **「AI 更准，所以会取代物理模式。」**
   ❌ AI 不内含物理约束、可解释性差，且**对训练数据之外的状态外推不可靠**——而气候预估的本质就是外推到从未出现过的状态。官方立场是**混合建模（hybrid modelling）**。

7. **「模式在区域尺度不准，所以区域预估没用。」**
   ❌ 这正是为什么要提高分辨率、做区域降尺度；而且讲师强调**「怎么把输出沟通出去」和分辨率同等重要**（农民案例）。「不准」是要改进的方向，不是放弃的理由。

8. **「Slide 28 写着『Cause and effect not finally established』，所以科学界对归因还没有共识。」**
   ❌ 这是**模式方法论层面的谨慎表述**。同一套材料里，答案 deck Q6 明写「it is **scientifically established** that human activities have been the **primary driver**」，并说「This is all **confirmed by the IPCC**」。两者不矛盾：模式相关性不等于严格的因果证明，但归因结论本身有多条独立证据支撑。

### 6.6 本周最难的三个点（建议重点攻克）

1. **理解「hindcasting 为什么能构成信心的来源」——以及它的局限。**
   难点在于这是一个**认识论**问题而不是技术问题：用过去检验的模型凭什么能预测未来？标准答案是「模式基于物理而非拟合，且经过盲测」；但要同时说得出局限（调参风险、未来状态可能超出历史范围）。

2. **把「模式」和「归因」的关系说清楚。**
   很多人只会说「模式预测未来」，而本周最重要的用法其实是**用模式做反事实实验来归因过去**（Slide 27 协议 + 「only when anthropogenic forcings are included」）。**这是 Slide 5 那条论证链的收口，也是例题第 3、4 条的核心。**

3. **RCP 的双重身份。**
   它既是「四种未来情景」（大家都知道的那一面），又是「跨模式可比性的标准化协议」（讲师强调、讲义没写的那一面）。**答题时两面都要写。**

## 七、本周术语表

| 英文 | 中文 | 一句话释义 |
|---|---|---|
| **Global Climate Model (GCM)** | 全球气候模式 | 把地球划分为三维网格、在每个格子内求解物理方程以模拟长期气候的计算机模式 |
| **Climate model** | 气候模式 | 同上；本课中与 GCM 基本混用 |
| **Climate（vs Weather）** | 气候（相对天气） | 长时间（典型 30 年）的平均天气状况；天气是小时至日尺度的具体状况 |
| **Climatological timescale** | 气候态时间尺度 | 约 30 年的平均时段，是 GCM 表现最好的时间尺度 |
| **Multiannual scale** | 多年尺度 | 数年至数十年，GCM 表现良好的时间范围下限 |
| **Radiative forcing** | 辐射强迫 | 某因子造成的地球能量收支净变化；讲义称其为「气候模式的核心概念」 |
| **Coupling / Coupled model** | 耦合 / 耦合模式 | 让不同组件模式（大气、海洋等）互相交换信息，从而模拟跨组件反馈 |
| **Coupler** | 耦合器 | 2010 年代架构中位于各组件模式之间、负责信息交换的中枢模块，使组件可自由替换 |
| **Earth system model** | 地球系统模式 | 在物理气候模式之上再耦合化学、生物圈、冰冻圈等的完整模式 |
| **Atmosphere model** | 大气模式 | 模拟大气动力与能量传输的组件（早期版本不含大气化学成分） |
| **Ocean model** | 海洋模式 | 模拟海洋环流与热力学的组件 |
| **Chemistry model** | 化学模式 | 模拟大气气体与气溶胶的相互作用、成分变化（臭氧、气溶胶、空气质量）的组件 |
| **Biosphere model** | 生物圈模式 | 模拟陆地植被、生态系统与生物地球化学循环（光合、呼吸、碳循环）的组件 |
| **Cryosphere model** | 冰冻圈模式 | 模拟冰盖、冰川、积雪与永久冻土动态的组件 |
| **Sea ice** | 海冰 | GCM 四大基础组件之一；影响反照率与海洋环流 |
| **Grid / 3-D grid** | 网格 / 三维网格 | 把地球沿水平、垂直（上至大气、下至海洋）切分成的立方体阵列 |
| **Grid cell / cube** | 网格单元 / 立方体 | 网格中的单个计算单元，逐个求解并与邻格交换输入输出 |
| **Resolution** | 分辨率 | 网格的精细程度；水平分辨率、垂直层数与时间步长都属此列 |
| **Horizontal resolution** | 水平分辨率 | 网格沿地表方向的尺度；早期 GCM 为 250–600 km |
| **Vertical levels** | 垂直层数 | 模式在大气或海洋方向上的分层数；HadCM3 为大气 19 层、海洋 20 层 |
| **Time resolution** | 时间分辨率 | 各网格被计算的时间频率；讲师强调与空间分辨率同等重要 |
| **Parameterisation** | 参数化 | 用经验或统计关系近似表示小于网格尺度、无法显式解析的过程（云、湍流、对流、降水） |
| **Rainfall parameterisation** | 降水参数化 | 降水过程的参数化；因涉及气溶胶—云相互作用而尤其困难 |
| **Cloud representation** | 云的表征 | 模式中对云的处理；因云同时致暖致冷且尺度过小，是最大不确定性来源之一 |
| **Hindcasting** | 后报 / 回报 | 用模式重现过去（约 100 年）的气候并与观测比对，以检验模式可靠性 |
| **Forecasting** | 预报 | 用模式预测未来气候 |
| **Ensemble mean** | 集合平均 | 多个模式（或多次模拟）结果的平均；抓得住多年趋势，抓不住年际起伏 |
| **Attribution** | 归因 | 判定观测到的气候变化由哪些因子造成；由物理 + 模式 + 测量共同支撑 |
| **Natural climate variability** | 自然气候变率 | 由海洋环流、太阳活动、火山等自然过程引起的气候波动 |
| **Anthropogenic climate change** | 人为气候变化 | 由化石燃料燃烧、毁林、工业排放等人类活动引起的气候变化 |
| **External forcing** | 外强迫 | 施加于气候系统之外的驱动（如温室气体辐射强迫）；Slide 27 协议第一步是「无外强迫」控制试验 |
| **Control run** | 控制试验 | 无外强迫的长期模拟，用作对照基准 |
| **El Niño–Southern Oscillation (ENSO)** | 厄尔尼诺—南方涛动 | 热带太平洋海气耦合的年际变率模态；可驱动几十分之一度的全球温度变化 |
| **La Niña** | 拉尼娜 | ENSO 的冷相位 |
| **Milankovitch cycle** | 米兰科维奇旋回 | 地球轨道参数的周期性变化，是长时间尺度自然气候变率的驱动之一 |
| **Positive feedback loop** | 正反馈回路 | 初始扰动被自我放大的过程，如暖海洋 → CO₂ 脱气 → 温室效应增强 → 继续升温 |
| **Outgassing** | 脱气 | 溶解气体从海水释放进入大气；暖水溶解 CO₂ 能力下降导致 |
| **Ice-albedo feedback** | 冰—反照率反馈 | 冰雪融化 → 反照率下降 → 吸收更多辐射 → 进一步融化 |
| **Albedo** | 反照率 | 地表或云对入射太阳辐射的反射比例 |
| **Aerosol** | 气溶胶 | 悬浮于大气中的固/液态微粒；对气候有致冷作用但机制不确定性大 |
| **Direct effect (aerosol)** | 气溶胶直接效应 | 气溶胶粒子自身散射/吸收太阳辐射造成的辐射效应 |
| **Cloud albedo effect / indirect effect** | 云反照率效应 / 间接效应 | 气溶胶通过改变云的形成、寿命与降水率间接影响辐射收支 |
| **Sulphate aerosol** | 硫酸盐气溶胶 | 大型火山喷发注入平流层的主要气溶胶种类，造成暂时性地表降温 |
| **Stratosphere / Troposphere** | 平流层 / 对流层 | 平流层气溶胶寿命长、冷却效应持续更久；对流层气溶胶寿命短 |
| **Tropospheric ozone** | 对流层臭氧 | 非直接排放，由一系列光化学反应生成 |
| **Lifetime (atmospheric)** | 大气寿命 | 物种在大气中的滞留时间；决定其时空分布是否均匀 |
| **Box model** | 箱模型 | 把一个空间区域视作内部充分混合的「箱子」，用源汇收支计算其中物种质量的方法 |
| **Source / Sink** | 源 / 汇 | 增加（排放、化学生成、流入）/ 减少（沉降、化学损耗、流出）箱内物种质量的项 |
| **Emission (E)** | 排放 | 物种被直接释放进入大气的源项 |
| **Deposition (D)** | 沉降 | 物种被移除至地表的汇项；含湿沉降与重力沉降 |
| **Wet deposition** | 湿沉降 | 通过并入降水而被移除 |
| **Gravitational settling** | 重力沉降 | 粒子因重力下沉而被移除 |
| **Chemical production (P) / Chemical loss (L)** | 化学生成 / 化学损耗 | 由化学反应造成的源项 / 汇项 |
| **Inflow / Outflow (F_in / F_out)** | 流入 / 流出 | 与相邻箱之间的输运通量 |
| **Representative Concentration Pathway (RCP)** | 代表性浓度路径 | 描述 21 世纪温室气体排放与浓度、空气污染物排放及土地利用的四条标准化路径 |
| **RCP2.6 / 4.5 / 6.0 / 8.5** | — | 分别为严格减排、两条中间情景、极高排放情景 |
| **Baseline scenario** | 基线情景 | 无额外减排努力的情景，落在 RCP6.0 与 RCP8.5 之间 |
| **Shared Socioeconomic Pathway (SSP)** | 共享社会经济路径 | 新一代情景框架；讲师口头提及（SSP1-2.6、SSP2-4.5），但本课讲义使用 RCP |
| **Negative emission technologies** | 负排放技术 | 主动从大气移除温室气体的技术；讲师称 RCP2.6 大概率需要它 |
| **IPCC** | 政府间气候变化专门委员会 | Intergovernmental Panel on Climate Change；汇集并评估全球气候模式与研究 |
| **FAR / SAR / TAR / AR4** | 第一/二/三/四次评估报告 | 1990 / 1995 / 2001 / 2007 年发布 |
| **Kyoto Protocol** | 京都议定书 | 1997 年通过；SAR 为其提供了重要科学依据 |
| **WGIII** | 第三工作组 | IPCC 负责减缓的工作组；Slide 32 的情景分档出自其情景数据库 |
| **HadCM3** | 哈德利中心第三代耦合模式 | 英国气象局哈德利中心开发的先驱性耦合 GCM；大气 19 层、海洋 20 层 |
| **Hadley Centre** | 哈德利中心 | 英国气象局的气候预测与研究中心 |
| **Met Office** | （英国）气象局 | HadCM3 的开发机构 |
| **Downscaling / local projections** | 降尺度 / 局地预估 | 把全球模式结果细化到区域尺度，如英国夏季降水预估 |
| **Nitrogen cycle** | 氮循环 | 尚未完整纳入 GCM 的生物地球化学循环之一 |
| **Biogeochemical cycle** | 生物地球化学循环 | 元素在生物圈、大气、海洋与岩石圈之间的循环 |
| **Artificial Intelligence (AI) / Machine Learning (ML)** | 人工智能 / 机器学习 | 从数据中学习模式的方法；用于加速模拟、改进参数化、预测极端事件 |
| **Hybrid modelling** | 混合建模 | 机器学习与物理模式结合的路线；答案 deck 认定为最可能的未来方向 |
| **Interpretability** | 可解释性 | 能否理解模型为何给出该结果；AI 模式的主要短板 |
| **Data bias** | 数据偏差 | 训练数据的偏倚导致模型结果失真 |
| **Nonlinear response** | 非线性响应 | 输出与输入不成比例的响应；耦合模式与 AI 模式都以捕捉它为卖点 |
| **Mitigation / Adaptation** | 减缓 / 适应 | 减少排放与变化本身 / 调整社会与生态系统以应对已发生的变化 |
| **Geoengineering** | 地球工程 | 主动干预气候系统的技术；Week 9 客座讲座主题 |
| **Marine cloud whitening / brightening** | 海洋云增白 / 海云增亮 | 通过改变海洋上空云的性质与寿命来增加反射的地球工程方案 |
| **Mentimeter** | （课堂互动工具） | 讲师用于收集课堂答案与复习课提问的在线平台 |
