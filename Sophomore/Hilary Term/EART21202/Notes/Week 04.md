## 二、核心概念

### 2.1 气溶胶（Aerosol）到底是什么

**是什么。** 讲义 Slide 8 给的是一个"必须知道"的教科书定义：

> **Aerosol is suspended particles in gas**（气溶胶是悬浮于气体中的颗粒物）
> 定义出处：*Atmospheric Chemistry and Physics: From Air Pollution to Climate Change*, Seinfeld, John H.; Pandis, Spyros N., Third edition, page 47.

Slide 9 进一步说：**气溶胶是气体与悬浮颗粒的混合物（a mixture of gas and suspended particles）**。

这里有一个学生极易踩的坑，讲师在课上专门强调了两遍：

> "Aerosol, suspended particles in gas, that's what it is. And it's **the combination of particles and gas, that is the aerosol**."

也就是说，**严格意义上的气溶胶 = 颗粒 + 承载它们的气体，两者合起来才叫气溶胶**；单说那些颗粒，正确的词是**颗粒物（particulate matter, PM）**。

但讲师紧接着补了一句极其实用的"行规"提醒（这句话只在转写里有）：

> "throughout the scientific literature when you see aerosol, most likely people will be talking about **particulate matter**. Although technically that's just one subset of the aerosol. It's the gas phase as well as the particle phase. People are going to be talking about particulate matter, okay?"

**结论**：定义题要按严格定义答（颗粒 + 气体）；但读文献时看到 "aerosol"，作者八成指的是颗粒物那一部分。这两件事都要知道。

**边界条件 / 怎么用。** 这个定义本身没有"适用范围"限制，但它决定了后面所有推理的起点：因为气溶胶里包含气相，所以**相分配**（下一节）才成为核心问题。

### 2.2 气–粒相分配（Gas–particle partitioning）与挥发性

**是什么。** Slide 9 原文：

> An airborne chemical will **partition between the gas- and particle-phase**（一种空气中的化学物质会在气相与颗粒相之间分配）。
> For example, **black carbon** has a very low volatility so is always in the particle-phase, whereas **water** is more volatile so is always present in the gas-phase, and often also in the particle-phase.

**为什么重要。** 因为一个物种在气相还是颗粒相，直接决定它有没有光学效应、能不能当云凝结核、能不能被湿沉降清除。相分配是气溶胶一切气候效应的前置开关。

**讲师的口头展开（转写独有，讲义没有）**：

- **水是最著名的气溶胶化学物种**："perhaps the most famous chemical for aerosol is water because it can be both present as vapour in the air, in a gas phase, but it can also condense into the condensed phase. And in fact it can freeze as well, so it can be a solid, a liquid and a gas in the atmosphere."（水在大气中固、液、气三相都存在。）
- **控制相分配的三个变量**："those sorts of phase transitions are true for a lot of species, depending on **the temperature** of the atmosphere where it is and **the volatility** of that species, as well as **its concentration**."
  → 记住这三个：**温度、挥发性、浓度**。
- **夜店烟雾机的例子**（Slide 9 上应有一张夜店照片，转写里讲师在解释它）："in the nightclub image on there, it's a high concentration of probably something like **glycol** that's been ejected from the smoke gun, but as it **dilutes** into the air, there's a tendency for it to **evaporate** and then you stop seeing the particles because it's gone from the particle phase into the gas phase."
  → 这个例子精准演示了"浓度"这一项：浓度高时凝结成粒可见，稀释后蒸发回气相就看不见了。
- **黑碳的反例**："Other things like black carbon have very low volatility, so they stay as a particle phase. Even if they're diluted or they go to very high temperatures, they stay as particles, they do not evaporate."

**一次 vs 二次颗粒（primary vs secondary particle）**——这是转写里明确给出的一对定义，讲义只在别处零散提到：

> "Something like black carbon, it's low volatility all the way throughout its lifetime and so it's a **primary particle**. As soon as it's emitted, it's a particle. Something that becomes a particle after emission in the gas phase is a **secondary particle**."

- **一次颗粒（primary）**：排放时就是颗粒（黑碳）。
- **二次颗粒（secondary）**：以气体形式排放，进入大气后经化学反应生成低挥发性产物，再进入颗粒相（硫酸铵、硝酸铵）。

讲师给的二次生成路径（转写）：

> "for ammonium nitrate, ammonium sulfate, you start with emissions of **ammonia**, the gas, and you start with emissions of **nitrogen dioxide** and **sulfur dioxide**. And so you have these gases, eventually they will meet, and what they form is a **lower volatility product** which goes into the particle phase."

📎 背景补充：把这条链写成反应式大致是 NH₃(g) + H₂SO₄(g/aq) → (NH₄)₂SO₄(s/aq)，其中 H₂SO₄ 由 SO₂ 氧化而来；硝酸铵同理由 NH₃ + HNO₃ 生成，而 HNO₃ 来自 NO₂ 的氧化。**这条反应式材料中没有写出，是笔记补充的，仅供理解"低挥发性产物"这句话，不必在考试里写出配平式。**

**这一节为什么对 Workshop 1 关键**：Workshop 1 要模拟"减少硫酸铵负荷"的效果。因为硫酸铵是**二次**气溶胶，所以**排放变化与颗粒实际生成之间存在滞后（lag）**——讲师明确把这一点当作"模拟必须跑得比 3 天久得多"的理由（见第五节）。

### 2.3 气溶胶的种类、来源、汇与寿命

这一部分对应 Slide 16 与讲师围绕 AR6 WGI FAQ 6.1 Figure 1 的逐列讲解。讲师说"这张图信息量很大，我们从左往右看"，下面就按他的顺序整理。

#### （1）短寿命气候强迫因子（short-lived climate forcer, SLCF）

> "it's a short-lived climate forcer. What that means is, whereas the methane and carbon dioxide you talked about had lifetimes of **years** in the atmosphere and so become **well-mixed**, we're talking on average **three days** for particulate matter or aerosol in the atmosphere... which is **too short to be well-mixed** throughout the troposphere. It means that you get some very **localized or regional** effects occurring."

**核心推理链（本周反复出现，务必记牢）**：

> **寿命短 → 来不及混合均匀（not well mixed）→ 浓度空间分布不均 → 辐射强迫是区域性/局地性的，而非全球均匀的。**

这条链对气溶胶（~3 天）和臭氧（~3 周）都成立，对 CO₂ 和 CH₄（年尺度）不成立。

#### （2）主要种类

讲义 Slide 17 提到 brown carbon、black carbon、"salts like ammonium sulphate"。讲师在转写里给了更完整的一份清单：

> "there are lots of different types of aerosol. **Black carbon, nitrate, sulfate, organic aerosols** are four of the key ones, where the nitrate and sulfate are often combined with **ammonium** to give you **ammonium nitrate** and **ammonium sulfate**, which is salt."

- 还有**化学工程/工业（chemical engineering）**

讲师在这里插了一段很重要的政策性评论：

→ 铵的排放和粮食安全绑定，是最难削减的一类。

#### （4）空气质量的交叉议题：PM2.5

这是讲义上完全没有、只在课堂口头讲的一整段：

#### （5）寿命与汇（sinks）

讲义 Slide 16 原文：

> Aerosols stay airborne on the timescale of **minutes to weeks, with an average of days**. Key loss processes are **dry deposition** and **wet deposition**.

讲师的口头量化与解释：

> "I've said the average is about **three days** in the atmosphere, but that **varies a lot**. It could just be **seconds** if you're in a windy, rainy situation because that will wet deposit particles straight out of the atmosphere as soon as they're emitted. Whereas on dry situations with a lot of uplift, you could be in the atmosphere for **weeks**."

两种沉降的定义（转写给得比讲义清楚）：

- **湿沉降（wet deposition）**："when precipitation catches and takes particulate matter out of the air and deposits it to the surface."（降水把颗粒物冲刷到地面。）
- **干沉降（dry deposition）**："when you can do that but without rain. So your particulate matter will just so happen to be ejected either close to the ground or **through a tree canopy** or something similar and then deposit onto surfaces there."（无降水情况下颗粒物直接沉降到地表/植被冠层等表面。）

#### （6）冷却还是增暖？——由"颜色"决定

讲义 Slide 16 一句话点题：

> **The colour of aerosol determines whether it absorbs or reflects incoming solar radiation.**（气溶胶的颜色决定它是吸收还是反射入射太阳辐射。）

讲师展开：

> "**Black carbon is most definitely warming** because of its colour. It absorbs incoming radiation really well, visible radiation, and therefore has a warming effect. Whereas **most types of aerosol, and by mass also most types, are reflecting and have a cooling effect**. The salts, for example, are primarily **white**. A lot of the organic aerosol are white as well, so they're reflective and have a cooling effect."

**边界条件**："颜色决定吸收/反射"是一个直觉化的简化说法。Slide 17 的 notes 给了更严谨的版本：直接效应的总体大小取决于气溶胶的**尺度（size）、化学组成（composition）与光学性质（optical properties）**，此外**它在大气中的位置（location）**也起关键作用（详见 2.6）。

### 2.4 辐射强迫（Radiative Forcing, RF）

**是什么。** 讲义 Slide 13 给了两层定义：

> **粗略版**：An effect on Earth's radiation balance（对地球辐射平衡的一种影响）。
> **精确版**：**The change in radiation balance at the tropopause between the incoming solar radiation and the outgoing earth's radiation (net irradiance).**
> （在**对流层顶（tropopause）**处，入射太阳辐射与出射地球辐射之间辐射平衡的变化，即**净辐照度（net irradiance）**的变化。）

讲师特别强调了"必须用精确版"：

> "the summary if you like is an effect on those radiation balance. But we **do need to be more specific** than that if we're talking about it with experts **or in your exams**."

→ **这是明确的应试提示：定义题要写"对流层顶处、入射太阳辐射与出射地球辐射之间辐射平衡（净辐照度）的变化"。** 答案 deck 的 Q2 参考答案第一句就是逐字照抄这个精确定义。

**为什么定义在"对流层顶"而不是地表或大气顶。** 讲师在开场就说本周要"specifically at the top of the troposphere"思考能量平衡。📎 背景补充：**传统 RF 定义取对流层顶，是因为平流层的热力调整（stratospheric adjustment）比对流层快得多，在对流层顶处扣除平流层调整后的净通量变化，能更好地预测地表温度响应。这条解释材料中没有给出，属于笔记补充。**

#### 辐射强迫的代数示例（Slide 13 图 + 讲师逐步讲解）

讲义 Slide 13 的 notes 只留了一句 "In this case, if 'C' affects the atmosphere, then a greenhouse gas is at play and the greenhouse effect results."，真正的讲解全在转写里：

> "I've done it **algebraically** because of that is **going down the slide is going forwards in time** and in the top row is **energy in equals A** and **energy out equals A**. So that's an earth at **energy equilibrium**.
> And then what if you change it, what if you have **radiative forcing plus C** for example. Then you'll **retain** some of that energy A that comes in and you're **not sending it out** back into space. You've lost that C from the radiation going out.
> **Eventually you regain equilibrium if C is maintained constant** because you **increase the temperature** of earth such that you then send out, you make up for that loss of C that occurred initially. So you get back to A. So eventually you do get back to equilibrium but you're at a **warmer state** than you were before."

把这段整理成一张"时间演化表"（**这张表的排版是笔记的整理形式，内容全部来自转写**）：

| 时间 | 能量进（Energy in） | 能量出（Energy out） | 净收支 | 地球状态 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| t₀（初始平衡） | A | A | 0 | 平衡，温度 T₀ |
| t₁（施加强迫 +C 的瞬间） | A | A − C | **+C（净吸收）** | 失衡，开始累积能量 |
| t₂ → ∞（C 保持恒定） | A | A（温度升高后重新达到） | 0 | **新的平衡，但温度 T₁ > T₀** |

**这张表要读出三个结论**：

1. 辐射强迫的**直接后果不是升温，而是能量失衡**；升温是系统消除失衡的**方式**。
2. 只要强迫 C **保持恒定**，系统**最终会回到辐射平衡**——但停在一个**更热**的状态。
3. 因此"辐射强迫"和"温度响应"是两件事：RF 是原因（W m⁻²），ΔT 是结果（K）。二者之间的桥梁是气候敏感度（本周未展开）。

#### 自然 vs 人为的辐射强迫

讲义 Slide 14：

> - Natural changes to radiative forcing can be **relatively extreme (in magnitude and rate)**, e.g. **volcanic eruption**.
> - Natural can include changes to: **greenhouse gas sinks and albedo**.
> - But, **for the industrial period, anthropogenic changes to radiative forcers have been much greater than natural changes**.
> - Although the sources vary between natural and anthropogenic drivers, **the resulting physics (radiative forcing) is the same**.
> - Today we look at **'Other human drivers'**.

**最后两条是本周的哲学基础**，务必理解：

- **物理是同一套**。火山喷发注入平流层的硫酸盐气溶胶，和人类烧煤排放的 SO₂ 生成的硫酸盐气溶胶，散射阳光的物理过程**完全一样**；区别只在"来源"与"时间尺度"。
- **但工业期以来，人为强迫的变化远大于自然强迫的变化**——这是把注意力放在人为驱动上的正当性来源。

**边界条件**：自然强迫在**量级和速率**上可以非常极端（火山），只是它**不持续**；人为强迫的特点是**持续、单向、累积**。

#### 辐射强迫定义本身的"内在问题"（转写独有，非常重要）

讲师在讲完定义后，主动抛出一个概念难点：

> "You might have noticed there's actually already **issues with this definition**. For example... when we're trying to attribute radiative forcing to a particular anthropogenic motivation like carbon dioxide, do we mean **the instantaneous forcing** that occurs because of carbon dioxide absorption of longwave radiation? Or do we include effects because of **the resulting increase in temperature on clouds**? Do we include effects that might result from **positive feedbacks such as increased wildfire emissions** because of increase in temperature? **Where do you stop attributing changes to a particular driver and where do you start?**"

→ 这就是 **ERF（有效辐射强迫）概念存在的理由**：它是对"到哪里为止算这个驱动因子的账"这个问题的一个**标准化回答**。下一节展开。

### 2.5 有效辐射强迫（Effective Radiative Forcing, ERF）与快速调整

**是什么。** 讲义 Slide 19：

> **Effective radiative forcing (ERF, W m⁻²)**: Quantifies the change in the Earth's energy balance caused by a climate driver.
> 由两部分组成：
> - **Instantaneous change in the energy budget**（能量收支的瞬时变化）
> - **Rapid adjustments（快速调整）**: Rapid changes in the atmosphere (such as changes in cloud and water vapor) that are **uncoupled to any global surface air temperature change**（与任何全球地表气温变化**不耦合**的、大气中的快速变化，例如云与水汽的变化）。

**这个"uncoupled to surface temperature change"是 ERF 定义的命门**，必须理解：快速调整**不是**"因为地球变热了所以云变了"——那属于**反馈（feedback）**，要算进气候敏感度里；快速调整是**在地表温度还没来得及变之前**，驱动因子直接改变大气状态所引发的调整。

Slide 19 的 speaker notes 补充了时间尺度：

> "rapid adjustments represent immediate changes in the atmosphere and surface in response to a climate forcer, normally taking place over **short timescales from days to months**."

**讲师的口头讲解（用黑碳做例子，讲义没有）**：

> "**effective radiative forcing**... it's considered one of the more helpful definitions of radiative forcing or rather **specifications**, and it's the forcing where you have **instantaneous change in the energy budget** — so take for example **black carbon** particulate matter, that has a warming effect, so as soon as that's emitted, that's absorbing any incoming so[lar] radiation and it's pretty good at doing that.
> In addition to that instantaneous change, there's a **rapid adjustment** which thinks about things that occur in the atmosphere relatively quickly because of that driver — so for black carbon, it will have an effect on the **cloud properties** that we talked about, **albedo and lifetime**, and that is a rapid adjustment.
> And when you combine those two things, the instantaneous and the rapid adjustment effects, you get the **effective radiative forcing or the ERF**. And that's going to be referred to quite a lot throughout this lecture."

**公式化理解**：

> **ERF = 瞬时辐射强迫（instantaneous forcing）+ 快速调整（rapid adjustments）**

**符号约定（Slide 19 notes 明写）**：

> **Positive ERF values indicate a warming effect and negative ERF values indicate a cooling effect.**（正值 = 增暖，负值 = 冷却。）

**边界条件与适用范围**：

- 单位：**W m⁻²**。
- 基准年：IPCC AR6 中气溶胶 ERF 的评估是 **1750–2014**，即"相对 1750 年"的变化被视为工业期以来的人为影响（Slide 19 notes: "The ERF relative to the Year 1750 is regarded as anthropogenic influence over the industrial era."）。
- 评估手段：Slide 19 notes 说 AR6 对气溶胶 ERF 的评估同时使用**基于观测的估计**（含卫星观测）与**基于模式的估计**（全球气候模式 GCM 与地球系统模式 ESM）。

**一个易混点**：土地利用变化的"影响能量垂直分布"**不是**快速调整，也**不是**辐射强迫（见 2.17）。讲师说得很直白："it didn't have an instantaneous effect or even a rapid adjustment, but it does affect local climate."

### 2.6 气溶胶–辐射相互作用（Aerosol–Radiation Interaction, ARI）＝ 直接效应

**是什么。** Slide 17 标题：**Direct effect**。Slide 17 的 speaker notes 是本周信息密度最高的一段之一，逐条拆解：

> "Firstly, aerosols can **directly** affect the Earth's energy balance by **interacting with solar radiation**. This process is known as **aerosol-radiation interaction** or the **direct effect**."

**两条相反的分支**：

| 类型 | 代表物种 | 光学行为 | 气候效应 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **非吸收性（non-absorbing）气溶胶** | 硫酸盐（sulfate）、硝酸盐（nitrate）、**大多数**有机气溶胶 | **散射（scatter）**阳光回太空 | 增大地球反照率 → **冷却** |
| **吸收性（absorbing）气溶胶** | **黑碳（black carbon）**——最著名的吸收性气溶胶；某些有机气溶胶，称为**棕碳（brown carbon）** | **吸收**太阳辐射，加热周围空气 | **增暖** |

Slide 17 notes 原文：

> "Non-absorbing aerosols, like sulfate, nitrate and most of the organic aerosols, can **scatter sunlight back into space**. This **increases the Earth's albedo (reflectivity)** and has a **cooling** effect on the climate.
> Some aerosols can **absorb** solar radiation, **warm the surrounding air** and contributes to a warming climate effect. The best-known type of absorbing aerosol is **black carbon**. Certain types of organic aerosols are also absorbing, which is called **brown carbon**."

**决定直接效应大小的因素（四项，Slide 17 notes）**：

> "The overall direct effects of aerosols depend on their **size**, **composition** and **optical properties**. **Their location in the atmosphere** also plays a crucial role."

**"位置"这一项的具体例子——非常容易考的一个细节**：

> "For example, if an **elevated absorbing aerosol layer is above low-lying clouds**, it will have a **stronger warming effect** compared to being over a **clear sky**. This is because the absorbing aerosol layers can **also absorb the solar radiation reflected by the underlying clouds**."

**为什么**：低云下方是明亮的反射面，阳光被云反射上来后**又一次**穿过上方的吸收性气溶胶层，等于给这层气溶胶提供了"两次"吸收机会；而在晴空（暗地表）上方，只有下行的那一次。所以**同一层黑碳，放在低云之上比放在晴空之上更增暖**。

**怎么用。** 在 Workshop 1 里，"直接效应"必须被列为**必须模拟的机制之一**（答案 deck 明确要求）。

**讲师口头补充的机制图解读**（转写）：

> "so you have your **emission of particles or gases that can become particles**... So you have incoming solar radiation and depending on **the colour of the particles, which depend on its composition**, you get **varying degrees of absorption or reflectance**. That's the direct effect."

### 2.7 气溶胶–云相互作用（Aerosol–Cloud Interaction, ACI）＝ 间接效应

**是什么。** Slide 18 列出两个间接效应：

> - **Indirect effect: Cloud microphysics → cloud albedo effect**（云微物理 → 云反照率效应）
> - **Indirect effect: The cloud lifetime effect**（云寿命效应）
> - 关键角色：**Cloud Condensation nuclei (CCN)（云凝结核）**

#### （1）前置事实：云对地球辐射收支的重要性

Slide 18 notes：

> "Clouds cover roughly **two-thirds of the Earth's surface**. They can modify Earth's **top of the atmosphere (TOA)** radiation budget by **reflecting incoming solar radiation, thereby enhancing the earth's albedo**."

讲师口头呼应 Week 3："You talked about albedo last week, right, when you were doing the energy budget of the Earth. Yeah, **clouds are a big part of Earth's albedo**."

#### （2）云凝结核（CCN）——这条链的起点

讲师的口头讲解（比讲义清楚）：

> "key to this is the knowledge that **to make a cloud in Earth's atmosphere, we need these particulates to act as cloud condensation nuclei**, and these things **occur naturally**. But if you have human perturbation, you increase the amount of particulate matter, **you're increasing the amount of cloud condensation nuclei**."

→ **关键点：云不能凭空形成，必须有 CCN。CCN 本来就天然存在；人为排放做的事是"增加 CCN 数量"，而不是"从无到有创造云"。**

**什么样的气溶胶是好 CCN？** Slide 18 notes：

> "The aerosol ability to become a CCN is dependent on its **hygroscopic nature（吸湿性）**, which is affected by aerosol **size** and **composition**. **Larger aerosol particles are generally more efficient CCN.** **Inorganic aerosols like sulfate and nitrate are generally more hygroscopic than carbonaceous aerosols.**"

讲师口头补了一个讲义没有的细节——**存在一个"尺寸甜点"**：

> "you need to be **above a certain size** to form a cloud d[roplet], but **you don't want to be too big**. There's a **sweet spot in size**."

（转写此处 ASR 把 "cloud droplet" 听成了 "cloud chocolate"，把 "hygroscopicity" 听成了 "high gross capacity"，把 "condensation nuclei" 听成了 "conversation nuclear"——见勘误表。）

📎 背景补充：**这个"尺寸甜点"的物理解释是 Köhler 理论——过小的颗粒因曲率效应（Kelvin effect）需要极高的过饱和度才能活化，而过大的颗粒虽然容易活化但数浓度低、且很快因重力沉降或碰并成雨滴而流失。"Köhler 理论"这个名词与解释材料中没有出现，属笔记补充。**

#### （3）云反照率效应（cloud albedo effect）

Slide 18 notes：

> "**More aerosols or CCN typically lead to more cloud droplets with smaller droplet size.** In this way, **clouds become more reflective** and enhance their cooling effect."

讲师口头版：

> "If you increase the concentration through increased emission, then what you're doing is **increasing the CCN number, which increases the droplet number inside the cloud. And that increases the reflectivity of the cloud or its albedo**."

**推理链**：

> 排放 ↑ → CCN 数浓度 ↑ → 在**同样的水汽总量**下，云滴数目 ↑ 且**每个云滴更小** → 云的总散射截面 ↑ → **云反照率 ↑ → 冷却**

📎 背景补充：**这个效应在文献中通常称为 Twomey 效应（Twomey effect）或"第一间接效应"。这个名字材料中没有出现。**

#### （4）云寿命效应（cloud lifetime effect）

Slide 18 notes：

> "The **smaller size of cloud droplets** also **increases their time remaining in the cloud, suppressing the precipitation**. This can **extend cloud lifetime**, further enhancing their cooling effect."

讲师口头版（更清楚地点出"水汽总量不变"这个前提）：

> "If you have **more CCN, the same amount of water vapor, you have smaller droplets**. They're **less likely to precipitate out** at any one time and so **the cloud lives for longer**. If the cloud lives for longer, **there's more coverage by clouds, the planetary albedo increases**."

**推理链**：

> CCN ↑ → 云滴更小 → 更难碰并长大到降水尺度 → 降水被抑制 → **云寿命 ↑ → 云量/云覆盖 ↑ → 行星反照率 ↑ → 冷却**

📎 背景补充：**这一效应在文献中常称 Albrecht 效应（Albrecht effect）或"第二间接效应"。名称材料中没有出现。**

#### （5）半直接效应（semi-direct effect）

**只出现在 Slide 18 的 speaker notes 里**，讲师在课堂上讲 ERF 时也间接提到了，方向与前两个间接效应**相反**：

> "The **rapid adjustments to atmospheric thermodynamics due to aerosols** is commonly referred as **'semi-direct effect'**. This can also affect cloud amounts. For example, when **absorbing aerosols, such as black carbon, immersed in or surround cloud droplets, they can absorb light, heat the surrounding air and evaporate clouds, thus reducing cloud cooling effect**."

**推理链**：

> 吸收性气溶胶（黑碳）在云中/云周 → 吸收阳光 → 加热周围空气 → **云滴蒸发、云被"烧掉"** → 云量 ↓ → 云的冷却作用 ↓ → **净增暖**

#### （6）"到底有几个机制？"——讲师的明确收束

这是转写里一句非常适合直接背下来的话：

> "So we talked about **three effects** there. On the previous slide, we talked about the **direct effect**, direct interaction with radiation. This one we talked about the **cloud albedo effect** and the **cloud lifetime effect**. **And that's where it stops. There's three mechanisms by which aerosol affect radiative forcing.**"

**所以标准答案的"三机制"是**：

1. **直接效应**（aerosol–radiation interaction）
2. **云反照率效应**（cloud albedo effect，属 aerosol–cloud interaction）
3. **云寿命效应**（cloud lifetime effect，属 aerosol–cloud interaction）

**边界说明**：半直接效应在讲义 notes 中被归入"快速调整"而非独立的第四机制，讲师课堂上也是把它当作 ERF 里"rapid adjustment"的一个例子来讲的。写答案时按"三机制"作答，可以把半直接效应作为"快速调整中的一项"补充提到。

### 2.8 气溶胶的 ERF 数值与不确定性

#### （1）核心数表（Slide 19 与 Slide 20 重复给出的同一张表）

**Anthropogenic ERF over the industrial era (1750–2014)**

| 效应 | 有效辐射强迫 ERF（W m⁻²） | 不确定性（W m⁻²）与信度 | 出处 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 气溶胶–辐射相互作用（Aerosol–radiation interaction） | **−0.3** | ±0.3（medium confidence） | Figure 7.5 及 AR6 WG1 第 955 页支撑文字 |
| 气溶胶–云相互作用（Aerosol–cloud interaction） | **−1.0** | ±0.7（medium confidence） | 同上 |

Slide 19 notes 的文字复述：

> "the global average ERF for aerosol-radiation interaction and aerosol-cloud interaction for **2014 relative to 1750** are assessed to be **–0.3 W m⁻²** and **–1.0 W m⁻²**, respectively. **The total aerosol ERF is assessed to be –1.3 W m⁻².** There is **strong evidence for a substantial global cooling effect of anthropogenic aerosols**."

#### （2）"简单的机制不是最重要的那个"——讲师的关键评论

> "It might be nice if the **really simple mechanism was the one that was most important**, because then we could forget about the more complicated cloud mechanisms. **That is the cloud mechanism that matters the most.**"

→ **考点：气溶胶–云相互作用（−1.0）的量级约为气溶胶–辐射相互作用（−0.3）的三倍多。最复杂、最不确定的那个机制恰恰是最重要的那个。**

（转写此处 ASR 把 −0.3 听成了 "minus 4.3"，把两个机制的名字也说反了一次——见勘误表。以**讲义表格**为准。）

#### （3）不确定性来源

Slide 20 原文：

> "Uncertainties are from **properties of aerosol: composition, abundance, number-size distribution, hygroscopicity**, which in turn affects aerosol-radiation and aerosol-cloud effects."

Slide 20 notes 补充了三条：

> 1. "aerosol are **short-lived species and unevenly distributed**. It is challenging to capture **spatial and temporal variations** in aerosol concentrations accurately."
> 2. "aerosol interaction with clouds and radiation are dependent on aerosol **physicochemical properties**. However, **characterizing these properties across different environments remains uncertain**."
> 3. "There are also **limitations with existing observational and modelling techniques**."

**讲师逐项口头拆解这四个性质（转写独有，讲得比讲义细）**：

- **组成（composition）**："what the aerosol actually made of... because that determines other properties like the direct effect and like the indirect effect."
- **丰度/排放量（abundance）**："there's still actually quite large uncertainties around **how much is being emitted in a particular area at particular times**"（他举的例子正是英国的燃木炉——ASR 记为 "the good learning stage in the UK"，见勘误表）。
- **数–粒径分布（number-size distribution）**——讲师定义得很清楚："for a given size of particle... what's its **number concentration**. And that's really important because **the size of the particle depends how good it is at scattering radiation**, where scattering involves reflection back into space as well. Number size distribution **also determines the effect on clouds** — you need to be above a certain size to form a cloud droplet, but you don't want to be too big; there's a **sweet spot** in size."
  → **一个性质同时控制直接效应（散射效率）与间接效应（CCN 活化）**，这是它特别重要的原因。
- **吸湿性（hygroscopicity）**："which relates to composition, so that's the **affinity of the particulate matter to water** and therefore its **ability to act as a cloud condensation nucleus**."

**讲师对"为什么这件事值钱"的口头评论**：

> "There's some ra[ther] large uncertainties around these things, **which is why millions of pounds of public money gets spent on research** to reduce those uncertainties so that we know much more certainly what the effect of aerosol is on radiative forcing."

以及在最后总结那张对比图时：

> "the black lines and whiskers on these plots represent the uncertainty and **it is dominated by uncertainty around the aerosols**... so the properties of aerosol we've talked about today are **dominating the minds of scientists and policy makers and politicians around the globe**."

→ **这是整周最重要的"元结论"之一：在 IPCC 的人为强迫柱状图上，误差棒最长的一项就是气溶胶。**

### 2.9 气溶胶强迫的时空演变

#### （1）时间与区域演变（Slide 21，出处 AR6 WG1 第 853 页）

Slide 21 notes：

> "Here is the **temporal evolution of global and regional aerosol ERF**. We can see, **until the late 20th century, ERF values indicate an increasing cooling effect** of anthropogenic aerosols. This trend aligns with **rapid industrialization and growing aerosol emissions**. However, as **emission regulations and air quality policies** have been implemented in many regions, **the aerosol cooling effect reached a peak and started to decline**."

**讲师的口头解读（讲义完全没有的历史背景）**：

> "this amazing plot shows the radi[ative] forcing on the vertical axis and the [time] on the x axis, and it shows that things are very [different] in different regions with time. That **rather large peak in 1975 is the European effect** — so that would have coincided with **Europe having particularly heavy industry and dirty cars**. It would have been around the same time as the **Nordic countries were worried about acid rain from the UK**, because of a lot of **coal burning in the UK for electricity generation releasing a lot of sulphur dioxide**. But that picture that you get from the UK of a lot of dirty emissions combined with the **dense population**... [spread] across Europe. But then the **big decrease** in terms of the absolute magnitude of that forcing from Europe — **a trend that's seen in many regions**."

**要读出的结论**：欧洲气溶胶强迫在 **1975 年前后**达到峰值（冷却最强），随后因空气质量政策而**绝对值下降**；这个"先升后降"的形状在许多区域重复出现。

> "I want to leave you with a **take-away question**: The most recent IPCC report we just presented provides valid aerosol ERF **until 2014**. **What do you think the trend of aerosol ERF will be in the future? Will it be continuously decreasing cooling effect or else?**"

（这道题材料未给答案。但下一张幻灯片的 Samset et al. 2025 实际上就是对它的一种回答。）

#### （3）东亚气溶胶减排实验（Slide 22，Samset et al. 2025，Fig. 2，doi.org/10.1038/s43247-025-02527-3）

讲义原文只有一句：

> "**Baseline simulations are compared against perturbation simulations** to quantify effect of changes such as **East Asian aerosol emission reduction**."

**真正的内容全在转写里**，这是本周最典型的"客座讲者带来的一手研究"（详见第四节 4.6）。讲师的讲解要点：

- **方法论——基线 vs 扰动实验（baseline vs perturbation experiment）**：
  > "one scenario you call a **baseline**, it's the '**if nothing changes**' scenario, and the other is a **perturbation** scenario... in the perturbation one you say 'oh but what if this changes', and then you can have a perturbation result [minus] a baseline result to **get out the effect of a particular driver**."
- **被扰动的量**：东亚的气溶胶排放。"a region that's gone through rapid growth, a lot of it powered by relatively dirty emissions from coal burning, but... **in the last 20 years emissions have reduced significantly**."
- **结果（全球）**：**2020–2050 年间约 +0.1 °C 的预估增暖**，纯粹因为气溶胶减排导致冷却作用消失。
  > "that has given an estimated warming of 0.1 degrees C from these simulations — well, a **projected warming of 0.1 degrees C between 2020 and 2050**, all because emissions have reduced, so we're no longer putting things into the atmosphere and therefore we're **losing the cooling effect**."
- **子图 (b) — 模式间离散度**：
  > "in figure subplot B here are results from **different [climate] models**. They give you an indication of the sort of **disagreement and the uncertainty** that we have. **In fact there's one model that gives a negative result**... whereas the **majority** of those models give a warming effect when you reduce aerosol."
  → **要读出的结论：绝大多数模式给出"减排气溶胶 → 增暖"，但存在个别模式给出相反符号。这就是"不要只信一个模式"的实证依据。**
- **子图 (c)、(d) — 区域分布，坐标为地表温度距平（surface temperature anomaly）**：
  > "you have this really **intense red area of East Asia** where the actual emission has reduced itself, so you've got this really **local effect**... you get **half a degree** change of a warmer change in the area where the emission actually occurs. But you also see that that **continues across the Northern Pacific and up into Alaska and Siberia** too — these are regions where the aerosol, because of the **dominant meteorology**, that's where it tends to get taken, and therefore it has an effect."
  → **要读出的结论：① 减排地本身约 +0.5 °C 的局地增暖；② 影响沿盛行气象输送路径向下风向（北太平洋 → 阿拉斯加、西伯利亚）延伸。这正是"寿命短 → 区域性效应"那条链的可视化证据。**
- **不只东亚**：
  > "it's **not limited to East Asia**... **Europe's emission [reduction] as well**, and we continue to see it through things like the **reduction of sulfur emissions from shipping**, which has occurred **in the last five years globally**, but affects Europe because of the **large shipping routes around Western Europe and the English Channel**."

📎 背景补充：**讲师所说的"近五年全球航运硫排放削减"，通常指国际海事组织（IMO）自 2020 年起将船用燃油含硫量上限从 3.5% 降至 0.5% 的规定（业内称 IMO 2020）。这个法规名称与数值材料中没有出现，属笔记补充。**

### 2.10 气溶胶的权衡（trade-off）：空气质量 vs 气候

**是什么。** Slide 21 一句话：

> **Aerosol reduction offers trade-off for climate and air pollution.**

**讲师的完整口头论证（讲义没有）**：

> "in some ways this is a **good news story** because of the **air quality** story — by reducing the particulate matter that's being emitted mostly [near] the surface, you're **reducing people's exposure** to it. In terms of **climate**, we've got a bit of a **trade off** situation, because generally **for aerosol, reduction is good for air quality but bad for climate**."

**为什么**（讲师让学生自己推）：

> "you can already work out in your heads, because we've talked about the **overall cooling effect of aerosols** and how it's **offsetting some of the warming effect of greenhouse gases**, and it only lives for **3 days** — so if you start to lose it, and you lose it **quickly** compared to longer[-lived] greenhouse gases, then you start to lose that **compensation effect**, and so you might expect a **warming** effect because of that. **And that's exactly what's been observed.**"

**完整推理链（务必能自己复述）**：

> 气溶胶净冷却（−1.3 W m⁻²）→ 长期以来**掩盖/抵消**了一部分温室气体的增暖 →
> 气溶胶寿命只有 ~3 天，而 CO₂ 寿命是百年尺度 →
> 一旦停止排放，气溶胶在**几天到几周内**就从大气中消失，而 CO₂ 仍在 →
> **抵消项瞬间消失，增暖项还在** → **净增暖显现（"unmasking"）**

**与臭氧对比（这是本周一个漂亮的对称结构）**：

- **气溶胶**：减排 → 空气质量改善 ✅ / 气候恶化 ❌ → **trade-off（权衡）**
- **臭氧**：减排（削减前体物）→ 空气质量改善 ✅ / 气候改善 ✅ → **co-benefit（协同效益）**（Slide 28 明写 "Ozone offers **co-benefits** for climate and air pollution"）

### 2.11 臭氧的两个"住址"：平流层与对流层

**是什么。** Slide 10：

> - **Ozone (O₃) is present in both the stratosphere and troposphere.**
> - **Human release of ozone-depleting-substances** has acted to **decrease** ozone concentrations in the **stratosphere at poles in winter**.
> - **Human release of ozone precursors** has acted to **increase** ozone concentrations in the **troposphere**.

Slide 11（图，经 OCR 识别，原文照录）：

**关键的反直觉之处（讲师反复强调）**：

And actually **it's the tropospheric ozone that we're going to focus on today**... **And that's despite only 10% by mass of ozone being in the troposphere** and the majority in the stratosphere. **Despite that minor proportion in the troposphere, it's actually going to be the focus of today's talk.**"

**为什么"只占 10% 的那一部分"反而是重点？** Slide 25 给了硬数据：

→ **对流层臭氧的正强迫约为平流层臭氧强迫的 20 倍。** 记住这个"20×"。

**为什么会这样？** 讲师的解释：

So these things that make ozone, and they tend to **concentrate in the troposphere relative to the stratosphere**. And so that's why increase in ozone due to those increase in precursor emissions is going to be the focus."

→ **人类排放的是"前体物"，而前体物排放在地面附近、集中在对流层，所以人为增量主要发生在对流层。**

### 2.12 对流层臭氧的化学生成机制

**是什么。** Slide 25 给出两步反应（原文照录）：

> **hv**
> **NO₂ → NO + O(³P)**
> **O(³P) + O₂ → O₃**

以及一条关键的循环条件：

> "**NO tends to convert to NO₂ in the presence of CO, CH₄, VOCs.**
> Therefore, **the precursors for O₃ are: NOₓ, CO, CH₄, VOCs**."

Slide 25 的 speaker notes 一句话点题：

> "**Vast majority of ozone production by humans is secondary**, i.e. humans release chemicals that are **not ozone**, but those chemicals can **produce ozone once in the atmosphere**."

**讲师的口头讲解（把两步串成一条循环）**：

> "it's a **secondary** [pollutant], it's quite rarely [emitted] directly, instead it has **precursors** which make it in the gas phase, and the key one is **nitrogen oxides, specifically NO₂**, [which] will **photolyse** — that means interact with incoming s[unlight]... The NO₂ will **dissociate** [giving] a single oxygen atom, which can react with the **abundant supply of [oxygen] in [the] troposphere** to give you **ozone**.
> And then **anything that helps you to convert from NO back to NO₂ will help to generate ozone**... **organics** are quite good at it — so methane, carbon [monoxide] and **VOCs**, which means **volatile organic compounds**. So anything that's in the gas phase will help you to generate ozone **if you've got nitrogen oxides also present**. So it's a **combination of nitrogen oxides and organics** that helps generate ozone in the troposphere."

**机制整理（这是本周最需要能默写的一条链）**：

```
第 1 步（光解）：  NO₂ + hv  →  NO + O(³P)
第 2 步（成臭氧）： O(³P) + O₂ →  O₃
第 3 步（再生 NO₂）：NO + [CO / CH₄ / VOCs 的氧化产物] → NO₂
                     ↑____________循环回第 1 步____________|
```

**为什么必须有第 3 步**：📎 背景补充：**如果没有 CO/CH₄/VOCs，生成的 NO 会直接把刚生成的 O₃ 吃掉（NO + O₃ → NO₂ + O₂），形成一个零净产量的空循环；正是 CO/CH₄/VOCs 提供了另一条把 NO 氧化回 NO₂ 的路径（通过 HO₂/RO₂ 自由基），才使 O₃ 得以净积累。这个"空循环"与自由基路径的解释材料中没有明写，属笔记补充——但它正是讲义"NO tends to convert to NO₂ in the presence of CO, CH₄, VOCs"这句话背后的道理。**

**前体物的人为来源（讲师口头，讲义只有笼统列表）**：

- **氮氧化物（NOₓ）**：
  > "nitrogen oxides [are] generate[d] when we **burn something at a sufficiently high temperature** — so it will happen inside the **internal combustion engine**, the car; **coal combustion for electricity**; even **industrial boiler units** will generate nitrogen oxide. **Though actually most of your domestic boilers, they don't work at a high enough temperature to generate much of it.**"
  → **关键判据：NOₓ 的生成需要"足够高的燃烧温度"。家用锅炉温度不够高，因此不是主要 NOₓ 源——这是一个讲义上完全没有、非常适合当细节考点的对照。**
- **有机物（organics / VOCs）**：
  > "**non-methane organics that are volatile** come from all sorts of things, a lot of sources. **In affluent countries [they] are from solvents and solvent use** — that's where we get a lot of organics from."
  → 讲师还补充说 Slide 26 的来源图标里"**homes should be on there as well**, because that's where a lot of our **solvent application** actually comes from"——**这是讲师对自己讲义的一处口头补充/更正。**

Slide 26 讲义列出的来源为：agriculture（农业）、extraction of fossil fuels（化石燃料开采）、chemical engineering（化学工业）、transport（交通）。

### 2.13 臭氧的辐射强迫

**是什么。** Slide 27 是本周关于臭氧的核心幻灯片，逐条拆：

> - **Ozone is able to absorb both incoming ultraviolet radiation AND outgoing infrared radiation. Both absorption bands mean that increased tropospheric ozone has a positive effective radiative forcing (warming).**
> - **Third most important greenhouse gas** behind methane and carbon dioxide.
> - **Shorter lived** than methane and carbon dioxide (**average lifetime in atmosphere of 3 weeks**).
> - **The magnitude of its ERF is around half that of aerosol and around double that of land-use change.**

**（1）"双吸收带"是臭氧的独门特征**

臭氧同时吸收**入射紫外（UV）**和**出射红外（IR）**。两条吸收带都存在，意味着对流层臭氧增加会产生**正的**有效辐射强迫。

答案 deck Q2 的表述方式略有不同，也要会：

> "Ozone affects radiative forcing by absorbing **short- and long-wave** radiation, thereby **reducing the radiation flux returned to space from Earth** when concentrations increase."

**（2）寿命 3 周 → 不能well-mixed**

Slide 26：

> "Ozone has an **average atmospheric lifetime of 3 weeks**, so it is **not well mixed** throughout the atmosphere."

讲师口头版：

> "it's got an average lifetime of **three weeks** in the atmosphere, which means it's **not well mixed throughout the troposphere**, and therefore its **radiative forcing and its air pollution effects are regional or local**."

**（3）人为影响的方向与原因**

Slide 26：

> - **Anthropogenic influence on atmospheric ozone has caused warming.**
> - "Warming results from the **increased atmospheric concentrations of ozone precursors (NOₓ, CO, CH₄, VOCs)** due to anthropogenic emission."

**（4）数值**

答案 deck Q2 给出：**臭氧自 1750 年以来的辐射强迫为 +0.5 W m⁻²**，"though **4 times less than carbon dioxide**"（约为 CO₂ 的四分之一）。

讲师转写中的对应说法：

> "according to that plot it's **the third most important greenhouse gas** by its magnitude, **behind carbon dioxide and behind methane**. About **four times less** effective radiative forcing than carbon dioxide. **You need to know that relative effect, comparing it to carbon dioxide.**"

📎 背景补充（推算）：**由答案 deck 给的 +0.5 W m⁻² 与"约为 CO₂ 的四分之一"可反推，材料所指的 CO₂ ERF 约为 2 W m⁻² 量级。这个 CO₂ 数值材料中没有直接给出，是笔记的推算，不要当作材料数据引用。**

**（5）一个诱人但站不住的政策想法（讲师的口头思想实验，讲义没有）**

> "but it's **short lived**, so in theory, if you were a **politician**, then you might say 'ah, let's get rid of the ozone', because after three weeks [it goes away] by itself, whereas we can't [take] up carbon dioxide — it would take hundreds of years. **But unfortunately those nitrogen oxides and those organics are so important to humans and what we do.** Moreover, the [fact that it's a] result of something really important to humans **doesn't mean we shouldn't try**."

→ **这段话既是一个记忆钩子（短寿命 = 见效快），也是一个现实约束（前体物与经济活动深度绑定）。**

### 2.14 臭氧：总量效应 vs 仅平流层效应（Slide 28，Skeie et al. 2020, Fig. 4）

讲义 Slide 28 原文（碎片式）：

> - "Complicated picture, but **all models agree that effect from tropospheric gain in ozone dominates over changes to stratospheric ozone**."
> - "**RF from total ozone change**" / "**RF from stratospheric ozone change**"
> - "**Ozone offers co-benefits for climate and air pollution.**"

**讲师的口头图解（这是理解这张图的唯一途径，讲义几乎没写）**：

> "the **three lines at the bottom** there, the **yellow and a couple of reds**, are the **radiative forcing from stratospheric ozone**.
> And **before the 1970s there was actually an increase in stratospheric ozone**, because some of the precursors from the troposphere [were] able to **penetrate and reach the stratosphere**.
> **1970s onwards, with the introduction of chlorofluorocarbons**, so you have **depletion of ozone in the stratosphere**, and that leads to a **cooling** effect — **but it is small compared to the warming effect in the troposphere**.
> I put it up there just to demonstrate because otherwise it can be confusing about which way things are going. So just to be really clear: **overall the effect is positive, and it's because it's dominated by the increase in ozone in the troposphere**."

**这张图要读出的三个结论**：

1. **平流层臭氧强迫在 1970 年代前后转向**：1970s 之前因对流层前体物上传而**增加**（正强迫），1970s 之后因 CFCs 引入而**耗损**（负强迫、冷却）。
2. **平流层的负强迫远小于对流层的正强迫**（呼应 Slide 25 的"20×"）。
3. **净效应为正（增暖）**，且**所有模式在这一点上一致**。

**为什么臭氧是 co-benefit 而不是 trade-off**：削减 NOₓ/VOCs → 对流层臭氧 ↓ → ① 光化学烟雾减轻（空气质量改善）② 正强迫减小（气候改善）。两个方向一致，因此是**协同效益**。

### 2.15 土地利用变化（Land-Use Change, LUC）：定义与归属规则

**是什么。** Slide 12：

> - **Human perturbation of land cover**（人类对地表覆被的扰动）。
> - **Note: emissions of carbon dioxide and methane due to agriculture are included under the radiative forcing of these gases rather than under land-use change (as reported by IPCC).**
> - **Affects radiative forcing and also vertical distribution of energy.**

**讲师对定义的口头补充**：

> "Maybe kind of like a strange title somehow, but it's **consistent with what you'll see in the IPCC report**, and it's the idea of **human perturbation to land such that the colour of that land changes from its natural original state**."

→ **一个非常好用的定义：土地利用变化 = 人类扰动使土地的"颜色"偏离其自然原始状态。**"颜色"直接指向反照率。

**归属规则（accounting rule）——这是一个高频易错点**：

> "**a lot of that change is because of agriculture**... **But the radiative forcing of carbon dioxide and methane is attributed to those molecules specifically rather than to land use change.** Land use change is going to be about **the change in cover** because of agriculture, whereas any **greenhouse gases from agriculture, the radiative forcing of those is attributed to the molecules themselves**, carbon dioxide and methane."

**记忆口诀（笔记整理）**：

> **土地利用变化这一栏，只记"地表颜色变了"的账；农业排放的 CO₂ 和 CH₄ 的账，记在 CO₂ 和 CH₄ 自己名下。**
> 否则会**重复计算（double counting）**。

**哪一类土地转换最重要？** Slide 29 的 speaker notes：

> "The perturbation of natural land cover by humans is **global**. **By surface area** (and therefore most influential on radiative forcing with regards to albedo and cloud cover), **the most important conversion is natural land to agriculture**, with **minor effects from conversion to other human uses like cities**."

讲师课堂上专门回应了这个常见提问（转写独有）：

> "a lot of people asked about **towns and cities** — relatively speaking, their land [area] could be **very small compared to agriculture**, and they're important **emitters of greenhouse gases**, but **in terms of albedo, cities are quite ineffective**. But **land use change because of agriculture is important**."

→ **考点：城市化对反照率的贡献小（面积小），农业扩张才是主角。城市重要在温室气体排放，但那笔账记在气体名下。**

**两个具体案例（Slide 29 配图 + 讲师课堂问答）**：

> "two pictures — does anyone want to guess where the left hand picture is from... **that is Nepal**. And the right hand picture... **that is the Lake District UK**. **Contrasting geographies** in terms of location, but **very similar issues in terms of land use change**. So whereas **both of these pictures would have been dominated by forests 300 years ago, now they are dominated by agriculture** — and that is land use change."

→ **英国湖区（Lake District）这个例子特别值得记：它看起来"自然风景优美"，实际上是三百年前森林被农业（放牧）取代后的人为景观。**

**分析框架（Slide 29 notes）**：

> - "For change to **radiative forcing** we must consider **the original and new cover type**. In particular, **the height of the vegetation structure, i.e. trees or no trees**."
> - "For change to **vertical distribution of energy** we must consider the change to **canopy evaporation, ground evaporation and transpiration**."

**寿命（lifetime of land-use change）**——Slide 29 提出这个问题，答案在转写里：

> "the **lifetime of land use change is essentially as long as we need agriculture**, and [there's] no sign of that [abating] anytime soon. **That's not changing very quickly.**"

答案 deck Q2 的表述：

> "The effect of land-use change on radiative forcing will **continue so long as demand for agriculture continues**."

→ **对比三者寿命：气溶胶 ~3 天，臭氧 ~3 周，土地利用变化 ~"只要人类还需要农业"（实质上是几十到几百年尺度，且不会自行消退）。这个对比是考点。**

### 2.16 土地利用变化的辐射强迫机制：反照率

**是什么。** Slide 29：

> **Affects radiative forcing, mostly through albedo change.**（主要通过反照率变化影响辐射强迫。）

Slide 30 给出**两种典型情形**：

> - "Changing **tree and shrub cover to farmland in semi-arid regions** reveals **soil that is brighter than leaves** → **increases albedo**."
> - "Changing **tree cover to farmland at higher latitudes** reveals **more snow-covered ground** → **increases albedo**."

**讲师的口头图解（把物理讲透了，讲义只有两行字）**：

**情形一：高纬度雪地（左图，鸟瞰有林区）**

> "it's a **bird's eye view of a wooded region** where the tree cover is stronger in some sections than others. **The snow falls in these high latitude regions during the winter — that's always going to happen** — but **the effectiveness of that snow in reflecting incoming visible radiation depends on the tree cover**. As you can see, **where the tree cover is greater, the light doesn't see the snow** — your eyes can't see the snow. **Where the trees have gone, you can see the snow, and the visible light can also see that.** [Where] the visible light strikes the tree... where there is relatively low snow, or no [snow visible], **it gets absorbed, less reflected**. So [lower] albedo there... but **that happens over vast regions of snowy areas, at least in Canada**."

**核心物理（务必理解）**：**雪一直都在下，森林砍不砍都在下。关键不是"有没有雪"，而是"阳光看不看得见雪"——树冠遮蔽（masking）了下面的雪。移除树木 = 揭开遮蔽 = 雪的反射得以生效 = 反照率上升 = 冷却。**

> 讲师的诊断语句很妙："**your eyes can't see the snow... the visible light can also see that**"——**用"眼睛能不能看见雪"来判断反照率变化，是一个可以直接用在考卷上的直觉工具。**

**情形二：半干旱区土壤（右图）**

> "the pictures on the right are of **semi-arid regions**... you can see the [individual] trees and you can track the tree cover... [contrast] with the **dry soil**, and you can see that **the soil is brighter**. **The soil is always there**, and now the **visible light can reach the soil because the trees and shrubs have been removed**, and it can have [an] influence. And then from a **satellite** point of view, you can see that **where there is dry soil you have a higher reflectivity than where you have forest cover**."

**核心物理**：与情形一结构完全相同——**明亮的下垫面（干燥土壤）一直都在，只是被更暗的植被遮住了；移除植被就把亮面暴露出来，反照率上升。**

**讲师的一句重要澄清（防止误读）**：

> "**I'm not saying this [is] Harrowing Desert** [荒漠化]. **This is a result of land use change by humans.** I'm just giving you a demonstration of how **albedo changes through changing surface type**."

→ **不要把半干旱区的这张图误解为"荒漠化"的证据；它演示的是"地表类型改变 → 反照率改变"这一物理过程。**

**共同结构（笔记整理）**：

> 两种情形都是同一个模式：**"更亮的下垫面（雪 / 干土）本来就在那里，被更暗的植被遮蔽；人类移除植被 → 暴露亮面 → 反照率 ↑ → 负辐射强迫（冷却）"**。
> 区别只在于**亮面是什么**：高纬度是**雪**，半干旱区是**土壤**。

### 2.17 土地利用变化对能量垂直分布的影响（不是辐射强迫！）

Slide 33 和 Slide 34 都用了同一个副标题，且是**加粗强调的**：

> ### **Affects vertical distribution of energy – not a radiative forcing**

**这是本周最容易搞错的一个边界，讲师强调了至少三次。**

**是什么。** Slide 33：

> - "**Solar energy incident on surface is converted to sensible and latent heat.**"（入射地表的太阳能被转化为**显热（sensible heat）**与**潜热（latent heat）**。）
> - "Can affect **local and regional climate** significantly."
> - "**More liquid water available means lower sensible heat : latent heat.**"
> - "**Less liquid water available means higher sensible heat : latent heat.**"

**讲师的口头机制讲解**：

> "it's **not a radiative forcing**... it **didn't have an instantaneous effect or even a rapid adjustment**, **but it does affect local climate**.
> And that's because **if you remove vegetation cover then you remove the evapotranspiration effect**, which is where **sun's energy transforms into latent heat and water vapour, which then rises through the atmosphere and can be released high in the atmosphere** to [form] clouds.
> But if you get really [reduced] evapotranspiration in places with **dry fields** for example, then **the sun's energy stays as sensible heat**, **doesn't get transported as well**, **so you have warm[ing] low down** compared to if you had that original vegetation cover. And that can really affect local climate."

**完整机制链（笔记整理）**：

```
有植被：太阳能 → 蒸散（evapotranspiration）→ 潜热 → 随水汽上升 → 在高空凝结时释放
       ⇒ 能量被"搬运"到高空，地表相对凉爽

无植被（干燥农田）：太阳能 → 无法蒸发 → 只能变成显热 → 留在近地面
       ⇒ 能量"堵"在低层，地表升温
```

**为什么这不算辐射强迫？**（这是理解的关键）

> 因为它**没有改变对流层顶处的净辐照度**。它只是把**同样多的能量**在垂直方向上**重新分配**了——从"上传到高空"变成"堆在近地面"。进出地球的辐射总量不变，所以按 RF 的定义（对流层顶的净辐照度变化），它**不构成辐射强迫**；它甚至不算 ERF 里的"快速调整"，因为它不是通过改变云/水汽来改变 TOA 通量的机制。

**边界条件**：这个效应是**局地/区域**的，不是全球平均的。但它可以很大——见下节。

## 三、公式与图表

### 3.1 公式与定量关系

本周**没有需要代入计算的封闭形式公式**（不像 Week 3 的 Stefan–Boltzmann 定律）。真正需要掌握的是**定义式、反应式与量级关系**。逐一列出。

#### 公式 1：辐射强迫（Radiative Forcing）的定义式

$$\text{RF} = \Delta(\text{net irradiance})\big|_{\text{tropopause}} = \Delta\left(F_{\downarrow,\text{solar}} - F_{\uparrow,\text{terrestrial}}\right)$$

**（此处的符号化写法为笔记整理形式，讲义只给了文字定义。）**

| 符号 | 含义 | 单位 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| RF | 辐射强迫 | **W m⁻²** |
| $F_{\downarrow,\text{solar}}$ | 入射太阳辐射通量（incoming solar radiation） | W m⁻² |
| $F_{\uparrow,\text{terrestrial}}$ | 出射地球辐射通量（outgoing earth's radiation） | W m⁻² |
| 评估位置 | **对流层顶（tropopause）** | — |

- **适用条件**：定义在对流层顶；正值 = 增暖，负值 = 冷却。
- **来源**：讲义 Slide 13，答案 deck Q2 逐字复述。
- **典型数值**：本周涉及的所有 RF/ERF 都在 **0.1 – 2 W m⁻²** 量级。

#### 公式 2：有效辐射强迫（ERF）的构成式

$$\text{ERF} = \underbrace{\text{Instantaneous forcing}}_{\text{瞬时能量收支变化}} + \underbrace{\text{Rapid adjustments}}_{\text{与地表温度变化不耦合的快速大气调整}}$$

| 项 | 含义 | 时间尺度 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 瞬时强迫 | 驱动因子刚出现时对能量收支的直接改变（如黑碳一排放就开始吸收太阳辐射） | 瞬时 |
| 快速调整 | 云、水汽等的快速变化，**与全球地表气温变化不耦合** | **天到月**（Slide 19 notes） |

- **单位**：W m⁻²
- **基准期**：AR6 中为 **1750–2014**
- **来源**：讲义 Slide 19 及其 notes；讲师用黑碳做了口头示例。
- **易错**：快速调整 ≠ 反馈（feedback）。反馈是经由地表温度变化产生的，不算进 ERF。

#### 公式 3：辐射强迫的能量平衡代数示例（Slide 13）

$$
\begin{aligned}
&\text{初始：} && E_{\text{in}} = A,\quad E_{\text{out}} = A &&\Rightarrow \text{平衡} \\
&\text{施加强迫 }+C\text{：} && E_{\text{in}} = A,\quad E_{\text{out}} = A - C &&\Rightarrow \text{净吸收 } +C \\
&\text{长期（}C\text{ 恒定）：} && E_{\text{in}} = A,\quad E_{\text{out}} \to A &&\Rightarrow \text{恢复平衡，但 } T\uparrow
\end{aligned}
$$

- **符号**：A = 初始平衡时的能量通量；C = 施加的辐射强迫。
- **来源**：讲义 Slide 13 的图 + 讲师口头逐步讲解（转写）。
- **要点**：**图是"从上往下 = 时间向前"**（讲师明说 "going down the slide is going forwards in time"）。
- **结论**：强迫恒定 → 系统最终重回平衡，但停在更高的温度上。

#### 公式 4：对流层臭氧生成的化学循环（Slide 25）

$$
\begin{aligned}
&\text{(1) 光解：} && \mathrm{NO_2} + h\nu \;\longrightarrow\; \mathrm{NO} + \mathrm{O(^3P)} \\
&\text{(2) 成臭氧：} && \mathrm{O(^3P)} + \mathrm{O_2} \;\longrightarrow\; \mathrm{O_3} \\
&\text{(3) 再生（讲义文字，非配平式）：} && \mathrm{NO} \xrightarrow{\ \mathrm{CO},\ \mathrm{CH_4},\ \mathrm{VOCs}\ } \mathrm{NO_2}
\end{aligned}
$$

| 符号 | 含义 |
| --- | --- |
| $h\nu$ | 光子（光解所需的太阳辐射） |
| $\mathrm{O(^3P)}$ | 基态三重态氧原子（ground-state atomic oxygen） |
| NOₓ | 氮氧化物（NO + NO₂）的统称 |
| VOCs | 挥发性有机化合物（volatile organic compounds） |

- **适用条件**：**对流层**内的臭氧生成。需要同时存在 **NOₓ** 与 **有机物/CO**，且有阳光。
- **前体物清单（务必记全）**：**NOₓ、CO、CH₄、VOCs**（Slide 25 原文）。
- **注意**：讲师口述第 3 步时把 CO（carbon monoxide）说成了 "carbon dioxide"——**以讲义为准，是一氧化碳 CO**，见勘误表。
- **性质**：这是**二次生成（secondary）**——人类排放的不是臭氧本身，而是前体物。

  2. **主要人为来源**（图标表示）：化石燃料开采、居民源（燃木炉）、交通、农业（牛与作物 → 氨）、化学工业。

  4. **增暖还是冷却**：由**颜色**决定——黑碳吸收 → 增暖；盐类与多数有机物为白色 → 反射 → 冷却；**按质量算，多数类型是反射性的、冷却的**。
- **要读出的结论**：**气溶胶是一个"混合袋"——不同种类的符号相反，但净效应为冷却。**

#### 图 C：气溶胶–辐射相互作用示意图（Slide 17，AR6 WG1 p.844）

- **图上元素**：太阳（☀️）、**brown carbon**、**black carbon**、"salts like ammonium sulphate"、"Emission of particles, and gases that can become particle"。
- **坐标/结构**：这是一张机制示意图，不是数据图。自下而上：地表排放 → 颗粒（一次）与气体（→ 二次颗粒）→ 入射太阳辐射与颗粒相互作用 → 分叉为**散射回太空**（白色/浅色粒子）与**吸收**（黑碳、棕碳）。
- **要读出的结论**：**直接效应的符号由粒子的颜色/组成决定；此外还取决于尺度、光学性质与在大气中的高度位置。**

#### 图 D：气溶胶–云相互作用示意图（Slide 18）

- **结构**：并列两个分支，均起于底部的 "Emission of particles, and gases that can become particle"：
  - **左：云反照率效应（cloud albedo effect）**——云中的小圆圈代表 CCN/云滴。**污染云中圆圈更多、更小**；对比的清洁云圆圈更少、更大。
  - **右：云寿命效应（cloud lifetime effect）**——同样是更多更小的云滴，但强调**降水被抑制、云维持更久**。
- **要读出的结论**：**两条路径的终点相同——行星反照率上升 → 冷却。**

#### 图 E：气溶胶 ERF 的时间与区域演变（Slide 21，AR6 WG1 p.853）

- **纵轴**：辐射强迫（W m⁻²）——**负值区为冷却**。
- **横轴**：**时间**（年）。
- **曲线**：多条，分别代表**全球**与**各区域**（欧洲、东亚、北美等）。
- **形状**：先向负方向变深（冷却增强），在某个年份达到**峰值（最负）**后回升（冷却减弱）。
- **要读出的结论**：
  1. **20 世纪后期之前，冷却持续增强**——对应快速工业化与气溶胶排放增长。
  2. **欧洲的峰值出现在约 1975 年**（讲师口述），对应欧洲重工业、"脏车"、英国燃煤发电大量排放 SO₂（同期北欧国家正为来自英国的酸雨担忧）。
  3. **此后随各地空气质量政策实施，冷却效应达峰并开始下降**——这个"先升后降"在许多区域重复。
- **单位提醒**：纵轴是 **W m⁻²**，不是温度。

#### 图 F：东亚气溶胶减排的基线–扰动实验（Slide 22，Samset et al. 2025, Fig. 2）

四个子图，讲师逐一讲解：

- **(a)**（讲师未细讲）：全局结果，给出**2020–2050 年约 +0.1 °C 的预估增暖**。
- **(b)**：**不同气候模式的结果并列**。
  - **要读出**：多数模式给出**增暖**（减排气溶胶 → 失去冷却 → 变暖）；**但有一个模式给出相反符号（冷却）**。这是模式间分歧与不确定性的直观展示。
- **(c) 与 (d)**：**地图**，色标为**地表温度距平（surface temperature anomaly, °C）**。
  - **要读出**：**东亚呈现强烈的红色（约 +0.5 °C）**——正是排放实际下降的地区；红色**沿北太平洋延伸至阿拉斯加与西伯利亚**——因为盛行气象把气溶胶输送到那里。
- **总结论**：**气溶胶效应是强区域性的，而且区域格局由排放地 + 输送路径共同决定。**

#### 图 G：臭氧辐射强迫（Slide 27，Figure 2.1 d) SYR + Fig 7.6 AR6 WG1）

- **形式**：IPCC 的强迫柱状图（水平柱 + 误差棒）。
- **讲师口述定位**：臭氧"**在最常见的那张 IPCC ERF 图里，是从上往下数的第三根柱**"，即**按量级排第三的温室气体，位于 CO₂ 与 CH₄ 之后**。
- **要读出**：**臭氧柱为正（增暖），长度约为 CO₂ 柱的 1/4。**

#### 图 H：臭氧总量 vs 仅平流层的辐射强迫时间序列（Slide 28，Skeie et al. 2020, Fig. 4）

- **纵轴**：辐射强迫（W m⁻²）。
- **横轴**：时间（年）。
- **曲线组**：
  - **上方一组**：**总臭氧变化引起的 RF**（RF from total ozone change）——**为正且随时间增大**。
  - **下方三条（讲师描述为"黄色和两条红色"）**：**平流层臭氧变化引起的 RF**（RF from stratospheric ozone change）。
- **曲线形状与转折（讲师口述）**：
  - **1970 年代之前**：平流层臭氧**增加**（因为对流层的前体物有一部分能穿透进入平流层）→ 该分量为**正**。
  - **1970 年代之后**：**氯氟烃（CFCs）引入** → 平流层臭氧**耗损** → 该分量转为**负（冷却）**。
- **要读出的结论**：
  1. 平流层的负强迫**在量级上远小于**对流层的正强迫。
  2. **所有模式一致认为对流层臭氧增加的效应压过平流层变化**（Slide 28 原文）。
  3. **净效应为正（增暖）。**

#### 图 I：土地利用变化的反照率示意（Slide 30 配图）

- **左图（高纬雪区）**：鸟瞰有林区，树冠密度不均。**树冠密处看不见雪，树被移除处露出雪。**
- **右图（半干旱区，上为地面/低空视角，下为卫星视角）**：可辨认单棵树与其树冠范围；周围是**明亮的干燥土壤**；卫星视角显示**干土区反射率高于森林覆盖区**。

- **讲师的澄清**：右图**不是**在讲荒漠化，而是在演示"改变地表类型 → 改变反照率"。

#### 图 J：土地利用变化的 ERF 时间序列与空间分布（Slide 31，Andrews et al. 2017, Fig. 1c & 3d）

- **左图（Fig. 1c）**：
  - **纵轴**：有效辐射强迫（W m⁻²）。
  - **横轴**：时间（年）。
  - **曲线形状**：**1850 年之前接近平坦**（农业尚未工业化）；**1850 年之后向负方向持续加深**，到 **2000 年代初约 −0.4 W m⁻²**。

  - **空间格局**：**美国中西部、加拿大、俄罗斯南部带、中亚**为强红区（雪暴露机制）；**南部非洲、南美中部**为红区（土壤暴露机制）；**世界其余大部分为白色（无土地利用变化）**。

### 4.4 讲义上没有的例子与比喻

### 4.6 客座讲者带来的一手研究内容：Samset et al. 2025

**这是本周讲义上几乎完全没有、只靠口头讲授的最大一块内容**（讲义 Slide 22 只有三行字：图注、doi、一句方法说明）。完整内容已整理在 2.9(3)，此处提炼考试可用的要点：

- **研究问题**：东亚气溶胶排放在过去二十年显著下降，这对全球与区域温度意味着什么？
- **方法**：气候模式的**基线 vs 扰动实验**。
- **主要结果**：**2020–2050 年约 +0.1 °C 的预估全球增暖**；东亚本地地表温度距平约 **+0.5 °C**；影响沿盛行气象向**北太平洋 → 阿拉斯加、西伯利亚**扩展。
- **不确定性证据**：多模式结果中**大多数给增暖，但有一个模式给出相反符号**。
- **为什么讲这个**：它同时演示了①"气溶胶减排 → 失去掩盖 → 增暖"这条因果链的**实证**；②"基线 vs 扰动"这个**方法**（正是 Workshop 1 要用的）；③"多模式分歧"这个**认识论要点**。
- **推广**："it's **not limited to East Asia**"——欧洲同样在减排，以及**近五年全球航运硫排放的削减**，因西欧与英吉利海峡的密集航线而对欧洲影响显著。

> "**the fact that it is a secondary aerosol** — i.e. you make it from gaseous precursors — **tells you that you actually want to run it definitely for more than three days**, because there is **a lag between when the emissions occur** (or **don't** occur, in the perturbation [scenario]) **and when the aerosol is actually formed**."

## 六、易错点·重点难点

### 6.1 概念混淆点

**（1）辐射强迫 vs 有效辐射强迫 vs 反馈**

| 概念 | 包含什么 | 关键判据 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **瞬时辐射强迫** | 驱动因子对能量收支的直接、立即改变 | 无任何调整 |
| **有效辐射强迫 ERF** | 瞬时强迫 **+ 快速调整** | 快速调整**与地表温度变化不耦合**，时间尺度天～月 |
| **反馈 feedback** | 经由**地表温度变化**才发生的后续变化 | **不算进 ERF**，算进气候敏感度 |

**判断口诀**：问自己"这个变化需不需要先让地球变热才会发生？"——**不需要 = 快速调整（算进 ERF）；需要 = 反馈（不算）。**

**（2）"气溶胶" ≠ "颗粒物"**

严格定义：气溶胶 = 悬浮颗粒 **+** 承载它们的气体。文献中的 "aerosol" 通常只指颗粒物（particulate matter）。**定义题按严格定义答；读文献时按行规理解。**

**（3）一次颗粒 vs 二次颗粒**

- 黑碳 = **一次**（排放即为颗粒）。
- 硫酸铵、硝酸铵 = **二次**（气相排放 → 反应 → 进入颗粒相）。
- **这个区分在 Workshop 1 里是得分点**：二次生成引入滞后 → 模拟必须跑得更久。

**（4）土地利用变化的"两个效应"必须分清**

| 效应 | 是不是辐射强迫？ | 机制 | 尺度 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **反照率变化** | **是**（这就是 LUC 的 ERF） | 移除植被 → 暴露更亮的下垫面 | 全球平均 −0.25 W m⁻² |
| **能量的垂直分布变化** | **不是**（讲义 Slide 33/34 加粗标题明写 "not a radiative forcing"） | 蒸散减少 → 潜热变显热 → 能量堆在近地面 | **局地/区域**，亚马逊最大可达 ~4 K |

**为什么后者不算 RF**：因为它**没有改变对流层顶的净辐照度**，只是把同样多的能量在垂直方向上重新分配。它甚至不是"快速调整"——讲师说 "it didn't have an instantaneous effect **or even a rapid adjustment**"。

**（5）土地利用变化的归属规则**

**农业造成的 CO₂ 与 CH₄ 排放，其辐射强迫记在 CO₂ 和 CH₄ 名下，不记在土地利用变化名下。** 土地利用变化这一栏只记"地表覆被/颜色改变"的账。混淆会导致**重复计算（double counting）**。

**（6）平流层臭氧 vs 对流层臭氧——方向相反！**

| | 平流层臭氧 | 对流层臭氧 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 占大气臭氧质量 | **90%** | **10%** |
| 人为扰动方向 | **减少**（ODS/CFCs 破坏，极地冬季最显著） | **增加**（前体物排放） |
| 辐射强迫符号 | **负（冷却）**，且 1970s 之前一度为正 | **正（增暖）** |
| 相对量级 | 1 | **约 20 倍** |
| **净结果** | **正（增暖），由对流层主导** | |

**最容易错的地方**：看到"臭氧空洞"就以为人类活动让臭氧减少、所以是冷却效应。**错。净效应是增暖，因为对流层的增量主导。**

**（7）trade-off（权衡）vs co-benefit（协同效益）**

| 驱动因子 | 减排对空气质量 | 减排对气候 | 性质 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **气溶胶** | 改善 ✅ | **恶化 ❌**（失去冷却掩盖） | **trade-off** |
| **臭氧（前体物）** | 改善 ✅ | 改善 ✅ | **co-benefit** |

**这一对必须能立刻说出方向和理由。**

**（8）云反照率效应 vs 云寿命效应**

两者都源于"CCN ↑ → 云滴数 ↑ 且更小"，但终点不同：

- **云反照率效应**：**同一朵云**变得更亮（散射截面增大）。
- **云寿命效应**：**降水被抑制** → 云**存在更久** → **云量/覆盖增加**。

**（9）半直接效应的方向与前两者相反**

吸收性气溶胶（黑碳）加热周围空气 → **蒸发云滴 → 云量减少 → 冷却作用减弱 → 净增暖**。写答案时不要把它和云反照率/云寿命效应混为一谈。

**（10）ERF 的正负号约定**

**正 = 增暖，负 = 冷却。** 本周三个驱动因子：气溶胶 **−**，臭氧 **+**，土地利用变化 **−**。

### 6.2 单位与量级陷阱

**（1）W m⁻² 是通量密度，不是温度**

Slide 21、Slide 31 左图的纵轴是 **W m⁻²**；Slide 22 的 (c)(d) 子图色标是 **°C**（地表温度距平）；Slide 14 的纵轴是 **°C**。**看图先看单位。**

**（2）不要把 ERF 数值和温度响应混用**

−1.3 W m⁻²（气溶胶 ERF）和 +0.1 °C（东亚减排的预估增暖）是两类完全不同的量。

**（3）符号别丢**

答题写数值时**必须带符号**：气溶胶 **−1.3**、臭氧 **+0.5**、土地利用变化 **−0.25**（单位均为 W m⁻²）。

**（4）不确定性的表达方式**

- 气溶胶–辐射：**−0.3 ± 0.3** —— 注意**误差与中心值同量级**，意味着符号在 1σ 内几乎可以翻转。
- 气溶胶–云：**−1.0 ± 0.7**。
- 总气溶胶：给的是**区间 −2.0 到 −0.6**，不是 ±。
- 全部为 **medium confidence（中等信度）**。

**（5）反照率是无量纲的 0–1 数**

不要给它加单位。新雪 0.9、地球平均 0.31、海洋 0.07。

**（6）寿命的量级差是本周的骨架**

| 驱动因子 | 寿命 |
| --- | --- |
| 气溶胶 | **~3 天**（分钟～周） |
| 臭氧 | **~3 周** |
| CH₄、CO₂ | **年～数百年** |
| 土地利用变化 | **只要农业需求存在** |

**（7）比例关系的近似性**

讲义给的"2×""4×"是**便于记忆的近似**。用具体数值验算会有出入（例如 1.3 / 0.5 = 2.6，1.3 / 0.25 = 5.2）。**答题时用"约（around/approximately）"限定，或直接给数值。** 这处近似不一致已登记在勘误表。

**（8）基准年与评估期**

气溶胶 ERF 表格的期间是 **1750–2014**；答案 deck 里臭氧与土地利用变化用的是"since 1750"。**Samset et al. 2025 的 +0.1 °C 是 2020–2050 的预估，不能与 1750 基准的数混淆。**

### 6.3 常见推理谬误

**（1）"气溶胶让地球变冷，所以气溶胶是好事"**

错在忽略两点：① 气溶胶（PM2.5）**危害健康**；② 它的冷却是**掩盖（masking）**而非解决——一旦停排，几天内就消失，被掩盖的增暖立刻显现。讲师的原话是"you start to lose that **compensation effect**"。

**（2）"最简单的机制应该是最重要的"**

讲师专门反驳过这个直觉：**气溶胶–云相互作用（−1.0，复杂、不确定）远大于气溶胶–辐射相互作用（−0.3，简单、清楚）。**

**（3）"砍树导致变暖"**

在**局地能量分配**层面（蒸散减少 → 显热增加 → 地表升温，亚马逊可达 ~4 K）确实如此；但在**全球辐射强迫**层面，砍树暴露更亮的下垫面 → 反照率上升 → **净冷却（−0.25 W m⁻²）**。**这两个结论不矛盾，因为它们描述的是不同的物理量与不同的空间尺度。** 这是本周最需要讲清楚的一对"看似矛盾"的结论。

**（4）"雪是砍树之后才有的"**

不是。讲师明说 "**the snow falls in these high latitude regions during the winter — that's always going to happen**"。变化的是**雪对入射辐射的"可见性/有效性"**，不是雪的有无。半干旱区的土壤同理："**the soil is always there**"。

**（5）"城市化是土地利用变化的主要贡献"**

错。按**面积**算，**自然土地 → 农业**才是主导；城市在反照率上"quite ineffective"。城市重要在温室气体排放，但那笔账记在气体名下。

**（6）"半干旱区变亮 = 荒漠化"**

讲师专门澄清过这不是在讲荒漠化，而是演示"地表类型改变 → 反照率改变"。

**（7）"一个模式的结果就是答案"**

Andrews et al. 2017 单模式给 −0.4 W m⁻²，IPCC 多模式最佳估计给 −0.25 W m⁻²，讲师认为前者**高估了反照率增量**。同理 Samset et al. 2025 的子图 (b) 中**有一个模式给出相反符号**。**永远要问：这是单模式还是多模式集合？**

**（8）"图上是蓝色就说明有明显的降低"**

见 4.10 的答疑：以零为中心的色标会让"落在第一档的极小变化"看起来很显眼。**读色标刻度，不要读颜色印象。**

**（9）"人类排放臭氧"**

不。人类排放的是**前体物**（NOₓ、CO、CH₄、VOCs），臭氧是**二次生成**的。Slide 25 notes 原文："humans release chemicals that are **not ozone**, but those chemicals can produce ozone once in the atmosphere."

**（10）"自然强迫可以忽略"**

讲义 Slide 14 的说法更精确：自然强迫在**量级与速率上可以非常极端**（火山），只是**在工业期整体上远小于人为强迫**。而且**物理过程是同一套**。

### 6.4 讲师明示的重点（速记清单）

按重要性排列：

1. **辐射强迫的精确定义**（对流层顶 / 入射太阳辐射 vs 出射地球辐射 / 净辐照度）——考试必须写精确版。
2. **五个驱动因子的相对量级排序**：CO₂ > CH₄ > 气溶胶（负） > 臭氧（正） > 土地利用变化（负）；以及分数链 **CO₂ ÷4 = 臭氧，臭氧 ÷2 = 土地利用变化，气溶胶 ÷4 = 土地利用变化**。
3. **气溶胶的三个机制**：直接效应、云反照率效应、云寿命效应——"and that's where it stops"。
4. **ERF = 瞬时 + 快速调整**，且快速调整与地表温度变化不耦合。
5. **对流层臭氧主导臭氧的人为强迫（约 20×），净效应为正**。
6. **气溶胶的不确定性主导整张强迫图的误差棒**，来源是组成、丰度、数–粒径分布、吸湿性。
7. **土地利用变化的快速调整 ≈ 0**（云效应正负相消）。
8. **寿命 → 混合程度 → 空间尺度** 这条推理链，对气溶胶与臭氧都要会用。
9. **两个 workshop 都是考题原型**，Workshop 2 尤其"very much like an exam"。
