## Course unit overview

This unit will cover a wide range of applications of geospatial data techniques across a range of scales in the Earth and Environmental science. Components will include:

- 1: Geodesy: Measuring the Earth
- 2: How GNSS work
- 3: Photogrammetry
- 4: Satellite remote sensing
- 5: Databases and Big Data in the Earth and Environmental sciences
- 6: Lidar Scanning
- 7: Sonar imaging
- 8: Using UAV’s for environmental monitoring and mapping.

## Aims

- 1: To give an overview of the main remote sensing techniques used in the geosciences.
- 2: To show how those techniques are applied in a variety of both academic and societal scenarios.
- 3: Introduce the concepts of how geospatial data can be collected and georeferenced, including fundamental concepts of how GNSS (GPS) and remote sensing works.
- 4: Develop skills required to deal effectively with 3D geospatial data through the introduction of GIS concepts and statistical techniques.

## Teaching and learning methods

The module will be delivered through a blend of on-line and physical lectures and practicals.  Lectures will typically last 40- 50 minutes, with practical sessions aimed to last 2 hours. The lectures will provide the background theory and context for the practical sessions.

The module will use online software, applications and web interfaces, and will increase both computer literacy and numeracy. Several of the practicals will use on-line tutorial videos to progress the student thought the steps involved.

There is no exam for this module, with assessment taking the form of on-line tests and an individual report.
