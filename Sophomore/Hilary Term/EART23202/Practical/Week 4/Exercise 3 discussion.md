# Identify these grains

Oncoids, echinoderm fragments, peloids, dasycladacean (green) algae, packstone texture

What depositional processes and environment are indicated?

Dasyclads live in very shallow (<10-15m) water (euphotic zone); peloids and oncoids are typical of lagoons; echinoderms may be washed in (they prefer normal salinity) – note these are mostly fragments (cf Exercise 3)
