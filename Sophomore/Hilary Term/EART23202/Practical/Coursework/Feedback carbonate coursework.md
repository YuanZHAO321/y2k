# **Feedback carbonate coursework**

Individual feedback is on the papers submitted to Canvas. Overall, the answers were all good to very good. The concept of A and S was largely applied correctly, although students did not systematically describe platform evolution using those terms. Question 1 did not ask to discuss each individual cycle, but the overall evolution. The discussion of controlling factors and factory types (Q3 and 4) was generally good, but the drivers for sea level variation were often missed. Most also failed to recognize the different drivers for the long-term backstep and the short-term progradational behavior. Logs were of variable quality and often did not provide enough detail through the slope and platform top facies. You also need to consider lagoonal or peritidal facies at the platform top, and the nature of the contact to the overlying deeper-water facies. Answers to questions 6 and 7 were very good across the board.
