## Slide 3

- Carbonate platform exercise:

- You are provided with paired global palaeogeographic and climate maps for the Upper Jurassic and Eocene (source: http://www.scotese.com). A legend for the paleoclimate indicators is provided.

- For each case, discuss the following:

- 1. Where would you predict carbonate platforms?

- 2. What platform types would you predict in each area?

- 3. Examining the map, what environmental and tectonic controls can you identify that would have promoted that particular platform type?
