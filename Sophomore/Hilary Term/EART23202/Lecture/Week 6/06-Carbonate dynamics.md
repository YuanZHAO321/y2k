## **Carbonate dynamics Summary**

**Carbonate facies provide an excellent stratigraphic record of past global environmental changes through their sedimentology, fossil content and geochemistry.**

**Facies prediction is fundamental to understand response of (bio)sedimentary systems to environmental change, and for applied geology (groundwater, petroleum, mining, quarrying….).**

**Careful observations on fossil content, microfacies, and geochemistry of carbonates and associated sediments are key to facies analysis and prediction.**

**Understanding dynamics of depositional systems and their response to relative sea level change is fundamental when predicting facies distribution and geometries in time and space.**

**Carbonate response to relative sea level can be understood by comparing rates of carbonate production and sea level rise/fall.**
