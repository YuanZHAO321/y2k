# **DESCRIBING  THE  GEOMETRY  OF  INTRUSIVE  IGNEOUS  BODIES**

## **What is the shape of the intrusion** ?

- Tabular: concordant ( **_sill_** ) or discordant ( **_dyke_** )

- Arcuate and parallel-sided: inwardly dipping ( **_cone-sheet_** ) or vertical / outwardly dipping ( **_ring dyke_** )

- Of more complex shape:

   - **_batholith_** : discordant, composite, outcrop area > 100 km<sup>2</sup> , no observable bottom

   - **_stock_** : discordant, outcrop area < 100 km<sup>2</sup> OR   discordant, circular outcrop < 20 km<sup>2</sup>

   - **_boss_** : discordant, circular outcrop < 25 km<sup>2</sup>

   - **_cupola_** : discordant, dome-like protuberance from a larger intrusion but isolated from it in map view

   - **_plug_** : discordant, vertical cylindrical feed channel of volcano

   - **_diatreme_** : discordant, deep, carrot-shaped volcanic vent infilled with breccia

   - **_laccolith_** : concordant, dome-like with flat floor

   - **_lopolith_** : concordant, saucer-shaped, concave-upward

   - **_phacolith_** : concordant, concavo-convex

## **Can anything be said about the mechanism of emplacement?**

• An apparent offset of layers in the country rock across the body implies that the intrusion was emplaced by dilation of the country rock; for tabular intrusions the opening direction can be established from two apparent offsets

- No apparent offset generally implies (except in special cases) intrusion by replacement or stoping
