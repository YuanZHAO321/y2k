**EART28202  Structural Geology**

# **GENERIC  ASSESSMENT  CRITERIA  FOR  FAULTS  AND  INTRUSIONS**

# **Geometry of faults  [20]**

- Fully annotated stereogram only showing relevant information

   - Well presented with appropriate title **[1; mark lost if irrelevant information is present]**

   - Planes and slickensides (if given) used in the fault plane section construction with apparent dips/pitches indicated **[2]**

   - Strike/dip of fault plane highlighted **[1]**

   - Plunge/plunge direction of slip vector highlighted **[1]**

- Fault plane section

   - Well draughted with appropriately worded title, strike/dip of section plane, correct orientation indicators, scale bar **[3, marks lost for omissions]**

   - Apparent dips and pitches going the right way and labelled, accurate offsets **[2+1]**

   - Intelligibility: FW/HW distinguished/offset surfaces labelled, slip vector labelled with arrowhead/pitch/components **[1+3]**

- Numbers for slip vector

   - Slip vector magnitude **[1]**

   - Dip-slip component magnitude and sense **[1+1]**

   - Strike-slip component magnitude and sense **[1+1]**

# **Geometry of intrusions  [5]**

- Type of intrusion/comment on shape of contacts **[1]**

- Orientation of contacts **[1]**

- True thickness with evidence of calculation **[1]**

- Comment on opening direction **[2]**
