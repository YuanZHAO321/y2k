## **Determine the orientation of the shear zone and slip vector, the sense of shear, and the magnitude of the slip vector**

1. Plot poles to dyke; the pole to the profile plane so defined lies in the shear plane

2. Plot poles to foliation; the pole to the profile plane so defined lies in the shear plane at 90° to the slip vector (it is the Y- direction)

3. Draw in the shear plane and check that its strike agrees with the map trend of the shear zone; count around 90° on the shear plane from the Y-direction and this is the slip vector

4. Determine sense of shear from orientation of slip vector and shear plane, noting that the sense of strike-slip follows from the offset of the dyke on the map
