## **Determine the orientation of the shear zone, the slip vector, and the sense of shear**

1. Plot poles to extension veins and poles to Riedel shears and plot the great circle containing these. The pole to that great circle is the Y-direction which lies in the shear plane at 90° to the slip direction

2. On the map connect the tips of the sigmoidal extension veins and measure the trend of the shear zone; the plane containing this trend and the Y-direction is the shear plane

3. Count around 90° on the shear plane from the Y-direction and this is the slip vector

4. Determine sense of shear from orientation of slip vector and shear plane, noting that the sense of strike-slip follows from the obliquity of the Riedel shears (or extension veins) on the map
