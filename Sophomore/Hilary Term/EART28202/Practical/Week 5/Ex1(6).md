## **EXERCISES  IN  2D  MOHR  DIAGRAMS**

### **Exercise 1**

For each of the following states of stress (all stresses MPa) in which the _x_ -direction points right and the _y_ -direction points up (as usual when plotting a graph), solve for the principal stresses, s 1 and s 2, by drawing a Mohr diagram. In each case use the Mohr diagram to find the value of normal stress ( s _n_ ) and shear stress ( t ) on a plane oriented at 30° anticlockwise to s 1.

- (a) Draw a square element and orient the stresses in the ( _x_ , _y_ ) coordinate frame. Note the tensor sign convention for the orientation of the stress components: look at the sides of the square that cut the negative coordinate axes; if the stress component is +ve it should point in the +ve coordinate direction on that side but if it is -ve it should point in the -ve coordinate direction.

- (b) Determine the sign of the shear stresses in the Mohr circle convention (anticlockwise shear couple is +ve, clockwise shear couple is -ve).

- (c) Plot the two pairs of points ( s _xx_ , s _xy_ ) and ( s _yy_ , s _yx_ ) and draw the Mohr circle. Evaluate s 1 and s 2. Draw arrows in the correct orientation on your diagram of a square element that indicate the direction of s 1 and s 2.

- (d) For the plane at 30° anticlockwise to s 1 note that the normal is 90° - 30° = 60° clockwise to s 1. Hence draw a line at 2 × 60° = 120° clockwise to s 1 to find the point on the Mohr circle corresponding to this plane. Read off s _n_ and t . Draw a line in the correct orientation on your diagram of a square element that indicates the orientation of the plane, and add a lazy arrow to show the sense of shear.
