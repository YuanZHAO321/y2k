## EART25102 Field Course, Scotland, Easter 2026

Dates:	Tuesday 7th April – Monday 20th April 2026

Staff:		Steve Covey-Crump, Margaret Hartley, Russell Garwood, Carson Richardson, Rufus Brunt

### Travel

Depart from Manchester: Tuesday 7th April. We will depart from the Oxford Road entrance to the School at 9 am by coach. Arrive at least 15 minutes before departure time. We will stop at a couple of motorway service stations on the journey – if you do not want to buy food at these places, bring a packed lunch. We will arrive in Fort William at about 5:30 pm

Transfer by coach from Fort William to Dundee: Tuesday 14th April

Arrival back in Manchester: The coach will leave Dundee at about 9 am on Monday 20th April; you can expect to be back in Manchester mid-afternoon depending on traffic

### Accommodation at Fort William

The accommodation is at Linnhe Lochside Holidays (http://www.linnhe-lochside-holidays.co.uk/) at Corpach, 5 miles NW of Fort William. The following contact details for the accommodation are only to be used in case of emergencies; please pass them on to relatives, etc.

Linnhe Lochside Holidays, Corpach, Fort William PH33 7NL

Tel: 01397 772 376                     E-mail: relax@linnhe-lochside-holidays.co.uk

The accommodation comprises caravans (two and three-bedroom). Each caravan has one double-bed, two or four single beds and one sofa bed. Before we arrive, you will need to organize yourselves into appropriately sized groups – two, three or four is probably optimal but you can see from the number of beds that there is some flexibility. Each caravan has a shower, a toilet, a kitchen, and a TV/DVD player. The accommodation is self-catering – we will stop at a supermarket every 2 to 3 days so that you can buy food. Towels and bed linen are not provided. There is a laundry. WiFi is available to purchase. Smoking is not permitted inside the caravans

### Catering at Fort William

You will need to provide your own breakfasts, (packed) lunches, and evening meals. There is a small shop at the accommodation site but we will stop at a large supermarket in Fort William on Tue 7th April and then once every 2 or 3 days while we are at Fort William

### Equipment

- Warm clothing: almost certainly it will be cold, wet and windy at times. A good quality waterproof jacket, stout walking boots capable of coping with boggy ground, and gloves are absolutely essential; a warm hat, waterproof trousers, and a fleece jacket are strongly recommended. Be prepared for the possibility that your clothes may not dry completely overnight. You will not be allowed in the field if you do not have adequate footwear and waterproof jacket

- Sleeping bag + pillowcase (and sheet if you want this). If you don’t bring these, hiring bedding at Fort William will cost you about £12 per person + £3 per person for towels (these were the prices in 2024). I need to know by Friday 27th March if you want to hire bedding and/or towels so that I can let the accommodation know

- Field notebook: bring your Aliaga notebook

- Pencils (HB, H or 2H), a set of coloured pencils with at least six distinctive colours, pencil sharpener, ruler, protractor. We won’t have spares of these

- Edding pens (black, green, red) for inking in: bring the ones you’ve been given; we will have some spares

- Stereogram, drawing pins and a few sheets of tracing paper: you need to bring these

- We will provide all other field equipment you need

Other Notables

- Since you will be expected to prepare and bring your lunch into the field, you may want a sandwich box. You will need a water bottle capable of holding 1½ litres (or two, each capable of carrying half that). You may also want to bring a flask so that you can have a hot drink in the field

- Expect to be away from the accommodation from 9 am to 6 pm each day

### Finance

Your accommodation and transport costs are paid by the Department but you will need money for your own food. In Week 1 it is possible that not everywhere has contactless payment machines (although supermarkets will), and so you might like to have some cash on your person in case

### Behaviour and Health

Let’s keep behaviour, both in the accommodation and in the field areas, within acceptable norms. In particular, keep noise levels down and your accommodation tidy. We get on very well with the owners of Linnhe Lochside Holidays and would like to keep it that way. You should also be aware that the locals are likely to be keeping an eye on our activities and won’t be shy about making their feelings known to the authorities should they spot anything that they deem to be dodgy.

If you have any other problems, please contact me.	Steve Covey-Crump, 14 January 2026
